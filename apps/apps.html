Put the free tools on your phone

These tools install like apps, straight from the browser: no app store, no account, nothing to pay. They open full screen, work offline once you've used them, and your numbers stay on your device.

How to install

iPhone and iPad

Open the app in Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen and Add. In Chrome on iPhone it's the Share button in the address bar.

Android

Open the app in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. Samsung Internet: menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow), or the menu, then Install. On a Mac, Safari's File menu has Add to Dock.

FAQ

Are these real apps?

They are web apps (sometimes called PWAs). They get an icon on your home screen and open full screen like any app, but they install from the browser instead of an app store, so there is no account and nothing to update by hand.

Where is my data kept?

In your browser's storage on that device. Nothing you type is sent to a server. Because of that, removing the app or clearing your browser data deletes it, so use each app's export or backup if it has one.

How do I remove one?

Like any app: press and hold the icon and choose Remove (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android). On a computer, open the app and use its menu.
