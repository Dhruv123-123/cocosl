About 1099 Money

Free tools and plain-English guides on taxes and money for US freelancers, 1099 workers and gig drivers.

How these pages are made

Pages are drafted with help from AI tools. The tax figures come from IRS, SSA and healthcare.gov publications, and each page names its sources or the form it relies on. A person decides what gets published and when, and each comparison page shows the date it went up. Figures are for the 2026 tax year and are re-checked each January.

The calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

How the site makes money

Some links on the comparison pages are affiliate links, labeled on the page: we may earn a commission at no cost to you. We also sell our own spreadsheets and templates. Neither changes which tools we pick or the order they're in, and every comparison page says how its picks were made. The full list is on How we make money.

What this isn't

This is educational content, not tax, legal or financial advice. For your own situation, check irs.gov or talk to a CPA or enrolled agent.
