First Year Freelancing

You just went freelance. Nobody withholds your tax, nobody sets up your health plan, and nobody tells you what to charge. This course covers the money side of your first year in seven short lessons, one every day or two, each with one thing to do that day.

Lesson 1 arrives right away. After the course you get a reminder a week before each quarterly tax date and one short email a week. Unsubscribe in one click.

The seven lessons

  1. Three accounts. Business checking, tax savings and personal, and the set-aside habit that makes April boring.
  2. How your first-year taxes work. Self-employment tax, the four payments, and the first-year safe-harbor rule that often keeps you penalty-free.
  3. Records from day one. What to keep, a mileage log, and the startup costs most people forget to deduct.
  4. Pricing your work. Work back from the life you want to an hourly floor, with real 2026 numbers.
  5. Getting paid. Agreements, deposits, invoices, and the W-9 and 1099 forms clients will ask about.
  6. Health insurance and retirement. Marketplace plans, the self-employed health deduction, SEP IRA and Solo 401(k).
  7. Your first-year calendar. Every 2027 date, and the routine for each one.
