Questions

What is the standard mileage rate for 2026?

72.5 cents a mile for business use of a car. Multiply your business miles by it and deduct the total on Schedule C. It replaces gas, oil, repairs, tires, insurance and depreciation, so you don't deduct those as well. Parking and tolls on jobs are still deductible on top.

Which miles count?

Miles you drive for the business: heading to a pickup, during the trip or delivery, and driving between jobs. Commuting to a regular workplace and personal errands don't count. Miles from home to your first pickup are a grey area, so a common, defensible habit is to start the log when you go online and stop when you go offline, and to keep the log that shows it.

Why isn't the app's mileage enough?

Many apps report only on-trip miles, which can leave out the drive to each pickup and the miles between jobs. The IRS expects a record you make at or near the time: the date, the miles and the business purpose. A mileage-tracking app or a notebook in the car both work. Enter the app's figure above to see what your own log is worth.

Standard rate or actual costs?

The other choice is actual expenses: the business share of gas, repairs, insurance, registration and depreciation. It needs every receipt and a mileage log anyway, to work out the business share. For most drivers with an ordinary car the standard rate is simpler and often bigger. If you want the standard rate for a car you own, use it in the first year the car is used for business.

How much should I set aside per delivery?

On $30,000 of payouts with 15,000 logged miles, a single driver with no other job owes about $2,840 in 2026 federal tax, about 9.5% of payouts, or roughly 95¢ for each of 3,000 deliveries. Without the mileage deduction it would be about $5,180. The result above works out your own rate and, if you enter your delivery count, a per-delivery amount.

Do I have to pay quarterly?

Generally yes, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more when you file. For 2026 income the dates are April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. The quarterly calculator works out each payment.

What doesn't this handle?

The new deduction for qualified tips (2025–2028, which lowers income tax but not self-employment tax, for jobs on the IRS list), actual-expense deductions, depreciation, a car used for both rideshare and another business, credits such as the earned income credit, and anything above the QBI threshold. Check irs.gov (Publication 463 and the Gig Economy Tax Center) or a tax professional for those.
