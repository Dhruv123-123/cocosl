Hi,

Every December, freelancers get told to "do something before the year ends." Some of it has a real December 31 date. Most of it doesn't. Here's the split for your **2026** taxes.

**Has to happen by December 31**

- **Equipment you want to deduct for 2026** has to be bought *and in use* by December 31. Ordered isn't enough. Items up to $2,500 each can usually be deducted right away; bigger ones usually through Section 179 or bonus depreciation. Only the business-use share counts.
- **Expenses on the cash method** count when you pay them. Business renewals you'd pay in early January anyway can be paid in December.
- **Health insurance for January 1:** HealthCare.gov's deadline is **Tuesday, December 15**. Some state marketplaces run later.
- **A Solo 401(k):** if you have one, or want one, and plan to make *employee* contributions for 2026, ask your provider about their deadline this week. Some need paperwork by December 31.

**Can wait until you file**

- **IRA and HSA contributions for 2026:** until April 15, 2027.
- **A SEP-IRA for 2026:** you can open *and* fund it until your filing deadline, including extensions. Roughly 20% of net self-employment earnings; about $11,000 at $60,000 of profit.
- **Most deductions:** mileage, home office, phone, software. They need records, not December spending.

**The one thing not to do**

Buy something you don't need for the write-off. At $60,000 of profit, a $1,000 purchase saves about $230 of federal tax. You're still out $770.
