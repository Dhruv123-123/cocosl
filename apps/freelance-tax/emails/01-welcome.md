The next date is **Friday, January 15, 2027**: the last estimated payment for your 2026 income.

**One habit before anything else.** Every time a client pays you, move a fixed percentage into a separate savings account the same day. Not at the end of the month. The same day.

How much? On $60,000 of profit, a single filer owes about $12,000 in 2026 federal tax. That's 20%, before state tax. So 25% is a sensible starting point for most people. The table on page 4 has other income levels, and the free calculator works out your own number: {{calculator_url}}
