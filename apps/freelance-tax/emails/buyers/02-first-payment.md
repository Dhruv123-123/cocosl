Hi,

Here's the 15-minute routine for your next estimated tax payment, using the tracker:

1. **Catch up the logs.** Every client payment since last time on Income, every business cost on Expenses, every drive on Mileage.
2. **Open the Quarterly tab.** Find the column for the current IRS period. The "Suggested payment" row is the lesser of the two penalty-safe methods, minus what you've already paid.
3. **Pay at irs.gov/payments.** Direct Pay or your IRS Online Account → estimated tax → 1040-ES → the right tax year. (January's payment is for the year before.)
4. **Log it.** Type the amount in the yellow "Actually paid" row and save the confirmation number.
5. **Check the set-aside block** at the bottom: is your tax savings enough for the rest of the year? If not, raise your percentage on Setup.

That's it until the next deadline.
