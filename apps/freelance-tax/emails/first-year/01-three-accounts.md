**Today's lesson: three accounts.**

The single most useful thing you can do in your first year takes about 10 minutes: give your money three homes.

- **Business checking.** Every client payment lands here, and every business cost comes out of it. Many banks offer a free second checking account. You don't legally need one as a sole proprietor, but it turns bookkeeping into "everything in this account is business".
- **Tax savings.** A separate savings account. Nickname it *Taxes, do not touch*.
- **Personal checking.** Where your rent and groceries come from. You pay yourself into it from business checking.

**The habit:** the day a client pays you, move a share into tax savings. For a single filer with no other income, 2026 federal tax is about **17% of profit at $30,000, 20% at $60,000 and 24% at $120,000**. Add your state's rate. If you also have a day job, it's often closer to 30%. If you don't know your number yet, start with 25%.

**Do this today:** open the tax savings account and move 25% of your most recent client payment into it.
