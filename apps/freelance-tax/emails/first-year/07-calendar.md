Hi,

Last lesson. Here's your first year on one page.

**Every payment:** move your set-aside percentage to tax savings the same day.

**Every Friday (5 minutes):** receipts and categories.

**Every month (30 minutes):** pull transactions, categorize them, chase unpaid invoices, and check that tax savings matches profit.

**The 2027 dates** (federal, for 2027 income unless noted):

- **Jan 15, 2027:** last estimated payment for **2026** income. It's also the end of marketplace open enrollment for 2027 coverage.
- **Feb 1, 2027:** 2026 1099-NECs due to you, and to the IRS.
- **Apr 15, 2027:** file your 2026 return or an extension. It's also the 1st 2027 estimated payment and the last day for 2026 IRA and HSA contributions.
- **Jun 15 and Sep 15, 2027:** the 2nd and 3rd payments.
- **Oct 15, 2027:** extended 2026 returns due.
- **Nov 1, 2027:** open enrollment starts.
- **Jan 18, 2028:** the 4th payment for 2027 (the 15th is a Saturday and the 17th is Martin Luther King Jr. Day).

Add your state's estimated payment dates too. Many match the federal ones.
