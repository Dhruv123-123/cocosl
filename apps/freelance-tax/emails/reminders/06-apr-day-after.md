Hi,

April's done. Save every confirmation (the 2026 balance and the 2027 payment) in your log.

One heads-up, because it catches people every year: the **2nd estimated payment for 2027 is due Tuesday, June 15**. That's two months after April, not three. The "quarters" aren't equal: the June payment covers just April and May.

The easiest way to be ready: keep moving your set-aside percentage into a separate account the day each client pays you. If your bill this April was a shock, that's the percentage to raise.

**Owe more than you can pay?** The IRS has payment plans you can set up online, and the failure-to-pay penalty drops to 0.25% a month while you're on one (if you filed on time). Interest still applies, so pay it off as fast as you can.
