Hi,

Quick reminder: the 4th estimated payment for your 2026 income is due **tomorrow, Friday, January 15**.

If you haven't worked out the amount yet, the short version: a quarter of last year's total tax is the safe harbor for most people (110% of it if last year's AGI was over $150,000). The calculator does the rest: {{calculator_url}}

Pay at **irs.gov/payments** → Direct Pay → Estimated tax → 1040-ES → **tax year 2026**. Then save the confirmation.

That's it. Five minutes.
