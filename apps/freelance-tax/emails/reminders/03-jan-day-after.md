Hi,

**If you paid yesterday:** nice work. Save the confirmation and write the amount and date in your payment log. In April you'll need the total you paid for 2026, and this is where it comes from.

**If you missed it:** pay now anyway. The underpayment penalty works like interest on the amount that was late, counted by the day. Paying a few days late costs a small fraction of what skipping the payment would. Same steps: irs.gov/payments → Direct Pay → Estimated tax → **tax year 2026**.

**What's next:** your 1099s are due to you by **Monday, February 1**. Check each one against your own income log when it arrives, because they're sometimes wrong, and some clients won't send one at all. (All of it is income either way.)
