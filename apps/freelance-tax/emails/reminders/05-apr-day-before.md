Hi,

Tomorrow, **Thursday, April 15**, is the deadline for your 2026 return and your 1st estimated payment for 2027.

**If your return isn't finished,** file an extension tonight: Form 4868, free through IRS Free File, or just make a payment at irs.gov/payments and mark it as an extension payment. That alone counts as filing the extension. Pay what you think you'll owe for 2026, because the extension doesn't move the payment date.

**Why it matters:** not filing (or extending) is the expensive mistake. The failure-to-file penalty is 5% of the unpaid tax per month. The failure-to-pay penalty is 0.5% a month. Both stop at 25%.
