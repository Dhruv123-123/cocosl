Hi,

Next **Tuesday, June 15, 2027**, the 2nd estimated payment for your 2027 income is due. It covers April and May only, so it comes around fast.

**If your income is uneven,** the safe harbor still works: the same quarter of last year's total tax each time, whatever you earned this spring. And if most of your year's income lands late, the annualized income method (Form 2210, Schedule AI) can lower your early payments. It's worth asking a tax pro about.

**The 15-minute routine:**

1. **Catch up your records.** Every client payment since last time in your income log, every business cost in your expense log.
2. **Pick your number.** The safe harbor (a quarter of last year's total tax, or 110% of it if last year's AGI was over $150,000), or 90% of this year's projected tax spread over the year. Paying the smaller one on time generally avoids the underpayment penalty. The calculator does the math: {{calculator_url}}
3. **Pay.** irs.gov/payments → Direct Pay (free) or your IRS Online Account → Estimated tax → 1040-ES. Pick the right **tax year**.
4. **Save the confirmation** and log the amount, so next time you know what you've already paid.

**Your state, too:** most states with an income tax have their own estimated payments, often on similar dates. (California, for example, wants 40% of its estimate in June and none in September.)
