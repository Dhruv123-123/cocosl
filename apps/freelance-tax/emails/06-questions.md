Hi,

A few questions we get about the Quarterly Tax Tracker, answered straight.

**I have a CPA. Do I need this?**
Most CPAs file your return once a year. The quarterly payments are on you. The tracker gives you the number in between, and gives your CPA clean income and expense totals in April.

**Why not an app?**
Apps are great if you want bank syncing and invoices, and they usually cost $10–$30 a month. The tracker is a one-time {{price}}, keeps your data in your own file, and does one job: what to pay and when.

**I just started freelancing. Is it too early?**
It's the best time. Your first year is the one where you have no idea what you'll owe. Note: if you had no tax liability last year, you may not owe a penalty this year, but you still owe the tax in April.

**Does it do state tax?**
It has a simple state rate field for your set-aside. Each state has its own estimated-payment rules, so check yours.

**What if I'm married or have a W-2 job too?**
Both are built in: filing status, W-2 wages and withholding.

**What does it not do?**
S corps, itemized deductions, credits, and incomes above the QBI threshold (about $200,000 single). For those, use a tax pro.
