Questions

Do I need invoicing software as a freelancer?

Not with two or three clients: a template and a log work fine. Software pays off once reminders, online payment and time tracking save you more than they cost.

Is FreshBooks better than QuickBooks for freelancers?

FreshBooks is better at invoicing and time tracking. QuickBooks is better at bookkeeping, mileage and tax estimates. Pick the one that matches the part you struggle with.
