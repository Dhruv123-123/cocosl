Before each payment

The 15-minute payment routine

Do this one week before each estimated-tax date.

1
Catch up your records. Every client payment since last time in your income log; every business cost in your expense log; every business drive in your mileage log.
2
Pick your number. Either the safe harbor (a quarter of last year's total tax, or 110% of it if last year's AGI was over $150,000) or the current-year amount (90% of this year's projected tax, spread out). Paying the lesser on time generally avoids the underpayment penalty.
3
Pay. irs.gov/payments → Direct Pay (free) or your IRS Online Account → Estimated tax → 1040-ES → the right tax year. January's payment is for the year before.
4
Save the confirmation. Screenshot or email it to yourself, and log the amount.
5
Pay your state if it has an income tax: separate website, often the same dates.
6
Check your tax savings. Does it cover the rest of the year plus any April balance? If not, raise your set-aside percentage.

Every month

The 10-minute monthly check

  • Label anything unclear on last month's business bank statement.
  • Add missing income, expenses and mileage to your logs.
  • Move any set-aside you forgot into the tax savings account.
  • Send or chase invoices: anything overdue gets a reminder today.
  • Pay yourself the usual amount from business to personal.
