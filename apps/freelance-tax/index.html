Questions

Who has to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

Generally, anyone who expects to owe $1,000 or more when they file, after withholding and credits. Nobody withholds tax from freelance payments, so most freelancers with steady income do.

When are they due?

For 2026 income: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. The periods are uneven on purpose (3, 2, 3 and 4 months). Pay at irs.gov/payments with IRS Direct Pay or your IRS Online Account, and choose the right tax year.

What is the safe harbor?

Pay at least 100% of last year's total tax (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000) in four on-time installments and you generally avoid the underpayment penalty, even if this year's bill is bigger. You still owe the rest when you file. Enter last year's tax above and the calculator compares both methods.

What is self-employment tax?

Social Security and Medicare, both the employee and employer halves: 15.3% on 92.35% of your net profit (the 12.4% Social Security part stops at the $184,500 wage base for 2026). Half of it is deductible when figuring income tax.

How much should I set aside?

On $60,000 of profit a single filer owes about $12,000 in 2026 federal tax, roughly 20%, before state tax. That's why 25–30% is a common set-aside. The result above gives your own number.

What doesn't this handle?

Itemized deductions, tax credits, capital gains, S corps, high incomes above the QBI threshold (around $200,000 single), and the annualized method for lumpy income. For those, use Form 1040-ES or a tax professional.
