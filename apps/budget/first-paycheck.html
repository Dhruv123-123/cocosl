Your first paycheck, set up right

Five steps for your first full-time job. It takes about 20 minutes, and you can stop and come back: your answers and progress stay in this browser.

  1. 1 Fill in your W-4

    The W-4 tells your employer how much tax to hold back. Answer four questions and we'll tell you what to fill in.

    1. 2 Get the full 401(k) match

      If your employer matches your 401(k), not taking it means turning down part of your pay. Check your benefits guide for the match.

    2. 3 Start an emergency fund

      A cushion keeps a car repair or a gap between jobs off your credit card. Keep it in a high-yield savings account, separate from checking.

    3. 4 Pick a way to budget

      Wait for your first paycheck and use the real number. Or estimate it with the paycheck calculator by state.
