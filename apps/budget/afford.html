    Questions

    How much car can I afford?

    A common guide is the 20/4/10 rule: put 20% down, borrow for 4 years or less, and keep all car costs (payment, insurance, gas and upkeep) to about 10% of gross income, or roughly 15% of take-home pay.

    Is it better to pay cash or finance a phone?

    Cash costs the least if it doesn't drain your emergency fund. A 0% carrier or maker installment plan costs the same as cash and spreads it out, but may tie you to a plan. Avoid paying interest on a phone.

    Is buy now, pay later a good way to buy something big?

    Pay-in-4 is interest-free only when every payment goes through on time, and it is often not offered for large amounts. Longer monthly plans can charge high interest. Compare the total you will pay, not just the payment.

    How does it decide?

    An option "fits" when its monthly cost is no more than half of what's left after your bills, and paying it doesn't take your savings below the amount you want to keep. "Tight" means it uses most of what's left. It's a rule of thumb, not advice.
