      Questions

      How much should I spend on holiday gifts?

      Start from what you can pay in cash by January without touching your emergency fund or bills. Set that as your cap, split it between the people on your list, and leave room for travel, food and wrapping.

      Is buy now, pay later free?

      Pay-in-4 plans usually charge no interest if every payment is on time, but late fees can apply. Longer monthly plans often charge interest. Several plans at once are easy to lose track of, and the payments land in January and February.
