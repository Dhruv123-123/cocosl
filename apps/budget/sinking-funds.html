    What is a sinking fund?

    Money you put aside a little at a time for a cost you know is coming. Instead of finding $900 for car insurance in one month, you save $150 a month for six months.

    Where should I keep sinking funds?

    In a savings account separate from your checking, ideally a high-yield one. Many online banks let you open several labeled savings "buckets" in one account.

    How is "save each month" worked out?

    What's still to save divided by the months left, counting the month it's due. If the date has already come, it shows the whole amount left. It's the same sum the Sinking Funds tab in Money Dashboard Pro uses.
