Where to dispute (free)

    Dispute with every bureau that shows the error: they don't share disputes with each other. Identity theft? Report it at IdentityTheft.gov first; the recovery plan it gives you lets the bureaus block those items.

    Questions

    How long does a credit bureau have to answer a dispute?

    Generally 30 days from when it receives your dispute. It can be 45 days if you disputed after getting your free annual credit report, and up to 15 days longer if you send more information during the investigation. The bureau then has 5 business days to send you the results.

    Do I need to pay a credit repair company to dispute?

    No. Disputing errors with Equifax, Experian and TransUnion is free, and you can do it yourself online, by mail or by phone. No company can legally remove accurate, timely information from your report.

    What if the bureau says the item is verified?

    You can ask how it was verified, dispute directly with the lender or collector, add a statement of up to 100 words to your file, or submit a complaint to the CFPB.
