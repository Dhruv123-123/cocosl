Make a money dashboard from your bank CSV

Download your transactions from your bank as a CSV and drop the file here. We sort every transaction into a category and build a spreadsheet with money in, spending and what's left each month. It opens in Google Sheets or Excel, and its totals are formulas, so it keeps working when you change things. Your file is read on this device and never uploaded.

No CSV? Paste your transactions instead

Select the transactions in your online banking, copy and paste them here. Each line needs a date, a description and an amount.

Your months at a glance

Optional: monthly budgets

Add a target for any category and the spreadsheet compares your monthly average with it. You can also fill these in later in the sheet.

Download your spreadsheet

Four tabs: Summary, Transactions (change any category from a dropdown), Categories (add your own and set budgets) and How to use.

Google Sheets: go to drive.google.com, choose New > File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets. Excel or Numbers: just open it.

Rather do it in an app?

The free 20-Minute Budget app plans each month ahead. The subscription finder reads the same CSV and lists every recurring charge.

What it does with your file

  • Reads the date, description and amount columns, from one file or several (checking plus cards). Duplicate rows are removed.
  • Sorts each transaction by keywords: rent, groceries, gas, eating out, subscriptions and 14 more. Anything it can't place goes in Other for you to sort.
  • Counts transfers between your own accounts and credit card payments separately, so card spending isn't counted twice.
  • Keeps the latest 12 months. Everything happens in this browser tab: no upload, no account, no bank login.
