Should I budget on take-home pay or salary?

Take-home: the amount that actually lands in your account after taxes, insurance and retirement contributions. That is the money you can plan.

What if I am paid every two weeks?

Plan on two paychecks a month. Twice a year a month has three, and that extra paycheck can go straight to savings or debt without breaking the plan.

What if my needs are more than 50%?

Common where rent is high. Use the zero-based plan with your real numbers, and close the gap from wants first. The split is a guide, not a rule.
