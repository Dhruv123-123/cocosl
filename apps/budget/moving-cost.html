    How much does it cost to move?

    It depends most on distance, how much stuff you have and whether you hire movers. A local move with a rented truck can stay in the hundreds; a long move with full-service movers often runs into the thousands. The costs people forget are the deposit, a few days of paying two rents and time off work.

    Are these estimates accurate?

    They're rules of thumb so you have somewhere to start, not quotes. Get at least three written quotes from movers or truck companies and type them in. The assumptions are listed on the page.

    How do I avoid moving scams?

    Use movers with a USDOT number for interstate moves and check it on the FMCSA website. Be wary of a quote far below the others, a large cash deposit, or a quote given without seeing your things.
