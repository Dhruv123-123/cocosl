      Questions

      Should couples split bills 50/50 or by income?

      50/50 is simple and works when you earn about the same. If one of you earns much more, splitting by income (each pays the same percentage of their pay) keeps things fair and leaves both of you similar room for your own spending.

      What is a money date?

      A short, regular check-in, about 30 minutes once a month, where you look at shared spending, settle up, plan big expenses and goals, and agree on each person's no-questions fun money. Starting with a win keeps it friendly.

      Joint account or settle up?

      With a joint account, each of you moves your share in every month and the shared bills come out of it. Without one, whoever pays a bill keeps track, and once a month the person who paid less sends the difference. This tool does both.
