    Questions

    How do I calculate my net worth?

    Add up the value of everything you own (cash, investments, retirement accounts, your home and car at what they would sell for), then subtract everything you owe (credit cards, loans, mortgage). The difference is your net worth.

    Is a negative net worth bad?

    It's common, especially early on with student loans or a new car loan. What matters most is the direction: if it rises from one snapshot to the next, you're making progress.

    How often should I check my net worth?

    Once a month or once a quarter is plenty. Investments move day to day, so a regular snapshot shows the trend without the noise.
