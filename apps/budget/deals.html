Questions

What is deferred interest?

Many store cards and some medical and furniture financing say "no interest if paid in full" by a date. Interest is still being worked out in the background. If any balance is left when the promo ends, all of that interest, back to the purchase date, is added at once.

How is that different from 0% APR?

With a true 0% APR offer, such as most balance transfer cards, interest only starts on what's left after the promo ends. Deferred interest is the expensive one: missing by a dollar can cost the whole period's interest.

Is the minimum payment enough?

Often not. Minimums on deferred-interest plans are frequently too small to clear the balance in time. Divide the balance by the months left and aim to finish a month early, because payments take a few days to post.

Where do I find the dates and APR?

On your statement or in the card's app: look for "promotional balance", "promo expiration" or "deferred interest". The APR shown for purchases is usually what applies after.
