        Is it fairer to split rent by income?

        When incomes are close, splitting equally is simple and fair. When one person earns a lot more, splitting by income means everyone pays the same share of what they take home, so nobody is stretched. Many households split rent by income and small shared bills like internet equally: pick the split for each bill.

        How does "by income" work?

        Each person pays their share of the group's total take-home pay. If Ana takes home $6,000 and Ben and Cy $3,000 each, Ana pays half of each bill and Ben and Cy a quarter each.

        How is settling up worked out?

        We add up what each person's share is and what they actually paid, then find the fewest payments that even it out. Amounts are split to the cent and always add up to the bill.
