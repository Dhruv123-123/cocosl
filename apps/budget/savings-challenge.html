52-week savings challenge

Save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, and so on up to $52. After a year you have $1,378. Or pick your own goal and style. Tick each week off as you move the money to savings. Your progress stays in this browser.

    Questions

    How much is the 52-week challenge?

    The classic version adds up to $1,378: $1 + $2 + … + $52. Set any goal and the weeks scale to it, so a $5,000 goal starts at about $3.63 and ends at about $188.

    Which style should I pick?

    Classic starts easy. Reverse front-loads the big weeks while you're keen, so the end of the year (and the holidays) is light. Even keeps every week the same, which is easiest to automate.

    What if I miss a week?

    Just catch up. The tracker shows how far behind you are and a small extra amount a week that clears it by the end. You can also tick off any week you like, in any order.
