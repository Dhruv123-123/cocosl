    How should I split a wedding budget?

    A common starting point is about 40% on the venue, catering and bar, around 12% on photos and video, and smaller shares for attire, flowers, music and the rest, plus a buffer of about 5% for the extras every wedding has. The honeymoon starts at zero because many couples budget it separately. It's a rule of thumb, not a rule: move money to what matters most to you.

    How do I work out the cost per guest?

    Divide the expected total by the number of guests. The page also shows the venue, catering and bar cost per guest, because that's the part that grows with every name you add.

    How is "save each month" worked out?

    What's still to pay, minus what you've saved, divided by the months until the wedding month. Vendors often want deposits earlier, so check each contract's due dates.
