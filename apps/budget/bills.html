Before you call

  • Have your latest bill in front of you: the plan name, what you pay, and the account number.
  • Look up what the company charges new customers, and one competitor's price. A real number is your best tool.
  • Ask for the retention or loyalty team. The first person you reach often can't change prices.
  • Be polite and specific: "What can you do to bring my bill to $50?"
  • No deal? Thank them and call back another day. You'll often get a different person with different offers.
  • Write down the new price, how long it lasts and a confirmation number, then log the call here.
