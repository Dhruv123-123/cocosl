How to cancel

  • App store subscriptions: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions.
  • Everything else: sign in to the service and look under Account, Billing or Membership. Several states require services to let you cancel online if you signed up online, so look for a cancel button before you call.
  • Can't find it or they won't stop charging? Ask your card issuer about blocking the merchant or disputing the charge.
  • Not ready to cancel? Ask for a cheaper plan. Many services offer one when you start to cancel.
