Questions

Can I charge a late fee on an invoice?

Generally only if the client agreed to it before the work, in your contract or in the payment terms on the invoice. Many freelancers use 1% to 1.5% a month on the unpaid amount. Some states limit late fees and interest, so check your state's rules.

How do I ask a client to pay a late invoice politely?

Start friendly: assume it slipped through the cracks, restate the invoice number, amount and due date, and include a way to pay. Get firmer at two weeks late, ask for a date, and offer a payment plan. At 30 days, send a final notice that says what happens next.

What does Net 30 mean?

The invoice is due 30 days after the invoice date. Net 15 is 15 days, and due on receipt means it's due right away.
