    Questions

    How much should I set aside for taxes from a side hustle?

    Self-employment tax alone is about 14% of profit (15.3% on 92.35% of it), plus federal and state income tax. Many people set aside 20% to 30% of profit and pay quarterly estimated taxes if they will owe $1,000 or more.

    Should I deduct mileage or actual car costs?

    Most gig drivers use the IRS standard mileage rate: 76 cents a mile for business driving from July 1, 2026 (72.5 cents before that). Keep a mileage log with the date, miles and purpose of each trip.

    Do I pay self-employment tax on a small side hustle?

    Yes, once your net earnings from self-employment for the year reach $400. Platforms send a 1099-K or 1099-NEC, but you owe tax on your profit whether or not you get a form.

    How is this worked out?

    Profit is your sales minus platform fees, costs and what driving really costs you. Taxes use the IRS mileage rate as your car deduction, self-employment tax at 15.3% on 92.35% of profit, and income tax at your bracket after half of self-employment tax and the 20% qualified business income deduction. It's an estimate: credits, other income and your exact return will change it. Platform fees are typical rates; check yours.
