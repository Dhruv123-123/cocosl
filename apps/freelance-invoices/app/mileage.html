Mileage log

Log each business trip with the date, miles and why you drove. The IRS wants a log kept as you go, and at 76 cents a mile it adds up fast.

Not tax advice. These are planning estimates from IRS rules for 2026 and 2027. Your real tax depends on your whole return. Check with a tax professional or the IRS before you file or pay.
