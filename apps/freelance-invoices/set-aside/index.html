    How is the set-aside worked out?

    Self-employment tax is 15.3% on 92.35% of your profit. Income tax uses your bracket after half of self-employment tax and the 20% qualified business income deduction. We add your state rate and take off your business costs. It's a rule of thumb that runs a little high for most people, which is the safe side.

    Do I have to pay quarterly?

    Usually, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year after withholding. Paying at least 100% of last year's tax across the four dates (110% if your income was over $150,000) protects you from the underpayment penalty, even if you owe more in April. See IRS Form 1040-ES.
