Privacy

Short version: what you check stays on your device. We don't collect it, store it or sell it.

The website checker

Messages, senders and links you paste are checked by code running in your browser. They are not sent to us or anyone else. If a guide page opens the checker with a message filled in, the message travels in the part of the address after #, which browsers never send to a server.

If you sign up for scam alerts, we keep your email address to send them, and you can unsubscribe from any email. Affiliate links may tell the partner that you came from us.

The browser extension

  • Text and links you check from the toolbar or the right-click menu are checked on your device and not sent anywhere.
  • When you open the extension, it reads the address of the tab you're on to tell you if it looks like a fake site. That check also happens on your device.
  • If you turn on look-alike warnings, the extension checks the address of each page you visit, on your device, to decide whether to show a red badge. It doesn't record, store or send your browsing history. Turning the setting off stops this.
  • The only thing it saves is that on/off setting, in your browser's own extension storage.
  • It has no analytics, no ads and no accounts.
