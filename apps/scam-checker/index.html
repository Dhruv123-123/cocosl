How it works

The checker looks for patterns that show up again and again in scams reported to the FTC and FBI: pressure to act fast, unusual ways to pay (gift cards, crypto, Zelle, wires), requests for codes or passwords, threats, prizes you never entered, and links that pretend to be a company they aren't. Each red flag adds to the risk score.

If it says low risk, is it safe?

Not necessarily. New scams can slip past any checker. If a message asks for money or personal details, contact the company directly using its official app or a number you look up yourself.

Where do I report a scam?

In the US, forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM) and report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Internet crimes go to IC3.gov. If you lost money, call your bank first.

Is my message stored?

No. Everything runs on your device. There is no server receiving what you paste.
