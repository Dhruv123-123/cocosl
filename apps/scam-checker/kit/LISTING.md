## Description
Scam texts and calls got smarter: fake toll and delivery texts, "bank fraud department" calls, and
"grandchild in trouble" calls that use AI-cloned voices. This kit gives your family one simple routine
and the exact words to use.

What's inside:
- **Stop. Check. Call.** A one-page fridge sheet with the 12 red flags behind almost every scam
- **Wallet cards** to cut out: "Before I pay anyone", "Never share", "People I call first", real phone numbers
- **What to say** when someone claims to be your bank, the IRS, tech support, a grandchild or a Marketplace buyer
- **Family safe word plan**, the FBI-recommended defence against voice-clone calls
- **30-minute account lockdown**: credit freezes, phone carrier PIN, two-step login, alerts
- **"I think I've been scammed"**: what to do in the first hour for each way you might have paid, plus a fill-in record page

Instant download. Print at home on US Letter paper. Based on public guidance from the FTC, FBI, IRS and
Social Security Administration. Educational guide, not legal or financial advice.
