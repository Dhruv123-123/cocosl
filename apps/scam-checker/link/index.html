How to spot a fake website

Read the address from right to left

The part that matters is just before the ending (.com, .org, .gov). In paypal.account-verify.com the real site is account-verify.com, not PayPal. Everything to the left is decoration a scammer can type freely.

Watch for swapped letters

A zero for an "o", a one for an "l", "rn" for "m": amaz0n, paypa1, rnicrosoft. On a phone they're almost invisible.

Is a padlock (https) a sign it's safe?

No. The padlock means the connection is encrypted, nothing more. Most scam sites have one.

What's the safest habit?

Don't log in or pay through a link in a text or email. Open the company's app, or type its address yourself.
