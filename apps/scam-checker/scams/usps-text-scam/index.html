Is this USPS text a scam?

Got a text saying your USPS package is on hold or needs a redelivery fee? Here is how to tell if it is a scam, what USPS actually sends, and what to do if you clicked.

What it looks like

USPS: Your package has arrived at the warehouse but cannot be delivered due to incomplete address information. Please confirm your address within 12 hours: https://usps.com-trackrd.top/us

An illustration based on reported messages. Names, amounts and links are made up.

Signs it's a scam

  • A link to a site that is not usps.com (look closely: "usps.com-trackrd.top" is a .top site)
  • A deadline of hours to "confirm" your address
  • A small fee (often $0.30 to $3) to "redeliver"
  • You never signed up for text tracking for that package

What the real one does

USPS says it will not send texts or emails without you first requesting them with a tracking number, and those messages will not contain a link asking for payment. If USPS really can't deliver, it leaves a physical notice.

How to check safely

Type usps.com yourself (or use the USPS Mobile app) and enter the tracking number from the seller's confirmation email.

If you already clicked or replied

You only opened the messageYou're fine. Delete it and block the sender.
You clicked the link but typed nothingClose the page. Don't download anything it offers. Keep your phone updated.
You typed a passwordChange that password now, plus anywhere you reused it, and turn on two-factor login.
You entered card or bank detailsCall your bank or card issuer using the number on your card and ask them to block it.
You paid by gift card, wire, Zelle or cryptoContact the company you paid through right away (gift card company, bank, or exchange) and report it. Speed matters.
You gave your Social Security numberFreeze your credit at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion (free) and see IdentityTheft.gov for a recovery plan.

Report it

Forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM). Report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, and internet crime or money lost at IC3.gov.
