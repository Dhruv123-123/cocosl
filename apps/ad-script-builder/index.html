Questions

Why does it ask for facts?

Ads that state specific, true details (what it's made of, the size, what's on the label) beat vague praise, and they don't get rejected for unprovable claims. The builder never adds claims of its own.

What's a hook, and why three?

The hook is the first three seconds, where most viewers decide to keep watching. Film the ad once, then swap only the opening to test which hook holds attention best.

Can an AI presenter say they love the product?

No. An AI presenter can present and demonstrate, but a first-person "I use it, I love it" line would be a fake testimonial. The builder flags that wording. If you want a personal story, pick "Me (the founder)" and film yourself.

Does it work for services?

It's built for physical products you can show on camera. Local services work with the "problem → solution" style if you have real photos of the place.
