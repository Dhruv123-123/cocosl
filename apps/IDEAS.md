## Why this order

- **1–3 are the cheapest wins**: they reuse code that already exists, rank in search on their own, and push
  people into the email list and affiliate offers we've already set up.
- **4 is the fastest "real sales" bet**: Etsy and Gumroad Discover have buyers browsing already, so it doesn't
  wait on our social accounts growing.
- **5 opens the best-paying affiliate niche** (business software pays $50–200+ per signup) and seeds the
  third brand.
- **6 and 8 are services dressed as products**: few sales needed to matter, but each needs a human step.
- **9 is the biggest upside but the slowest**: store review, ongoing maintenance, and a real support load.

## Ideas we passed on

- **Native iOS/Android apps**: $99/year Apple fee, store review, and little extra reach over a web app that installs to the home screen (the budget app already does).
- **Stock/crypto signal tools**: regulatory risk and they'd undercut the brand's "no hype" promise.
- **Bank-connected budgeting (Plaid)**: per-user fees and security obligations before there's revenue to cover them. Revisit if Budget Pro passes ~200 subscribers.
- **Credit card rewards optimizer**: needs constant offer data upkeep and issuer affiliate approval, which usually requires existing traffic.
