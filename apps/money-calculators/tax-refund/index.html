Questions

When will I get my refund?

The IRS usually starts accepting 2026 returns in late January 2027. Most e-filed returns with direct deposit are paid within 21 days. Returns claiming the EITC or the additional child tax credit can't be paid before mid-February by law.

What changed for 2026?

The standard deduction is $16,100 single, $32,200 married filing jointly and $24,150 head of household. The child tax credit is $2,200 per child, with up to $1,700 refundable. New deductions let you subtract up to $25,000 of tips and up to $12,500 of overtime premium ($25,000 joint), and people 65 and older get an extra $6,000 deduction.

Is a big refund good?

It feels good, but it means too much tax came out of your paychecks during the year. If you would rather have the money monthly, update your W-4 with the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator.

What does this leave out?

Itemized deductions, the Earned Income Tax Credit, education credits, self-employment tax, capital gains rates and state tax. It is an estimate. Your tax software or a preparer will give the final number.
