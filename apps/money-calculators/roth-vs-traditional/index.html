Questions

When does Roth win?

When your tax rate in retirement is higher than it is today, which is common for students and early-career workers in low brackets.

When does Traditional win?

When you are in a high bracket now and expect a lower rate in retirement. You get the tax break up front.

Can I do both?

Yes. Splitting contributions is a reasonable hedge when you are unsure about future tax rates. Check the IRS income and contribution limits for the year.
