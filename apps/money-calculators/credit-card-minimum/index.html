Questions

How is the minimum payment calculated?

Commonly the interest for the month plus 1% of the balance, with a floor such as $25 or $35. Your card agreement has the exact formula.

Why does a fixed payment help so much?

A fixed amount does not shrink as the balance falls, so more of each payment goes to principal every month.

What about a balance transfer?

A 0% transfer can help if the fee is small and you clear the balance before the promo ends. Do not add new spending to the old card.
