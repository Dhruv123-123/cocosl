Questions

What return should I use?

Broad US stock index funds have averaged roughly 7% a year after inflation over long periods, with big swings in between. Use a lower number to be cautious. Past returns do not guarantee future ones.

Why does starting early matter so much?

Growth earns growth. Money invested in your twenties has decades longer to compound than money added later, so small early amounts often end up larger.

Does this include fees or taxes?

No. Subtract your fund expense ratio from the return to include fees, and use a tax-advantaged account like a 401(k) or IRA to reduce tax drag.
