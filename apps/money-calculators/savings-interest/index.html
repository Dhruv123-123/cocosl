Questions

What is APY?

Annual percentage yield: the yearly return including compounding. A 4% APY on $10,000 earns about $400 in a year if the rate stays the same.

Are high-yield savings accounts safe?

Ones at FDIC insured banks or NCUA insured credit unions are protected up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. Check the insurance before you open one.

Do rates change?

Yes. Savings rates are variable and usually move with the Federal Reserve's rate. Re-check every few months.
