Questions

Where do I find my expense ratios?

In your plan's yearly fee disclosure (often called the 404a-5 notice), or on your 401(k) website next to each fund. It is shown as a percent, like 0.04% or 0.85%.

What is a good expense ratio?

Broad index funds often cost under 0.10% a year, while many actively managed funds cost 0.50% to 1% or more. A higher fee has to be earned back by better returns, and most funds don't manage that over long periods.

Should I switch to the cheapest fund?

Not automatically. Compare funds in the same category, and look at what each one holds and how it fits your plan. This tool shows costs; it doesn't tell you what to buy. If you have questions, your plan provider or a fee-only fiduciary adviser can help.

Are there other 401(k) fees?

Often yes: plan administration and recordkeeping fees, sometimes charged as a flat dollar amount. Your fee disclosure lists them. This checker covers fund expense ratios only.
