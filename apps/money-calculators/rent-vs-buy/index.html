Questions

How long do I need to stay for buying to make sense?

Buying and selling costs often 8% to 10% of the price, so buying usually needs several years to beat renting. The calculator shows your break-even year.

What costs does owning add beyond the mortgage?

Property tax, home insurance, HOA dues, upkeep (often 1% to 2% of the value a year), closing costs to buy and agent and closing costs to sell.

Is rent just throwing money away?

No. Mortgage interest, property tax and upkeep are also money you do not get back. The fair comparison is what each path leaves you with.
