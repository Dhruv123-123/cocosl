Questions

What is the avalanche method?

Pay the minimum on everything, then put every extra dollar on the debt with the highest APR. When it is gone, roll that payment to the next-highest. It costs the least interest.

What is the snowball method?

Same idea, but you target the smallest balance first. You pay a little more interest, but you clear whole debts sooner, which keeps many people going.

Which one should I use?

The one you will stick with. If avalanche saves only a few dollars, snowball is fine. If it saves hundreds, it is usually worth the patience.
