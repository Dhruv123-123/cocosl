Questions

What is the Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP)?

RAP opened July 1, 2026. You pay 1% to 10% of your AGI a year depending on income, minus $50 a month for each dependent, with a $10 minimum. Unpaid interest is waived, the government adds up to $50 a month to principal on on-time payments, and any balance left after 30 years is forgiven.

Which plans can I use if I borrowed after July 1, 2026?

If any of your federal loans was first paid out on or after July 1, 2026, your choices are the Tiered Standard plan (10 to 25 years depending on balance) and RAP.

What happened to SAVE?

A court order ended SAVE in March 2026. Servicers are notifying SAVE borrowers, who have 90 days from their notice to pick another plan. PAYE and ICR close no later than July 1, 2028.

Is forgiveness taxed?

Forgiveness at the end of an income-driven plan may count as taxable income. Public Service Loan Forgiveness is not taxed.
