Questions

How many months should I save?

Three months of essential costs is a common target, six if your income is irregular or you support others. Start with a $1,000 starter fund.

Where should I keep it?

In a separate, FDIC or NCUA insured savings account you can reach in a day or two, but not with your spending card.

Should I pay off debt first?

Many people build a small starter fund first so a surprise bill does not go on a credit card, then attack high-interest debt, then finish the full fund.
