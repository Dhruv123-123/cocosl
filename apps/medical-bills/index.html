How to send it

  • Mail it to the billing address on the statement. Certified mail with a return receipt gives you proof it arrived.
  • Keep a copy of every letter, and note the date you sent it.
  • Call the billing office a week later, confirm they received it and ask them to note your account. Write down who you spoke to.
  • Compare the itemized bill with your insurer's explanation of benefits (EOB). Charges that don't match are worth asking about.

Questions

Should I pay a medical bill right away?

For a large bill, first ask for an itemized bill and compare it with your insurer's explanation of benefits. Ask the billing office to hold the account while you review it, and keep copies of everything you send.

What is hospital financial assistance?

Nonprofit hospitals must have a written financial assistance policy, often called charity care, that can reduce or cancel bills for people under certain incomes. Many for-profit hospitals have programs too. Ask for the policy and an application, even if the bill is already old.

What does the No Surprises Act cover?

It generally protects insured people from out-of-network bills for emergency care and for certain out-of-network doctors at in-network hospitals and surgery centers. Uninsured or self-pay patients can dispute a bill that is at least $400 more than their Good Faith Estimate within 120 days of the bill. The federal No Surprises Help Desk is 1-800-985-3059.
