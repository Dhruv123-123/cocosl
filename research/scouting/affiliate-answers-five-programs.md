## Answers by program

All brand descriptions, promotion methods, "methods we don't use" and the compliance statement come from
PR #5's kit. The answers below are the program-specific parts.

### Aura (Impact)
- **Why Aura / how we'll promote it:** already drafted in PR #5's kit. Keep "free steps first (credit
  freezes, two-step login), then Aura for families who want monitoring": that's also our editorial rule.
- **Ask before promoting:** what counts as a "qualified enrollment", and the rules on brand bidding and
  claims. The page doesn't say; email affiliates@aura.com. The page shows $65 in the body but "up to
  $125" in its metadata, so confirm the rate in Impact before quoting any figure.

### NordVPN (direct)
- **Traffic source:** "YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram explainers on scams and account security, with
  a guide on our site about when a VPN helps (public Wi-Fi) and when it doesn't (it won't stop phishing or
  scam calls)."
- **Audience:** Scam Decoded's block from PR #5. US adults 25-65 and adult children helping parents.
- **Why us:** "We recommend a VPN for the situations where it actually helps, so the people who click are
  the ones who need one. We don't use fear-based hooks or 'you're being hacked' claims."
- **Ask them:** rules on coupon sites and brand bidding (not on the page). Write to affiliate@nordvpnmedia.com.
- **Editorial caution:** a VPN doesn't protect against most scams we cover. Every mention keeps that line,
  or the post overclaims (`risk-memo.md`, misleading claims).

### Keeper (Tune)
- **Promotion:** "1099 Money's free quarterly tax calculator and tax-deadline emails, plus short videos on
  deductions freelancers miss. Keeper fits as the 'find write-offs in your bank transactions' option next
  to our free mileage log."
- **Audience:** 1099 Money's block from PR #5.
- **Ask them:** whether the payout is per signup or per scan, and the cookie window. The page says "up to
  $50 per referral", paid when someone scans their purchases.
- **Editorial caution:** no "write off everything" framing (PR #5's 1099 Money description already
  promises that).

### Wise (Wise app for influencers, Partnerize for websites)
- **Which route:** the **influencer** route for social, and **Partnerize** only if the site gets a Wise
  page. Either way, Wise's guidelines say traffic should "direct readers to your own website first".
- **Promotion:** "1099 Money explains how freelancers with international clients get paid: comparing the
  fee plus exchange-rate margin on the same day and amount, using Wise's own published fees."
- **Rules from Wise's guidelines (read today):**
  - No paid ads of any kind (search, native, Google Ads) without written consent.
  - No domains or social handles containing "Wise".
  - No "free transfer" coupon promotion on websites.
  - Don't advertise services Wise doesn't offer.
  - Comparisons against named competitors need approval from partnerwise@wise.com.
  - Use Wise's approved claim language.
- **Compliance:** don't call Wise a bank or "FDIC-insured" loosely (`affiliates-for-new-creators.md`, FDIC
  rule). Copy Wise's own wording.

### Remitly (Impact): hold
- What they ask, per their page: an Impact onboarding form, and an audience in a listed country. They
  select partners with "high quality" content and a "relevant and engaged" audience.
- Draft line for when Dinero resumes: "Dinero Simple explica en español cuánto llega realmente a casa: la
  tarifa más el tipo de cambio, el mismo día y por la misma cantidad". It needs the reviewer's sign-off
  and Spanish disclosures (#publicidad).
- **Conflict to settle first:** the Dinero thread rules out ranked remittance-provider comparisons. An
  affiliate link to one provider in a "compare the total cost" post needs Dhruv's call on how it's framed.
