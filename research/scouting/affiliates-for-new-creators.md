# Affiliate programs that take brand-new creators, by brand, with the finance rules that apply

Written 2026-11-07 from programs' own pages where they load, and directories where they don't (marked).
**Docs only.** Nothing was applied for. This adds to PR #5's application kit (`launch/affiliates/programs.json`,
31 programs), which has the "what to say" side. This memo answers **who will accept us now**, cookie lengths,
and the rules that limit how we promote finance products.

## TL;DR

1. **The website is the key that opens most programs, not follower counts.** FreshBooks says it outright:
   "Ad-only, social-only, and email-only publishers aren't accepted"; it wants a "fully launched" site with
   original content and an active blog. Apply **after** each brand's site is live with its first 10-20
   articles, not on day 1 of the social accounts.
2. **Easiest to get into now (no stated audience minimum, open sign-up):** Remitly (Impact; "a website isn't
   required", creators on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram named), Wise (Partnerize, 1-year cookie), NordVPN
   (direct sign-up), Aura (Impact, $65 per enrolment, 60-day cookie), Keeper Tax (Tune, up to $50),
   DeleteMe (30-40% of order value), beehiiv (50% for 12 months).
3. **Strict:** SoFi ("strict, requiring a detailed review of your content and audience", per Creator Hero,
   May 2026), FreshBooks (site rules above), the robo-advisors (SEC rules below). Expect a "no" or
   "reapply later" while we have no traffic, and don't take it personally.
4. **Longest cookies matter more than the headline rate for slow buyers:** Wise 1 year, FreshBooks
   (PartnerStack; PR #5 has 120 days), Aura 60 days, Remitly and NordVPN 30 days, Chime **1 day** (Creator
   Hero, via CJ).
5. **Three finance rules shape every post:** the FTC's disclosure rules (in the video and in the same
   language, so **Spanish disclosures for Dinero Simple**), the SEC Marketing Rule for investment advisers
   (they'll hand us required wording), and the FDIC's rule on misrepresenting deposit insurance (never call
   a fintech app a "bank" or say "FDIC-insured" unless the provider's own copy says so).

## 2. The rules that limit finance promotion

| Rule | What it means for us | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **FTC Endorsement Guides** | Disclose any paid or affiliate relationship **with the endorsement itself**, not only in the bio or description. In video, say it and show it on screen. Use plain words ("I earn a commission if you sign up"), not "sp", "collab" or "ambassador". **"The disclosure should be in the same language as the endorsement"**, so Dinero Simple posts disclose in Spanish. Platform tools are in addition, not instead | [FTC, Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) |
| **SEC Marketing Rule (206(4)-1)** | Investment advisers (robo-advisors and Empower's advisory side) must disclose that promoters are paid, and need a written agreement with any promoter paid over $1,000 in 12 months. Expect required disclosure wording and content review. Use their copy word for word | [Smarsh summary](https://www.smarsh.com/regulations/sec-marketing-rule-206-4-1) (not legal advice) |
| **FDIC false advertising rule (12 CFR 328, subpart B)** | Non-banks (Chime, Acorns, SoFi Invest, Wise) can't be described as banks or as FDIC-insured unless that's accurate and says through which bank. The FDIC has sent cease-and-desist letters over exactly this. Copy their own wording ("banking services provided by …, Member FDIC") | [Federal Register, Jan 2024 final rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2023-28629.pdf) |
| **Rates change** | APYs, sign-up bonuses and fees change monthly. Date every number ("as of 1 Jan 2027") and link the provider's page | Our guardrails |
| **Remittance comparisons** | Compare total cost (fee plus exchange-rate margin) on the same day and amount, and say when we were paid by one of the providers | Our judgment; providers must disclose both under the CFPB remittance rule |
| **Program rules** | Wise: no bidding on brand terms, no coupon sites, paid search must land on our page first. FreshBooks: no self-referrals; spam means immediate termination | Program pages |

PR #5's guardrails already cover "I've used", "guarantee" and "risk-free". Add **"FDIC-insured" and "bank"
next to fintech names** to the flagged words.

## 3. Order to apply (when each site is live)

1. **Week the site goes live:** Remitly and Wise (Dinero Simple), Aura and NordVPN (Scam Decoded), Keeper
   (1099 Money), beehiiv (all). They're open and have the clearest terms.
2. **After 10-20 articles and some traffic:** FreshBooks, Gusto, QuickBooks (site rules), Credit Karma,
   Acorns, Quicken.
3. **After about 3 months of traffic data:** SoFi and the investment programs (strict review, SEC wording).
4. **Before 5 January:** the tax software (FreeTaxUSA, TaxAct), so links are live for the January peak.
