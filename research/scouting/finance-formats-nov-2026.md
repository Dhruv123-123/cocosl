# Personal finance formats working on Instagram and TikTok, November 2026

Researched 2026-11-07 from public sources only: Instagram's and TikTok's own posts, dated trade analyses,
and one consumer survey. **No scraping, and no accounts or APIs were used.** The factory's `watchlist.py`
can track our own competitors through official APIs once Dhruv's tokens exist.

**A warning on evidence quality.** There's no official "what's working in finance this month" data from
either platform. TikTok's Creative Center trend pages need a login-free browser session that our fetch
tool can't render. Most "November 2026 trends" posts are SEO pages with unsourced numbers. So this file:
- Weights the few **dated, sourced** analyses.
- Labels everything else as a signal.
- Ends with a cheap way to measure it ourselves.

**Formats to avoid** (seen in the same roundups):
- "DM us for investing help" calls to action.
- Faith-based prosperity bait.
- Finance hashtags on unrelated viral content.
- AI-avatar "financial advisor" explainers.

Each one conflicts with our rules: no personal investment advice, no engagement bait, and YouTube's and
TikTok's inauthentic and AI-avatar risks.

## 2. Add: a savings-challenge series per brand

Inviting the viewer to do something with you "beats telling them what to do" (Draper). It's also a natural
serial: people come back for day 2, which builds follows. Faceless-safe versions:

| Brand | Challenge | Shape | Ties to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MMS** | **"$300 by New Year's Eve"** (8 weeks from 7 Nov, or a 4-week "$150 by Christmas" if it starts later) | Day-1 Reel with the plan, then **one short check-in a week**: "Week 3: cancel one subscription, +$15". A printable tracker as the lead magnet | Bill Cut Kit ($5), 20-Minute Budget ($12) |
| **MMS (January)** | **"No-spend January, the realistic version"**: 4 weeks, one rule a week | The existing "4 rules for a no-spend January" post becomes the day-1 post of a series | Chime's "No Spend" and "Debt Pay-Off Winter" themes |
| **1099 Money** | **"Set aside 25% from every invoice until 15 Jan"** | Weekly: "Week 2: open a separate tax savings account". Ends on payment day with the IRS payment walkthrough | Quarterly calculator, Quarterly Tax Club waitlist |
| **Scam Decoded** | **"7-day family scam-proof challenge"** (one 5-minute task a day: freeze credit, set a family code word, turn on carrier spam blocking...) | 7 Shorts plus a carousel checklist. Peak: holiday visits | Family Kit ($9) |
| **Dinero Simple** | **"Reto de diciembre: $10 al día"** or **"Reto de las remesas"** (cut fees on sending money home) | Weekly check-ins in Spanish | Spanish templates (idea #5 in the product memo) |

**Rules for challenge posts:**
1. **Numbers are an example, not a promise.** "Example: $300 in 8 weeks if you..." Never "you will save".
2. **No fake personal progress.** We're faceless, so there's no invented "I saved $X" story. Either show the
   plan's maths, or show a fictional example labelled "Example (fictional)". Use real community results only
   with permission.
3. **Each check-in stands alone,** so a new viewer understands it without day 1. It also links day 1 (Shorts
   related-video link on YouTube; a pinned comment on TikTok).
4. **Count the series inside the cadence caps** (`launch-playbook.md`). Replace weaker slots; don't add on
   top.

## 3. Hook fixes for the packs (cheap)

- **Remove age anchors** from hooks ("If you're 25..." → "If you're starting from zero..."), unless the
  topic really is age-specific (Roth at 18, Medicare at 65).
- **Lead with the rule, then the number:** "5 things I'd never do with a credit card" beats "Credit card
  tips". The `hooks_library.json` mechanics `contrarian` and `numbered` already cover this. Favour them for
  November-December.
- **Milestone reveal** was the top like-rate hook (8.7%), but it needs a real person's real number. For
  faceless brands, use only **brand milestones** ("We fact-checked 100 scam texts this year: here's what
  repeats") or a clearly fictional worked example. Never invent a personal milestone.

## 5. Measure it ourselves (instead of trusting roundups)

1. **Judge at 72 hours.** About 70% of Instagram views come in the first 3 days (Metricool via the 1 Nov
   roundup). `factory metrics` should snapshot at 24 and 72 hours, and `factory report` compares formats on the
   72-hour number.
2. **Tag every post with its hook mechanic** (the hook library already stores `mechanic`). After 4 weeks,
   rank mechanics by sends-per-reach on Instagram and average watch time on TikTok and YouTube.
3. **Trial Reels for format tests** once eligible (`instagram-growth-2026.md`): the challenge day-1 post vs a
   list post on the same topic.
4. **Monthly 15-minute manual scan,** by hand and within the terms:
   - TikTok Creative Center → Trends, filtered to "Financial services" and US, last 30 days.
   - Instagram's own @creators account.
   - Search 5 terms each in TikTok and Instagram ("budget challenge", "no spend", "sinking funds", "1099",
     "scam text").

   Write what's rising in `watchlist.csv` notes. No scraping and no bots.
