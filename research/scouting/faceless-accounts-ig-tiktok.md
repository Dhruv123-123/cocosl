# The biggest faceless finance and scam accounts on Instagram and TikTok, and what we can use

Written 2026-11-07 from public ranking pages, press articles and directories. **No profiles were opened
with scraping tools, and nothing was logged into.** Follower counts are what each source reported on its
date. Where a source didn't give a cadence or hook, the table says so rather than guessing. This adds
Instagram and TikTok to `faceless-teardown.md` (which covers five faceless *YouTube* channels) and
`creators.md` (mostly face-led creators).

## TL;DR

- **The top of finance Instagram is face-led.** Of CreatorRiver's top 30 US finance Instagram accounts
  (followers 207K-3M), only **2 have brand-style names**: Finance Unfolded (585.6K) and Baddies & Budgets'
  cash-stuffing account (298.1K). The other 28 are named people. Engagement is low for all of them
  (0.11%-1.22%, apart from one two-person account at 12.2%).
- **The faceless accounts that scale fall into four formats:**
  1. **News and infographic cards.** @wealth has 15M followers.
  2. **Hands-only "cash stuffing" and budget videos.** Baddies & Budgets: about 628K on TikTok in 2023,
     and supply sales of about $850K in 2022 (CNBC).
  3. **Illustrated character and palette carousels.** @mywealthdiary (109K), @organized.finance (60.2K),
     @themoneyconfidential (24.2K).
  4. **Brand meme and relatable posts.** Revolut gets **11.2% engagement** on 761K followers, roughly ten
     times most personal creators (Buzzlytics, Nov 2026).
- **Scam awareness has no big faceless Instagram or TikTok account in the sources we found.** The large
  scam accounts are on YouTube, and they're voice-led rather than face-led: Scammer Payback (~9.5M) and
  Kitboga (~4M) are mostly voice and screen (`creators.md`). That leaves an opening for a short-form,
  card-based scam account, which is what Scam Decoded is.
- **We found 15 accounts we could check, not 20.** Public rankings rarely tag accounts as faceless, and
  inventing handles to reach 20 would mislead. The `watchlist.py` Instagram Business Discovery call can
  measure these accounts properly once a Meta app exists.

## 2. What the formats have in common

- **One look, repeated.** The illustrated accounts are recognised by a fixed palette and font, never by a
  face. That's the "signature visual per brand" in `faceless-teardown.md`.
- **The product comes from the format.** Baddies & Budgets sells the envelopes it films, and the chart
  account sold the template it charted. Ours should film and chart **our own printables**.
- **Brands win on humour.** Revolut's engagement comes from relatable posts, not product posts.
- **Scam content on short-form is thin.** The big scam accounts are long YouTube videos. Scam Decoded's
  card-based alerts don't have a large faceless competitor on Instagram or TikTok in our sources.

## 3. The three best ideas, and where they go

| # | Idea | Based on | Thread |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Fill it with me" hands-only videos using our own printables**: a 20-40 second overhead clip of hands filling in the savings challenge, budget binder page or bill tracker, ending on "the sheet is in my shop". Weekly. Real hands and a real printout keep it human, which helps on TikTok's AI-content rules | Baddies & Budgets (cash stuffing to $850K of supplies); the chart account's $17 template | Money Made Simple content (and products) |
| 2 | **"Scam alert of the day": one card per weekday** (the scam's name, the exact text, the red callouts, "what to do"), taken from FTC, FBI IC3 and AARP alerts with the source on the card. Fast to make, easy to share, and a feed people can follow like news | @wealth (15M, news cards); no big faceless scam account on IG or TikTok | Scam Decoded |
| 3 | **Relatable freelancer meme cards, twice a week** ("client: can you do it for exposure", "me on Jan 15 paying quarterly taxes"), with the quarterly calendar strip as the brand visual and a tip in the caption | Revolut's 11.2% brand engagement on relatable posts | 1099 Money |
