# 1099 Money: niche scan before January 2027

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only. Not tax advice.** Public information only: channel directories and stats
pages (no scraping of YouTube itself), and the IRS's own announcements, read through IRS pages or
reputable reprints where IRS pages didn't load. 1099 Money is brand 3 in `two-brand-launch-plan.md`, so
this is preparation for the tax season it may launch into.

## TL;DR

1. **The big tax channels talk to business owners, not first-year freelancers.**
   - The large ones sell strategy: Karlton Dennis (about 1.14M subscribers, about 17 uploads a week),
     Mark J Kohler (613K), Toby Mathis (627K), LYFE Accounting (584K).
   - Their topics are S-corps, real estate, "family incorporation" and the "kids tax strategy".
   - ClearValue Tax (2.9M) has moved to macro news: rates, jobs, the election.
   - The channels about the self-employed specifically are small: The Self-Employed Tax Guy (61.7K), The
     Tax Geek (15.3K).

   **Nobody big is doing "your first quarterly payment, explained plainly".** That's 1099 Money's lane.
2. **Three rule changes make January 2027 unusually confusing, and they're our best content:**
   - **The 1099-NEC/MISC threshold rose from $600 to $2,000** for payments made after 31 Dec 2025 (the One
     Big Beautiful Bill Act, sec. 70433), indexed from 2027. **Many freelancers won't get a 1099 for 2026
     work under $2,000, but the income is still taxable.**
   - **The 1099-K threshold is back to $20,000 and 200 transactions** (sec. 70432), so fewer PayPal, Venmo
     and Etsy sellers get one. It's the same message: no form doesn't mean no tax.
   - **The 2026 business mileage rate changed mid-year:** 72.5¢ a mile from 1 Jan, then **76¢ from 1 Jul**
     (IRS Announcement 2026-11, citing fuel costs). It's the first mid-year change since 2022. Drivers
     need to split their 2026 miles at 30 June. Check the Announcement before scripting.
3. **"No tax on tips" (2025-2028, up to $25,000, phased out above $150K single and $300K joint) is a
   question magnet, with an unclear answer for gig drivers.** The final regulations (TD 10044, 13 Apr
   2026) use a closed list of occupations. Secondary summaries name taxi drivers under "transportation and
   delivery" but don't confirm rideshare or delivery app drivers. **Don't say drivers qualify.** Say "check
   whether your job is on the IRS list" and link it. That's the honest, unanswered question.
4. **Date every number.** The Nov 2026 midterm results put 2027 tax changes in play (ClearValue's 4 Nov
   video is about it). Keep a "rules as of [date]" line on every tax video.
