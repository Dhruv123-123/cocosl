# TikTok and Instagram creator money, and the AI and originality rules

Researched 2026-11-07. TikTok's own Community Guidelines page and Meta's newsroom are primary sources; the
rest is trade press and creator-tool blogs, marked as such. Where only a tool vendor's blog makes a claim
and cites nothing, it's marked **unverified**. Treat those as risks to check, not as facts.

## TL;DR

1. **None of our TikToks can earn from Creator Rewards today.** The program only pays for original
   videos **over one minute**, and our `short` format is 30-55 seconds.
2. **The bigger TikTok risk is "fully AI-generated" content.** A creator-tools blog says Creator Rewards
   now excludes videos where AI made everything, visuals and voice. That's our pipeline exactly. TikTok's
   own pages don't confirm it, but if it's true, the fix is a human voice or real screen footage in every
   rewards video.
3. **Instagram doesn't pay per view for accounts our size.** The Reels bonus is invite-only and needs
   5M reel views a month for three months in a row in the US. Instagram money for us is affiliate links,
   our products and later Subscriptions (around 10k followers).
4. **Facebook is where Meta pays for original Reels** (Content Monetization), and our own Reels
   cross-posted to a Page qualify as original. That's the most realistic platform payout in the next 90 days.
5. **On AI labels we're fine:** TikTok gets `is_aigc` on every post and Instagram gets `is_ai_generated`.
   Neither platform requires a label for TTS narration over illustrations, but we label anyway.

**What this means for us:**
- **The account type matters.** Creator Rewards excludes Business accounts. If the brand accounts are
  set up as Business to get the commercial music library or ads tools, they can never join. Set them up
  as **personal accounts**, and check that the Content Posting API connection still works on that account type.
- **The length matters.** Our 30-55 second shorts earn nothing from Rewards at any size. Add a "minute"
  variant of 65-90 seconds (about 170-230 words) for TikTok only, and keep the 30-55 second cut for Reels
  and Shorts, where short wins. The extra 20 seconds should be a worked example, not padding; retention
  still decides reach.
- **Realistic money:** at $1 RPM, 100,000 qualified views a month is about $100. Rewards is a bonus, not
  the business. Shop affiliate at 5k followers suits Scam Decoded (security keys, shredders, safes) the
  same way YouTube Shopping does.
- **The followers bar is the real gate.** 10k followers and 100k monthly views comes before anything
  pays. Until then, TikTok's job is sending people to the newsletter and the products.

### Meta's originality and AI rules (already in `instagram-formats.md`)

- **13 Mar 2026, Facebook:** unoriginal content (re-uploads, stitched clips, narrating other people's
  content, minor edits like borders, captions or speed changes) is deprioritised, and Pages that mostly
  post it can be demonetised. Original Reels views roughly doubled in H2 2025.
  ([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/))
- **30 Apr 2026, Instagram:** aggregator accounts (most posts in 30 days are reposts) lose recommendations
  for photos and carousels. How-to guides and photo series are named as original.
- **31 Aug 2026, Instagram:** profiles that feature an AI-generated *person* must carry the
  "AI-generated profile" label, or their reach is limited.

## 3. Our formats against these rules

| Our format | TikTok | Instagram / Facebook | Risk and fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **30-55 s faceless short** (stills, Ken Burns, captions, TTS) | Allowed in For You if the script is original. **Can't earn Rewards** (under 1 minute), and **may count as fully AI-generated** if that rule is real | Fine for Reels; original under Meta's rules because the script and art are ours | **High for TikTok money, low for reach.** Make a 65-90 s TikTok cut, and give every Rewards video one human element: Dhruv's real voice on the narration, or a screen recording of a real tool |
| **Carousels** (`media/carousel.py`) | TikTok photo mode (not a Rewards format) | **Named as original** ("how-to guides"); carousels had the best views per post in the 2026 benchmark | **Low.** The best Instagram format we have. Keep it |
| **Long explainer** (6-10 min) | Over 1 minute, but long for TikTok; cut 60-90 s segments instead | Too long for Reels | Clip 65-90 s segments from long videos for TikTok, each with its own hook |
| **Cross-posting the same video to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook** | Fine: it's our own original. Strip other platforms' watermarks (the renderer doesn't add any) | Fine; Facebook is where Meta pays originals | **Low.** Never repost other people's clips, and never add only a border or caption to someone else's video |
| **AI labels** | `publish/tiktok.py` sends `is_aigc` from `ai_generated`, which the pipeline always sets to True | `publish/instagram.py` sends `is_ai_generated: true` | **Low.** Over-labelling is safe. Keep `realistic_synthetic_media: false` only while nothing photoreal is generated |
| **Sponsored posts** | `brand_content_toggle` from `meta.sponsored` | Branded posts don't earn bonuses anyway | **Low.** Already handled |
| **AI presenter / persona** | Must be labelled; private-individual likeness banned | Must carry the profile label from 31 Aug 2026 | **Not used.** Keep it that way (`instagram-formats.md`) |
| **Volume** | "High-volume commercial content" is platform manipulation | Aggregator rules target reposts, not volume | **Low-medium.** The cadence cap (2 a day per platform) is fine. Don't run several near-identical brand accounts posting the same videos |

## 4. Fixes, most important first

1. **TikTok accounts: personal, not Business.** It's a setting at account creation. Check each brand's
   TikTok before applying for anything.
2. **A 65-90 s TikTok cut.** Add a TikTok-only length to the writer (`FORMAT_SPECS`) or a per-platform
   length on `short`, and have the TikTok publisher refuse anything under 61 s for Rewards-enrolled
   accounts. The 30-55 s cut stays for Reels and Shorts.
3. **A human element in TikTok Rewards videos.** Record Dhruv's narration for the TikTok cut (Kokoro is
   fine everywhere else), or add a 10-20 s screen recording of the real thing (a credit freeze page, IRS
   Direct Pay, a bank's alert settings). This also covers the YouTube inauthentic-content risk from
   `youtube-monetization.md`, so it's one fix for two platforms.
4. **Cross-post every original Reel to a Facebook Page** and register interest in Content Monetization on
   Facebook for Creators. That's the most realistic platform payout before 10k followers anywhere.
5. **Turn on Instagram Subscriptions at 10k** only if there's something to put behind it (a weekly
   members-only worked example). Otherwise the newsletter does that job for free.
6. **TikTok Shop affiliate at 5k followers** on Scam Decoded, the same product list as YouTube Shopping.
