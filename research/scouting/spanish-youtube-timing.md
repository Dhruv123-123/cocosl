# Spanish YouTube: start before or after the paid reviewer?

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only.** This weighs starting a Spanish (Dinero Simple) YouTube channel now
against starting once a paid Spanish reviewer has checked the launch set. It builds on
`spanish-and-legal-review-options.md`, `dinero-simple-creator-landscape.md`,
`youtube-policy-review-nov-2026.md` and `two-brand-launch-plan.md`. It weighs risks; it isn't legal advice.

## Recommendation

**After the reviewer, but only after the cheap first step, not the full review.**
1. **Now (free):** reserve the handle and set up the channel page, but upload nothing.
2. **January:** a **two-hour paid test** with a student or freelance reviewer, about $40-50 at $20-25 an
   hour. That's Dhruv's call. It shows whether the copy is close or needs heavy work.
3. **February-March:** the reviewer checks the **lean set** (about 10-20 hours, $200-500), plus a $35
   lawyer read of the ITIN and credit pages.
4. **April:** first uploads, timed to Financial Literacy Month and the late-April to 10 May remittance
   season (`seasonal-demand-feb-jun-2027.md`).

This also matches the launch order: Dinero Simple is brand 4, after the brand-3 gates
(`two-brand-launch-plan.md`).

**In the meantime there's a free, low-risk way to reach Spanish speakers:** YouTube's automatic dubbing.
Per press reports from Feb 2026, it's available to all channels in 27 languages, and Spanish has the
"Expressive Speech" voice. It can put Spanish audio tracks on MMS videos with no new channel. It isn't a
substitute for Dinero Simple, whose topics (ITIN, sending money home, scams aimed at immigrants) are
different, but it tests Spanish demand for free. Check in Studio whether dubs can be reviewed or turned
off per video before relying on it.

## Why not start before the reviewer

| Risk | Why it matters for this channel | How bad |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong or awkward Spanish** | The factory's Spanish is machine-written. Our audience is Spanish-first adults and recent arrivals (Pew: 56% of immigrants who arrived in the last 10 years get news mostly in Spanish), who notice unnatural phrasing at once. A channel's first videos set its audience signals | High: hard to undo once people have seen it |
| **Immigration-adjacent claims** | ITIN, credit without an SSN and remittance topics sit next to immigration advice, which only attorneys and accredited representatives may give. "Notario" fraud is a known harm to exactly this audience | High: legal and trust risk |
| **No one to answer comments** | Every brand needs about 15 minutes a day of a person replying (`launch-playbook.md`). Replies need fluent Spanish. Machine-translated replies to people asking about their own money or status are a bad idea | High, unless Dhruv or a helper is fluent |
| **YouTube's originality and spam rules** | Mass-produced AI videos with minimal changes risk limited reach or strikes. A Spanish channel of translated MMS scripts would look exactly like that, and Dinero's thread already rules out ranked remittance comparisons | Medium |
| **Burnout** | The two-brand plan exists because four brands at once is the top risk in `risk-memo.md` | Medium |

## Why not wait for the full 116-175-hour review

| Cost of waiting | Size |
|---|---|
| Losing the handle | Small: reserve it now for free |
| Missing spring 2027 (Financial Literacy Month, the Mother's Day remittance peak) | Medium: the lean-set path still makes April |
| Spending $2,300-4,400 on a full review before any proof of demand | High: the reason to review only the lean set |

## What "ready to upload" means

- The reviewer has signed off the first **10 Shorts**, the welcome emails and the two product pages
  (`factory review-language <id> --by NAME`, which the factory already enforces).
- The ITIN and credit pages have had the lawyer's read, or are left out of the first batch.
- Someone fluent can answer comments for 15 minutes a day.
- Kokoro's Spanish voice (`em_alex` or `ef_dora`) is set, never the English voice (`pipeline-tools-round3.md`).
- Every video carries the Spanish AI disclosure line and links to official Spanish pages (IRS, CFPB, FTC)
  instead of giving individual advice.
