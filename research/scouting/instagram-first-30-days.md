# The first 30 days on Instagram for a faceless finance account (Nov 2026)

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only. Organic only. Nothing has been posted.** It uses dated public studies and
Instagram's own statements, plus the public faceless accounts in `faceless-accounts-ig-tiktok.md`. It's a
30-day plan for Money Made Simple first. The same plan works for Scam Decoded from week 3
(`two-brand-launch-plan.md`). Ranking rules, hashtags and Trial Reels are covered in detail in
`instagram-growth-2026.md`, and throttling rules in `first-100-followers-2026.md`. This file doesn't repeat
them; it turns them into a month.

## TL;DR

1. **Reels find new people, and carousels and images keep them.** For accounts under 10,000 followers,
   Reels reached **4.9% of followers per post**, against 3.5% for images and 2.6% for carousels. They also
   reached about **3× as many non-followers as carousels**. But images had the better interaction rate:
   0.51%, against 0.34% for Reels (Fanpage Karma, 3M+ posts, Jan-Apr 2026). Metricool's study of 24M posts
   found **carousels get 9× the saves of single images**, and single-image reach fell 22% year on year
   (Jun 2026). **So the mix is mostly Reels for reach, carousels for saves, and few single images.**
2. **The faceless accounts that grow repeat one look.** @organized.finance (60.2K) uses one palette and
   font. @wealth (15M) uses news cards. Baddies & Budgets films hands counting cash. None shows a face, and
   each is recognisable from one frame. Ours is the MMS palette and the "one money move" card.
3. **Send-worthy beats like-worthy.** Instagram's head has said sends per reach is the signal that matters
   most for reaching new people. "Send this to your partner" is a natural ask in money content.
4. **Realistic expectations: a few hundred followers in month 1, not thousands.** The only large 30-day
   dataset we found is a growth vendor's own customers: a **median of 346 followers in 30 days**, with the
   middle half between 73 and 549 (Outfame, 981 accounts, Nov 2026). It's vendor-written with no control
   group, so treat it as an optimistic ceiling for an account with no paid help. Stories of
   "0 to 15K in 30 days" are outliers, and are usually selling a course.
5. **Don't restart the account if month 1 is slow.** Mosseri's advice is to keep the existing account and
   pivot unless the change is drastic (Influencer Marketing Hub, May 2025). Fix the hooks, not the handle.

## 1. What's working now, with evidence

| What | Evidence | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Reels for non-follower reach | Fanpage Karma: Reels lead reach per follower even for the smallest accounts, and reach ~3× the non-followers of carousels | 5 Reels a week (the cut-down Shorts) |
| Carousels for saves | Metricool: carousels get 9× the saves of single images and beat them on every main metric | 3 carousels a week. Make the last slide the one people save: a checklist or a number to keep |
| Fewer single images | Metricool: single-image reach −22%, interactions −25% (Jan-Feb 2026 vs 2025). Fanpage Karma: images still lead on interaction rate for small accounts | At most 1 a week, as a "one number" card that's easy to send |
| Watch time over length | Metricool: average Reel watch time doubled to 8.5 seconds | Put the number on screen in the first second. Our Shorts already do this |
| Original only | Instagram, 30 Apr 2026: accounts that mostly repost lose recommendations, now including carousels | Never repost a screenshot or a clip, even with credit |
| Up to 5 hashtags | Instagram, Dec 2025 | 3-5 topic tags. They label the post, they don't grow it |
| One fixed look | The faceless accounts in `faceless-accounts-ig-tiktok.md`, section 2 | One palette, one font, one cover layout for 30 days. Change nothing about the look in month 1 |
| Collabs | Instagram: one post shown to both accounts' followers | From week 3, MMS × Scam Decoded on overlapping topics (credit freezes, fake bank calls) |
| Replying | Buffer: replying to comments gave +21% engagement on Instagram (`first-100-followers-2026.md`) | 15 minutes a day, in the first hour after each post |

## 3. Day-30 review

Use `launch_thresholds.json` and `check_thresholds.py`, with Instagram figures typed into `manual.json`.
Judge every post at 72 hours.

| Look at | If | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Sends per reach, by post | One topic has twice the median | Make 3 more posts on that topic in month 2 |
| Reel 3-second hold (in Insights) | Under 50% on most Reels | Rewrite the first line. Put the number first (`finance-formats-nov-2026.md`) |
| Carousel saves | Under 1% of reach | Make the last slide a keepable checklist |
| Account Status | Anything other than eligible | Stop and fix the cause before posting more |
| Followers | Under 100 | Not a reason to stop. The 90-day check in `launch-metrics-30-days.md` is the decision point |

## 4. What not to do in month 1

- **No bought followers, likes or engagement pods.** Bought "indicators of social media influence" are
  illegal under the FTC's reviews rule, at up to $51,744 per violation (`risk-memo.md`). Growth services
  like the one behind the 346-follower study are a grey area, so we don't use them either.
- **No mass follow and unfollow.** New accounts get action-blocked for bursts. Keep follows to a few dozen
  a day.
- **No link-drop comments** ("check my page"). They're spam under Instagram's guidelines.
- **No redesign.** Changing the look in month 1 resets the recognition that the format depends on.
- **No age anchors in hooks** ("if you're 25…"). `finance-formats-nov-2026.md` found they narrow reach.
