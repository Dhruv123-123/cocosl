# The first 100 followers per platform for faceless finance accounts, 2026

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only. Nothing was posted.** Sources are the platforms' own help and policy pages,
plus large studies (Buffer, Metricool). A lot of "first 1,000 followers" advice online is undated or
unsourced: account "warm-up" rituals, "follow 50 accounts a day", "never post links". This memo leaves it
out unless a platform page or a dataset backs it, and says where nothing reliable exists. It adds to
`instagram-growth-2026.md` (Instagram ranking, Trial Reels, collabs), `launch-playbook.md` (cadence ramps,
the 15-minute daily routine) and `posting-times-2026.md`.

## TL;DR

- **The two things with data behind them are consistency and replying.** Buffer (100K+ users, Jun 2026):
  creators who posted in 20 or more of 26 weeks got **about 450% more engagement per post** than those who
  posted in 4 or fewer. Buffer (nearly 2M posts, through Dec 2025): replying to comments goes with
  **+21% engagement on Instagram** and +9% on Facebook, compared with the same account's posts without
  replies. That's an association, not proof.
- **Posting more doesn't hurt.** Instagram: 6-9 posts a week got about **18% more reach per post** than
  1-2, and follower growth kept rising (Buffer, 2.1M posts). TikTok: median views per post are flat from
  1 to 10 a week (Buffer, 11.4M posts). Shorts: Metricool (800K videos) found no ceiling, and channels
  posting about 5 Shorts a month got about **46% more views per long video**.
- **What gets a new account throttled is written down, and it's mostly about originality and fake
  engagement:** reposts and watermarks (Instagram, TikTok), QR codes and "extremely short clips" (TikTok),
  "like-for-like" and incentivised engagement (TikTok, YouTube), identical "check out my channel" comments
  (YouTube), and mass-produced near-identical videos (YouTube). Our packs already avoid most of these
  (`launch-playbook.md`). Two things to watch are below.
- **Collabs, Remix and Stitch are the official ways to borrow someone else's audience.** Instagram tells
  creators to use Collabs and Remix instead of reposting. TikTok Stitch and Duet need the other video to
  allow them. Use them on **public-interest sources** (FTC, CFPB and IRS videos, news clips with Stitch
  on), not on other finance creators without asking.
- **Commenting on bigger accounts has no data behind it.** No study we found measures it. It's allowed if
  each comment is a real answer, and banned on YouTube if it's "check out my channel". Our rule: comment
  as the brand only where we add a fact, never with a link, and at most a few a day.

## 1. TikTok

| | What the source says | For us |
|---|---|---|
| **Not shown in For You** | "Reproduced or unoriginal content that is imported or uploaded without any new or creative edits", including someone else's watermark; "extremely short clips and exclusively-GIF based videos"; QR codes; "content that tricks or manipulates others as a way to increase engagement metrics" such as "like-for-like" promises; high-harm misinformation while it's being fact-checked ([TikTok FYF standards](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)) | No QR codes on end cards. No "like if you agree" / "follow for part 2" bait. Never upload our own Reels or Shorts exports with another app's watermark |
| **Account-level** | Accounts run "in bulk" or by "unauthorized automation" aren't allowed; several accounts are fine for "authentic creative expression" (`launch-playbook.md`). TikTok also says low views may come from "a lack of community engagement rather than being ineligible" | One account per brand. Never post the same video on two brand accounts |
| **Frequency** | Median views per post: 489 at 1 a week, 506 at 2-5, 487 at 6-10 (Buffer, 11.4M posts). Buffer's regression estimates +17% to +34% per post at higher frequency | Our 5 a week is in the safe range |
| **Stitch and Duet** | The other account must be public and have Stitch on for that video. A Stitch appears only on the creator's profile ([TikTok Help: Stitch](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/stitch)). No dataset measures growth from them | **Scam Decoded:** Stitch a viral "is this a scam?" video or a news clip and explain the tells. **1099 Money:** Stitch "how much should I save for taxes?" questions. Always add something (a fact, the fix), never just react |
| **Comments** | Reply-to-comment-with-video exists. No 2026 dataset for TikTok replies (Buffer's reply study doesn't cover TikTok) | Reply with a video to the best question each week. That's a free script from a real viewer |

## 2. Instagram

| | What the source says | For us |
|---|---|---|
| **Not recommended** | Content that breaks the Recommendation Guidelines reaches followers only. Repeated violations, or a bio or profile photo that breaks them, can make the **whole account** ineligible for a time. Only public accounts are recommended ([Instagram for Creators, 2022, updated 2023](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)) | Check **Settings > Account Status** weekly (already in `instagram-growth-2026.md`) |
| **Lower reach** | Instagram is "less likely to recommend reposts of a reel that's already on Instagram". Noticeable watermarks, and accounts that regularly reshare others' content, are less likely to be recommended ([Tips for improving your reach, 2023](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach)) | Originals only (our packs already are). Export Reels clean from the factory, not from the TikTok app |
| **Search** | Keywords in the handle or name, bio and caption; hashtags in the caption, not the comments | The handles are already brand names. Add a keyword line to each bio ("budgeting · credit · first investing") |
| **Frequency** | 6-9 posts a week: about +18% reach per post and faster follower growth than 1-2 (Buffer, 2.1M posts, Aug 2025) | Our 8 a week is fine (`posting-times-2026.md`, corrected) |
| **Replies** | **+21% engagement**, and about 63% of profiles did better when they replied (Buffer, Dec 2025) | Reply to every comment in the first hour, in the 15-minute routine |
| **Collabs and Remix** | Collabs "create a single post that is shared to both of your followers". Use Remix "instead of reposting" | Our own brands collab from week 2 (`instagram-growth-2026.md`). Remix public-agency Reels (FTC, CFPB) with our explanation |

## 3. YouTube Shorts

| | What the source says | For us |
|---|---|---|
| **Banned growth tactics** | "Posting identical or similar 'check out my channel' messages across hundreds of videos"; "coordinated 'sub-for-sub' schemes"; "offering rewards in exchange for likes, views, or subscribers"; "using automated tools or AI to churn out high volumes of similar content with minimal changes"; maliciously misleading titles or thumbnails. Asking viewers to like, comment or subscribe is fine. Three strikes in 90 days can end a channel ([YouTube spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)) | Never comment-promote on other channels. Vary the structure across Shorts (our packs rotate hooks from the hook library) |
| **Where views come from** | "About 6 in 10 organic views on the platform come from the Shorts feed." Channels with 2K-10K subscribers grew Shorts views by more than 200%. About **83% of a video's interactions arrive in its first ten days** (Metricool, 800K videos, 71K accounts, Aug 2026) | Reply to comments in the first days. Pin a comment that asks a question |
| **Shorts help long videos** | Channels posting about 5 Shorts a month averaged about 46% more views per long video; the benefit faded above 35 a month (Metricool) | Our 20 Shorts a month plus 4 long is in the range |
| **Frequency** | Long videos: 2-4 a week is best per video. Shorts: no ceiling found (Metricool) | 1 long a week is below that, but it's what we can make well. Don't raise it before the originality fixes (`youtube-policy-review-nov-2026.md`) |

## 4. Pinterest

Pinterest isn't a follower platform: people find pins by search, and followers matter little. The rules
and the plan are already in `pinterest-playbook-2026.md` and `pinterest-seo.md`: fresh pins (Tailwind:
**3-5 a week for new accounts**, then 1-3 a day), the same URL no more than once every 7 days, keywords in
titles and boards. Measure Pinterest by outbound clicks and email signups, not followers.

## 5. Where our plans could still trip a rule

1. **The same short goes to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.** That's allowed, since it's our own original on
   each platform. But it must be uploaded **from the factory's clean export**, not saved from one app and
   uploaded to another. TikTok and Instagram both name other apps' watermarks.
2. **Caption calls to action.** Lines like "comment READY and I'll send it" or "follow for part 2" are
   close to TikTok's "tricks or manipulates others as a way to increase engagement metrics". Use plain
   asks ("Questions? Ask below") and put the link in the bio. **Checked on this branch:** the Money Made
   Simple pack uses "Follow for one money move a week" (104 times) and "Save this" (54), both plain asks,
   and no keyword-comment or "part 2" lines. The other brands' packs are on their own branches, so their
   threads should run the same check.

## 6. First 100 followers: the order, per brand

| Week | Do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post at half cadence, every day, from day 1. Bio with keywords. Reply to every comment within an hour | Buffer consistency and replies; `launch-playbook.md` |
| 1-2 | One Stitch or Remix a week of a public-agency or news video, adding the fix | TikTok Stitch; Instagram Remix |
| 2+ | Collab carousels between our own brands, once a week | `instagram-growth-2026.md` |
| 2+ | A reply-with-video to the best question each week (TikTok) | TikTok feature |
| Weekly | Check Instagram Account Status, TikTok post analytics for 0-view posts, and YouTube Studio for warnings | Instagram for Creators; YouTube spam policy |
| 4+ | Collabs with outside creators of a similar size (Dhruv sends) | `instagram-growth-2026.md` |

**What we don't know:** none of these sources reports how long a brand-new faceless account takes to
reach 100 followers. Measure it: `launch-metrics-30-days.md` already tracks followers per platform.
