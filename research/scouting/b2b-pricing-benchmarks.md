# B2B pricing: financial-wellness content for credit unions, employers and libraries, against PR #13

Written 2026-11-07 from public pages. **Docs only.** PR #13's prices (from `launch/b2b/*/README.md`):

| Product | Tiers |
|---|---|
| Credit union scam alert | **$49 / $99 / $149 a month**; 2 free months for the first 3 |
| Employer wellness | **$59 / $129 / $249 a month** by headcount (up to 100, 100-499, 500+) |
| Community Scam Watch (libraries, senior centers) | **$19 a month** per location, **$59** for a system; talks $150 |
| Freelancer client letter (CPA firms) | **$39 / $79 / $129 a month** |

## TL;DR

- **Almost nobody in this market publishes a price.** SmartDollar, Your Money Line, BudgetSimple,
  FinancialFeed and Vested all say "request a quote". G2 rates SmartDollar's cost at the top of its scale.
  **Our public price list is a selling point** for small buyers who don't want a sales call. Keep it on the
  sales sheets.
- **Credit unions:** the one public price we found is CU\*Answers' pre-designed member newsletter,
  **$750 a year for 4 quarterly issues**, and the credit union supplies the content itself. Our $99 tier is
  $1,188 a year for weekly, written, ready-to-send content, so it's priced right. The $49 tier is cheap,
  which is fine as the way in.
- **Employers:** in EBRI's employer survey, **43% spend under $50 per employee per year** on financial
  wellness and 21% spend over $500. Our Starter ($59 a month, up to 100 staff) is **about $7 per employee a
  year** at 100 staff. That's far below what even the low-spending group pays, so there's room to go up. We
  suggest $79 for Starter, but only after the first 3 pilots convert (fix nothing before we have data).
- **Libraries:** library systems pay **$33K-245K a year** per e-resource across a system (San Joaquin
  Valley Library System minutes, Aug 2024: Brainfuse $231,700, LearningExpress $97,915, a Gale package
  $33-63K). Single branches and senior centers have far smaller budgets, so **$19 a location is right for a
  branch**. But a **system price of $59 a month (up to 10 branches) is very low** next to what systems spend.
  Suggest a system tier priced per branch ($12 a branch a month, so $120 for 10), and quote larger systems
  on request. The full proposal is in `b2b-library-tier-note.md`.
- **A new route: sponsorship.** Banzai, used "by over 2,500 partners", is free to schools because a local
  bank or credit union pays for it. The same model fits Community Scam Watch: **a credit union sponsors the
  library's or senior center's scam alert**, with its name on it. One sale covers several locations, and it
  matches the credit-union pitch we already have. Add it as a fourth option on the CU sales sheet.

No public price was found for scam-alert content sold to credit unions. The fraud vendors in that space
(Stickley, Carefull) sell training or monitoring, not member newsletters.

## 2. Suggested changes (for the B2B thread, after the first pilots)

| Product | Now | Suggestion | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| CU scam alert | $49 / $99 / $149 | Keep | — |
| CU scam alert | — | **Add "sponsor a library or senior center"**: $99 a month gives up to 5 local locations Community Scam Watch with "brought to you by [CU]" | Next sales-sheet update |
| Employer wellness | $59 / $129 / $249 | Starter to **$79** once 3 pilots convert | Month 3 |
| Community Scam Watch | $19 a location; $59 a system | Keep $19 for a single location; **system = $12 a branch a month** (see `b2b-library-tier-note.md`) | Now; there are no customers yet |
| CPA client letter | $39 / $79 / $129 | Keep (no benchmark found) | — |

**Why the caution:** these benchmarks are few, old (2019-2024) or quote-only. The model in
`revenue-model-2027.md` already assumes very few sales. Getting the first 3 paying customers matters more
than the price, so the free months and the $49 way in should stay.
