# Newsletter sponsorship: what a finance newsletter earns at 1k, 5k and 10k, and where to sell it

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only.** It adds to `newsletter.md` (which couldn't confirm ad CPMs) and
`sponsor-kit.md` (the rate-card formula and the gate before pitching). Each source below is either
**marketplace data** (real bookings) or a **vendor blog** (unsourced estimates), and the two differ by about
10×.

## TL;DR

- **The only real-booking figure we found is low.** Paved's Q3 2026 marketplace data, from completed
  campaigns over 12 months: **finance averages $7.11 per 1,000 subscribers** per placement (ppc.land,
  30 Aug 2026). Small niche lists earn more per reader (HR, ~24K subscribers, $18.47) than huge ones
  (lifestyle, ~766K, $1.22).
- **Vendor blogs quote $25-75 CPM under 5K subscribers** for finance (SponsorGap, Mar 2026), but name no
  data. Treat that as the most a directly sold, well-matched sponsor might pay, not the norm.
- **What that means per send (one sponsor slot):**

| List size | Marketplace level (Paved finance avg) | Direct-sold ceiling (vendor blogs) | Per month at 4 sends, marketplace level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | ~$7 | $25-75 | ~$28 |
| 5,000 | ~$36 | $125-375 | ~$142 |
| 10,000 | ~$71 | $500-1,500 | ~$284 |

  These are before the marketplace's cut (Paved 30%, Passionfroot 15% on deals it finds). beehiiv's own
  calculator implies about **$0.08 per subscriber a month** (50K list, CPC ads).
- **Below about 5K, sponsorships are small money.** Our own products and affiliate links per email beat a
  $7-36 slot. The `revenue-model-2027.md` line for sponsors stays small in year 1, which this confirms.
- **The platform choice matters:** `newsletter.md` recommends Sender's free plan. **beehiiv's Ad Network and
  Kit Ads only work if you send on beehiiv or Kit.** On Sender, the options are Passionfroot, Paved Booker
  and SparkLoop, which work with any email tool.

## 1. The marketplaces

| Marketplace | Works with Sender? | Minimum list | Creator's cut | How it pays | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **beehiiv Ad Network** | No (beehiiv senders only, paid plan from Lite up) | None stated | Not published | CPM per 1,000 unique opens, or CPC per "payable click"; paid on the 20th for last month, into a beehiiv Wallet | [beehiiv Ad Network FAQ](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/17507491038231) |
| **Kit Ads** | No (Kit senders) | None stated | **76.5%** (Kit keeps 23.5%) | Click-based programmatic and performance ads, plus flat-rate sponsorships; via Stripe; programmatic paid 45 days after month end. | **Paved** | Yes | None stated | Marketplace 70% (Paved takes 30%); **Booker 100%** less Stripe (for deals we find ourselves); Ad Network 50% | Flat sponsorships, or CPC ($1.10-6 per click, per Dupple) | [Paved publisher help](https://www.paved.com/help/articles/8142375-getting-started-as-a-publisher) |
| **Passionfroot** | Yes | None stated | **100% on our own deals** (the brand pays a 5% fee); 85% on deals Passionfroot finds | Storefront where brands book set packages | [Passionfroot pricing](https://help.passionfroot.me/creators/getting-started/pricing) |
| **SparkLoop** (paid recommendations, not ads) | Yes | Not stated (`thank-you-offers.md`: partner links from ~500) | 76.5% (20% + 3.5% fees) | $2-20 per new subscriber we send to another newsletter | [Dupple comparison, 3 Nov 2026](https://dupple.com/learn/newsletter-ad-marketplaces-compared) |

## 2. What to do at each size

| Size | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Under 1,000** | Products and affiliates only. Start SparkLoop partner links around 500 (`thank-you-offers.md`) | Pitch sponsors (the `sponsor-kit.md` gate isn't met) |
| **1,000-5,000** | Open a free Passionfroot storefront and use Paved Booker for any deal we find ourselves (0-5% fees). Sell flat-rate packages: **$25-50 a send, or 4 sends for $120-150**, matching the marketplace rate with a small niche premium | Pay a platform fee to unlock an ad network worth $7-36 a send |
| **5,000-10,000** | Add Paved Marketplace for inbound deals. Re-check whether a beehiiv paid plan's Ad Network would beat its monthly cost (it only pays if the list is on beehiiv) | Sell dedicated sends at vendor-blog prices without our own open and click data |
| **10,000+** | Direct sales to finance brands from `sponsor-kit.md`, priced from our measured clicks | Take sponsors who conflict with the brand (`sponsor-kit.md` list) |

**Finance-specific:** sponsor slots follow the same FTC disclosure rules as affiliate links ("Sponsored"
at the top of the slot), and investment-adviser sponsors bring SEC-required wording
(`affiliates-for-new-creators.md`, section 2).

## 3. Caveats

- Paved's figure is an **average price per 1,000 subscribers across finance deals**, not a CPM on opens, and
  it mixes list sizes. Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates open-based numbers for everyone.
- VoxBooster's roundup cites Paved finance at **$85 per 1,000 subscribers**, which conflicts with the
  $7.11 above. It only links to vendor home pages, so we use the ppc.land report, which describes its
  method (categories with 20+ completed campaigns, past 12 months).
- None of the marketplaces publishes a subscriber minimum. In practice brands filter by size and niche,
  so expect little inbound demand under 5K.
