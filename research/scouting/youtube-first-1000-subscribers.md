# YouTube: the first 1,000 subscribers for a faceless finance channel

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only.** It uses YouTube's own Help Center, the Metricool 2026 study (800K
videos, 71K accounts) and Buffer's datasets. It builds on `youtube-seo-playbook.md` (titles, descriptions,
chapters, end screens, playlists), `youtube-policy-review-nov-2026.md` (originality and AI rules) and
`youtube-monetization.md` (YPP thresholds). No source we found says how long a new faceless channel takes
to reach 1,000. This is the order of work that the evidence supports.

## TL;DR

1. **Shorts find the viewers and long videos keep them.** About **6 in 10 organic views** come from the
   Shorts feed, and channels posting about **5 Shorts a month got about 46% more views per long video**.
   The benefit faded above 35 a month (Metricool, Aug 2026). Our plan of about 20 Shorts and 4 long videos
   a month is in that range.
2. **The first ten days of each video decide most of its engagement.** About **83% of a video's
   interactions arrive in its first ten days** (Metricool). Reply to every comment in that window, and pin
   a comment that asks a question.
3. **Consistency beats bursts.** Creators who posted in 20 or more of 26 weeks got about **450% more
   engagement per post** (Buffer, 100K+ users). Don't stop for a week once you start.
4. **Two free YouTube features are made for small channels, but only after the 500-subscriber mark:**
   **Hype** (500-500,000 subscribers, YPP members only, long videos in their first 7 days, a weekly
   country leaderboard and a "Hyped" badge) and **Collaborations** (co-owned videos recommended to each
   creator's audience; piloted Aug 2025, so check whether Studio offers it). Plan for them at about 500.
5. **What gets a small channel stopped:** identical "check out my channel" comments, sub-for-sub,
   rewarding people for engagement, near-identical mass-produced videos, and misleading titles or
   thumbnails (YouTube spam policy; three strikes in 90 days can end a channel). Scam Decoded's long videos
   with no original element are the biggest risk we have (`youtube-policy-review-nov-2026.md`).

## 1. The milestones and what changes at each

| Subscribers | What opens up | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0-100 | Nothing extra | Daily Shorts (half cadence in week 1, `launch-playbook.md`), 1 long video a week, every Short linking its related long video. Search-first titles (`youtube-seo-playbook.md`) |
| 100-500 | Nothing extra; analytics become usable after about 30 uploads | Check YouTube Studio's "When your viewers are on YouTube" and returning viewers. Move the long-video slot if it's clearly better elsewhere (`posting-times-2026.md`) |
| **500** | **Hype**, but only once in YPP. YouTube's [Hype eligibility page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15509925) requires both YPP and 500-500,000 subscribers. YPP's lower tier opens at 500 subscribers, so check its current terms | Join the lower YPP tier if offered. Ask viewers to hype a new long video **in its first 7 days** (Shorts can't be hyped). Asking is fine; rewarding isn't |
| ~500+ | **Collaborations**, if available | Co-own one long video a month with a similar-size finance or scam channel (Dhruv sends the invite). Our own brands can collaborate where topics overlap (MMS × 1099 Money on taxes, MMS × Scam Decoded on fraud) |
| **1,000** | Full YPP needs **1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 long-form watch hours** (8,000 for new applicants from 1 Feb 2027) **or 10M Shorts views in 90 days** (`youtube-monetization.md`) | Long-form watch hours are the slow part. Shorts revenue is $0 in our model |

## 2. What to do on every upload

| Step | Source |
|---|---|
| Search phrase first in the title, 70 characters at most; use Test & compare on the thumbnail and title | `youtube-seo-playbook.md` |
| Each Short links its related long video; long videos get an end screen and a playlist | `youtube-seo-playbook.md` |
| Pin a comment that asks a question ("What's your number?"); reply to every comment in the first 10 days | Metricool (83% of interactions in 10 days); Buffer replies data |
| One original element per long video (our own chart, worked example, screen recording) | `youtube-policy-review-nov-2026.md` |
| Ask for the subscribe once, at the payoff, not in the first 5 seconds | YouTube: "It's okay to ask viewers to like, comment, or subscribe" |
| No "comment READY and I'll send it" lines, and no giveaways for subscribing | YouTube spam policy (incentivised engagement) |

## 3. The content that converts viewers into subscribers (faceless finance)

From our own teardowns (`faceless-teardown.md`: How Money Works, WhiteBoard Finance and others) and the
Metricool pattern:

- **A series people can binge.** "Money Reset Day 1-7", "Scam of the week", "Quarterly taxes in 15
  minutes". A playlist per series means someone who likes one video gets the next one served.
- **Shorts that are trailers, not standalone tips.** End each Short on "the full walkthrough is linked
  above". The related-video link is the only clickable link in Shorts.
- **One clear promise in the channel banner and trailer:** "One money move a week, explained plainly."
  Subscribers subscribe to a promise.
- **Comment-driven follow-ups.** Turn the best question each week into a Short that replies to that
  comment. It's free, original material, and it tells commenters they get answers.

## 4. A 90-day order per brand

| Days | Do | Expected signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1-14 | Half cadence (`launch-playbook.md`). 1 long video a week. Every Short links its long video | Shorts views; any long-video click-through from Shorts |
| 15-45 | Full cadence (5 Shorts, 1 long a week). Reply to every comment within the 10-day window. First series playlist | Returning viewers in Studio |
| 45-90 | Double down on the series with the most subscribers gained per video (Studio > Content > subscribers). Drop the weakest series | Subscribers per 1,000 views by series |
| At 500 | Hype asks on new long videos; first collaboration; the lower YPP tier if offered | Hype leaderboard placements |

**Stop rules (from `launch-metrics-30-days.md`):** any spam or inauthentic-content warning means hold the
cadence and fix the flagged video before uploading more.
