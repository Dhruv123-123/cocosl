# Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026: plan for our products

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only.** Nothing is listed, discounted or emailed. The product thread (PR #6)
owns listings, and Dhruv presses every button. Dates: **Thanksgiving Thu 26 Nov, Black Friday Fri 27 Nov,
Cyber Monday Mon 30 Nov 2026.** This builds on what `launch/PRODUCTS_CATALOG.md` already plans: "Black
Friday sale is bundles only", 25% off on Etsy, 27-30 Nov.

## TL;DR

1. **Keep the catalog's plan (bundles only, 27-30 Nov), but change *how* the discount is shown.**
   - Most of our products go live **9-16 Nov**. A strikethrough "was $39" price only 11-18 days later is
     risky. Etsy says you can't inflate a price and lower it to look like a sale: "the original price must
     be in place for a substantial amount of time". The FTC's pricing guide needs a former price "openly and
     actively offered for sale, for a reasonably substantial period of time".
   - So for anything listed after about 1 Nov, **use a code for email and social followers, or launch
     pricing, not a strikethrough sale**.
2. **Etsy's Cyber Specials event (23 Nov-1 Dec) asks for at least 25% off, and the opt-in for features
   closed on 27 Oct.** We can't be featured this year. A sale still shows in normal search, so don't
   distort pricing to chase it.
3. **Black Friday isn't our peak. January is.** Etsy searches for "budget planner" are flat through Nov-Dec
   (about 60.5K a month) and peak in **January (74K)** (RankHero, updated 28 Oct 2026). Treat BFCM as a list-
   building and first-reviews weekend, and keep the best offer energy for the **New Year push (26 Dec-15
   Jan)**.
4. **Lead with brand-safe "gift" angles:**
   - Scam Decoded's Family Kit as "the gift that protects your parents".
   - 1099 Money's year-end bundle as "set up 2027 before 1 Jan".
   - MMS bundles as "start 2027 with a plan".
   - Dinero Simple products launch on 7 Dec (after native review), so they skip BFCM and go to December
     remittance and aguinaldo season as planned.

## 1. Platform rules and fees that matter (from official pages)

| Item | Etsy | Gumroad |
|---|---|---|

Overlapping sales: only the highest applies. Bundles don't stack | Discount codes (Checkout → Discounts; can be restricted per product). Our ladder codes already exist on paper (`UPSELL_PATH.md`) |

| Sources | [Etsy: sales and discounts](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014260108), [Etsy: sales events](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360039531534-How-to-Participate-in-Sales-Events-on-Etsy), `launch/etsy/SEO_NOTES.md` | [Dodo Payments, verified Oct 2026](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained) |

**FTC former-price rule** ([16 CFR 233.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/233.1)): a "was" price must be
"the actual, bona fide price at which the article was offered to the public on a regular basis... openly
and actively offered for sale, for a reasonably substantial period of time... honestly and in good faith."

**What's still honest for brand-new listings:**
- **"Bundle price vs buying separately"** (the Toolkit is $39 vs $62 for the five kits separately). That's a
  comparison with real, current component prices, not a former price.
- **A code** ("BLACKFRIDAY: 25% off bundles, 27-30 Nov") sent to followers and email subscribers. No
  strikethrough "was" price.
- **Launch pricing** stated plainly ("launch price $29 until 30 Nov, then $39"), only if the price really
  goes up afterwards.

## 6. What success looks like (and stop rules)

- **Goal:** first reviews and list growth, not revenue. A good BFCM for a 3-week-old shop is 10-30 orders.
- Track per code in Gumroad and Etsy (orders, revenue after fees), and email open and click rates.
- **Stop or change** if a code leaks to coupon sites (set a total-use limit on Etsy codes; Gumroad codes can
  be disabled), or if refund requests spike (check that the files open on phones).
