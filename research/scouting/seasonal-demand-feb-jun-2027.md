# Seasonal demand, February to June 2027: product and content angles per brand

Written 2026-11-07. **Docs only.** Dates are from official or primary pages where they exist. Tax dates are
the standing IRS rules: individual returns and Q1 estimated tax are due **15 April 2027**, and Q2 estimated
tax is due **15 June 2027**. The 2027 filing-season start isn't announced yet, and the IRS usually names it
in January. What our packs already cover is from the brand threads' memory notes: MMS months 1-4 run to
20 Mar 2027; 1099 Money month 7 starts 3 Jun 2027; Scam Decoded's tax pack runs Jan-Apr 2027 and its weekly
plan to 3 Jul 2027.
