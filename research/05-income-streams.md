## Newsletter: why it's the next build

- It's the only channel we own. Faceless brands rely on newsletters and affiliates because sponsors and
  platforms are less reliable for them.
- The factory already has double opt-in capture and a weekly digest (`funnel/`). Pointing the digest at
  beehiiv (or keeping our own sender and joining SparkLoop) turns the list into money from ~500 subs.
- One newsletter can cover all brands at first (sections per brand), then split.

## YouTube policy timeline to plan around

| Date | Change | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Jul 15, 2025 | "Repetitious" renamed "inauthentic content"; mass-produced content ineligible | Keep the human review + original scripting; no near-duplicate uploads |
| Jan 2026 | Enforcement wave removed mass-produced / AI-slop channels from YPP | Faceless AI voiceover over stock with no original commentary is the highest-risk pattern |
| May 27, 2026 | YouTube auto-labels undisclosed photorealistic AI | Keep `realistic_synthetic_media` honest; we already set the disclosure flag |
| Jan 31, 2027 | Deadline to accept new YPP terms | Accept in Studio |
| Feb 1, 2027 | New applicants need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views; Shorts pool needs 10M views/90 days | Long-form watch hours become the way in. Another reason to raise long-form cadence now. |
