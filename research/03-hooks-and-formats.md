# Hooks and formats that are working (Nov 2026)

What the data says about short-form in 2026, turned into rules the factory's writer can follow.
Most datasets here come from tool vendors (viral.app, Revid) measuring their own users, so they show
correlation, not cause. They're still the best public numbers available.

## Headline findings

1. **Same video, same odds on TikTok and Reels.** Across ~150,000 cross-posted pairs, the TikTok copy
   beat the Reel copy exactly half the time. Cross-posting is fine. But a breakout on one platform only
   breaks out again on the other ~23–25% of the time, so don't judge a video by one platform.
2. **Longer is now rewarded on TikTok.** Breakout rate (10x the account's median): over-60s TikToks 8.0%
   vs 4.3% for under 15s; 1–3 minute TikToks 7.5%. Reels are flat across lengths (4.5–5.3%).
   **Implication:** make TikTok cuts 60–90s with real substance; keep Reels/Shorts at 30–45s.
3. **TikTok is slow, Reels is fast.** Reels that hit 100K got 66% of week-one views in day one; TikToks
   only 38%, and 18% of TikTok breakouts had under 1,000 views early. Don't delete or re-post a TikTok
   after a slow first day.
4. **Numbers in the opening line win.** Caption breakout rates: starts with a number 6.8% on TikTok;
   numbered list 7.4% on Reels (baseline ~5%). "This app…" openers are the worst on Reels (2.4%).
5. **Faceless is not a handicap on TikTok.** Among TikToks with 1,000+ likes, faceless videos had a
   higher median (114K) than one-person-on-camera (67K). Videos with little speech also did better.
6. **Animated word-by-word captions** are the standard (97% of captioned exports). We already burn them in.
7. **Under-15s videos get shared most** (1.41 shares per 100 views vs ~1.0). Use very short pieces for
   share-bait facts, longer pieces for watch time.

## Hook families for Money Made Simple (ranked)

| # | Family | Template | Example in our voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specific number** | "[$ amount] is what [ordinary thing] costs you." | "$18,000. That's what a 1% fee costs on a $50K 401(k)." |
| 2 | **Numbered list** | "[N] [things] I'd do before [milestone]." | "3 bank alerts I'd set before payday." |
| 3 | **Real math vs. common belief** | "Everyone says X. Here's the actual math." | "Snowball vs avalanche on $12K of debt. One saves $1,140." |
| 4 | **Milestone / age-gated** | "If you're [age] with [situation], do this first." | "25 with no savings? Don't invest yet. Do this." |
| 5 | **Mistake you didn't know** | "You paid it in full and your score still dropped. Here's why." | (video #06) |
| 6 | **Rate/roast** (series) | "Rating your budget: [income], [rent], [the one line that's killing it]." | New series, see below |
| 7 | **How-to with time box** | "Do this in [10] minutes tonight." | "Freeze your credit in 10 minutes. Free." |

**Avoid:** "This app…" openers, "POV", "Me when…" for finance (all below baseline), and anything that
reads as engagement bait (the guardrails already block "comment X for…").

## Rules to put into the writer prompt

- First line must contain a number, a named mistake, or an age/situation. No greetings.
- Write the hook last, after the payoff is known.
- If it works in 30 seconds, don't pad it to 90. Long TikTok cuts must add a second worked example, not filler.
- Every number shown on screen goes into `claims_to_verify`.
