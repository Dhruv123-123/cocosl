## The ten things to act on

1. **Join Impact and CJ this week**, then apply to SoFi, CIT Bank, Empower, Rocket Money, Monarch, Aura
   and Acorns once ~10 posts are live. Replace the placeholder HYSA link with the first approval.
2. **Flag brokerage and adviser links for compliance review** (FINRA 2210 / SEC Marketing Rule). Banking
   and budgeting-app links are low-friction; Robinhood, M1, Betterment links are not.
3. **Open with a number.** "Starts with a number" and numbered lists are the best-performing caption
   openers (6.8% / 7.4% breakout vs ~5% baseline). "This app…" is the worst on Reels.
4. **Make 60–90s TikTok cuts.** Over-60s TikToks break out ~2x as often as under-15s; keep Reels and
   Shorts at 30–45s.
5. **Don't judge a TikTok on day one.** 18% of TikTok breakouts started under 1,000 views.
6. **Launch a "Rate My Budget" series** from link-in-bio submissions: a faceless take on the audit
   format that built Caleb Hammer's ~7.6M-follower business.
7. **Raise long-form to at least two a month.** Finance long-form pays $13–26 RPM vs $0.04–0.32 for
   Shorts, and from Feb 1, 2027 new YPP applicants need 8,000 watch hours (or 20M Shorts views).
8. **Start the newsletter on beehiiv** (or join SparkLoop): it pays from ~500 subscribers and it's the
   only channel we own.
9. **Next brand: Scam Shield** (fraud and identity theft). Record $15.9B in reported 2025 losses, $20–30
   CPM, and affiliates like Aura ($65+) and NordVPN (up to 40%).
10. **Cheapest second brand: Dinero Simple**, the Spanish-language version of Money Made Simple, with a
    native-speaker review step. Third: **1099 Money** for the ~27.6M full-time US freelancers.
