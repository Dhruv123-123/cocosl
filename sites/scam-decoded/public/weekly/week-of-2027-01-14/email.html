Toll texts come back every January, and this week they're everywhere. Here's the one to delete, plus two that are still going around after the holidays.

Urgent scam alert

"Unpaid toll" texts are back

Arrives by: text · From: a toll agency, from a number or email address you don't know (fictional example)

NOTICE: You have an outstanding toll balance of $6.99. To avoid a $50 late fee and license suspension, pay by 11:59 PM today: ezdrive-tollpay.top

3 tells

  1. A small amount with a big threat attached: late fees, license suspension, today.
  2. A link that isn't your toll agency's real website, often with an odd ending like .top or .xyz.
  3. It arrives as a text from a number you don't know, sometimes even if you haven't driven a toll road.

Do this

  1. Don't tap. Log in to your toll account by typing the agency's website yourself, or call the number on your statement.
  2. Check the real balance there. No balance? It's fake.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM), and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid on that page? Call your card company today, ask for a new card, and dispute the charge.
