How we make money

A site about scams should be easy to check, so here is all of it. Scam Decoded is paid for by people who buy our own guides, and by labelled affiliate links. That's it for now.

What stays free

The scam checker, every scam explainer, the data breach checklist, the weekly newsletter and the printable checklist are free, and they'll stay free. You never need to buy anything to use the advice we give.

1. Our own guides

The main way we get paid is printable guides we write and sell ourselves. When we mention one in a video or post, we say it's ours.

  • The Family Scam-Proofing Kit, $9
  • Protect Your Parents, $29
  • Spot It, Stop It: Scam Awareness Training Pack, $99
  • Scam Decoded for Seniors, $7

2. Affiliate links

Some links go to paid services, and if you sign up through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. These are the partners in our tools comparison:

  • Aura (identity protection)
  • LifeLock (identity protection)
  • Bitwarden (password manager)
  • 1Password (password manager)

Every affiliate link is labelled. A commission never decides what we recommend, and when a free option does the job, we say so first. A credit freeze, for example, is free and blocks most new credit accounts in your name, while paid monitoring tells you after something happens.

3. Sponsors and platform payouts

We have no sponsors today. If we take one, the video or email will say "sponsored" at the start, the sponsor won't see or change any scam explainer before it's published, and we won't take money from the kinds of companies we warn about: crypto trading, debt relief, "recovery" services, or anything that promises returns.

If our videos qualify for YouTube's or TikTok's creator programs, those platforms share some of their ad revenue with us. We don't choose those ads.

How we use AI

We use AI tools to help with visuals and drafts. A human chooses every topic, checks the facts and approves everything before it is published. AI tools help with first drafts, illustrations and captions. Narration on our TikTok videos is a human voice, not an AI one. Every fact in a video lists its official source (FTC, FBI, IRS and others) and is checked against it before posting. Example messages, names and numbers are made up, and we say so.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your email address. Unsubscribe in one click, any time.
  • Our scam checker never sends anything you paste anywhere, and never saves it.
  • We never take money for a review or a ranking.
  • We never give investment or trading tips.
  • We never offer to "recover" lost money. Anyone who does, for a fee, is usually running a second scam.
  • We never use fake reviews. We only quote real buyers, word for word, with permission.
  • We will never message you first asking for money, a code, or your details. Anyone who does is pretending to be us. Report the account, and paste the message into our checker.
