Toll texts, fake bank calls, puppy sellers, "your computer is infected". The stories change every week. Underneath, almost every scam has the same three parts:

- Rush. A deadline, a countdown, "today only", "within 2 hours". Urgency stops you from checking.
- Secrecy. "Don't tell your bank." "Keep this between us." "Leave the dating app and text me." Secrecy stops anyone else from checking.
- A strange way to pay. Gift cards, wire transfers, crypto, a Bitcoin ATM, a payment app to a stranger, cash to a courier. These are fast and nearly impossible to reverse.

See two of the three? Stop. Hang up, close the tab, and check on your own: call back on a number you look up yourself, or type the website in yourself.
