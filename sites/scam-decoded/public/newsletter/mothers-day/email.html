Mother's Day is Sunday. The grandparent scam twist to know, and a 5-minute safe word.

Hi,

Mother's Day is Sunday, and lunch with Mom is the easiest place to have the scam talk. Here's the scam to start with.

This week's scam: the grandparent scam, with a courier

Spotted this week (fictional example): "Grandma, it's me. I was in a car accident and I'm in trouble. Please don't tell Mom. My lawyer is going to call you. He'll send someone to pick up the bail money."

The voice may be a good guess, or cloned from a short clip online. Then a "lawyer" calls with an amount, and a "courier" comes to the house to collect cash. Coming to the door gets around the warning signs banks look for.

The tell

Secrecy plus urgency plus cash. "Don't tell anyone" is the scam talking.

What to do

  1. Hang up and call the family member back on the number you already have, or call another relative.
  2. Already handed over money? Call the police, and if the courier is coming back for more, tell them.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Quick hits

  • A family safe word: pick a word only the family knows, say it out loud (don't text it), and agree that anyone calling in a panic for money has to say it.
  • "Free" Medicare tests: if it wasn't ordered by her own doctor, it's not free.
  • Fake florist sites: order flowers from a shop you've used before or found yourself, not from an ad.

Your 5-minute job this week

At lunch on Sunday, pick the family safe word together. Five minutes, and nobody can fake your voice to your mom.
