January is the busiest month for job hunting, and for job scams. If you or someone you know is looking, this one's for you.

This week's scam: the task job

Spotted this week (fictional example): "Hi! I'm a recruiter with a digital marketing agency. We have flexible remote work, 60–90 minutes a day, rating products for $300–$800 daily. Interested?"

The first payouts are real, $20 or $40. Then your account goes 'negative', and you have to top it up, usually in crypto, to unlock your earnings. That money doesn't come back.

The tell

A job that asks you to pay, deposit or buy anything to get paid. Plus a recruiter you never contacted, offering big pay for little work, moving you to WhatsApp or Telegram.

What to do

- Don't reply. Look the company up yourself and apply on its real careers page.
- Never pay for training, equipment or 'unlocking' tasks.
- Already paid? Stop, even if they say one more deposit releases your money, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Quick hits

- Interviews by text only: real employers do video or in person. Our job scam explainer (https://scamdecoded.netlify.app/scams/job-and-task-scams/) lists the signs.
- A check to buy your work laptop: it'll bounce, and you'll owe the bank what you sent the 'vendor'.
- SSN before an offer letter: that's identity theft, not onboarding.
