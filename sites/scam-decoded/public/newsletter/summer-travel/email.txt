This week's scam: the fake canceled-flight text

Spotted this week (fictional example): "Your flight DL0427 on 05/29 has been canceled due to a schedule change. To rebook at no cost, call our support line at (888) 555-0193."

The number goes to a fake agent who "rebooks" you for a fee, takes your card details and sometimes your frequent flyer login. Fake airline support numbers also show up in search ads and on social media.

The tell

A cancellation you can't see in the airline's own app. Real changes show up there, and the airline's real number is on its own website.

What to do

- Open the airline's app or type its website yourself, and check your booking there.
- Already paid a "rebooking fee"? Call your card company and dispute the charge, then check your real booking.
- Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM), and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Quick hits

- Lookalike hotel sites: book from the hotel's own website, or a booking site you've used before, and pay by credit card.
- Gas pump skimmers: tap to pay when you can, use credit rather than debit, and tug the card reader before you insert.
- Vacation rentals: keep messages and payment on the platform. A host who wants payment by app is the red flag.

Your 5-minute job this week

Turn on card alerts in your bank's app before you leave, so a skimmed card shows up in minutes, not at the end of the month.
