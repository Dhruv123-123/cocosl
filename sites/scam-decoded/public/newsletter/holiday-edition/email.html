This week's scam: the gift card balance text

Spotted this week (fictional example): "Your gift card balance of $100.00 is about to expire. Verify your card number and PIN to keep your funds: giftcard-balance-●●●●.com"

If you got gift cards this year, expect a few of these. The page asks for the card number and PIN, and then the balance is gone.

The tell

A text or email about a gift card you didn't register anywhere. Card companies don't text you about expiring balances.

What to do

  1. Check a balance only on the website printed on the back of the card, or in the store's own app, typed in yourself.
  2. Never type a gift card's number and PIN into a page you reached from a text.
  3. Already did? Call the gift card company right away and ask them to freeze the card.

Quick hits

  • New phone for Mom or Dad? Before it's wrapped up for the night: turn on spam call filtering, install updates, and save their bank's real number as "BANK (real)".
  • Selling unwanted gifts? Meet buyers in person, get paid on the spot, and ignore emails saying a payment is "pending" until you upgrade your account.
  • "Merry Christmas from the IRS"? No. The IRS doesn't text or email you about refunds or penalties.
