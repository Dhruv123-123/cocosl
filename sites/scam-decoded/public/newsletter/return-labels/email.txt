This week's scam: the refund that needs your details

Spotted this week (fictional example): "We're sorry your return couldn't be processed. To receive your refund of $84.50, please confirm your card details here within 24 hours."

It looks like the shop you just returned something to. It isn't. The page takes your card number and login, and the 'refund' never comes.

The tell

A refund that needs you to do something through a link. Real refunds go back to the card you paid with, automatically.

What to do

- Check the return's status in the shop's own app or order page.
- Never enter card details to receive a refund.
- Already did? Call your card issuer and change that account's password.

Quick hits

- New devices: set up a passcode, turn on automatic updates and Find My, and use a unique password for the account the device signs into.
- Smart speakers and doorbells: change the default password and keep the app updated.
- Old phone you're giving away? Sign out of every account and do a factory reset first.

Your 5-minute job this week

Pick the one account that resets all the others, your email, and check where it's signed in. Sign out of any device you don't recognise.
