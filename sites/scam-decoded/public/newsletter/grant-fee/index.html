Scam Alert · issue 18 · 2027-03-25

the "government grant" a friend sent you

Hi,

This one arrives from someone you know, which is why it works. A friend's account messages you about free government money.

This week's scam: the grant fee

Spotted this week (fictional example): "OMG I just got $9,000 from a government grant program, no strings! I saw your name on the list too 😍 Message the agent, he'll process yours today. There's just a $250 delivery fee."

The friend's account was hacked, or copied. The "agent" asks for a fee, then another one for taxes or insurance, and the grant never arrives. Each new victim's account can be taken over to message their friends next.

The tell

Free government money that comes with a fee. The government doesn't charge you to receive a grant, and doesn't pick people from a list on social media.

What to do

  1. Call or text your friend on a number you already have and ask if they really sent it.
  2. Already paid? Call your bank or the payment app today. If you gave your password, change it and turn on two-step sign-in.
  3. Report the account to the platform, and the scam at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Quick hits

  • "FINAL NOTICE: your tax balance is due today": check what you owe only in your IRS Online Account.
  • Real grants: federal grants are listed on Grants.gov, and they're mostly for organizations and specific projects, not cash for individuals.
  • Two-step sign-in on social accounts: it's what stops your account from being the next one sending these.

Your 5-minute job this week

Turn on two-step sign-in for the social media account you use most. Settings, security, two-step. It's how you avoid becoming the "friend" in this email.
