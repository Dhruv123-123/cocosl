SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-15

Puppies for Christmas that don't exist

Arrives by: website or social ad · From: a breeder you found online (fictional example)
"Two puppies left for Christmas delivery! $400 deposit holds yours. We ship nationwide. Pay by Zelle or gift card, no visits due to holiday rush."

3 TELLS
1. You can't visit or see a live video.
2. A deposit by app transfer or gift card.
3. Then extra fees: shipping, a crate, insurance.

DO THIS
1. Don't pay until you've seen the puppy in person or on live video.
2. Search the breeder's photos and name online.
3. Pay by credit card if you pay at all.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid a deposit? Stop paying any extra fees, contact the payment app, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
