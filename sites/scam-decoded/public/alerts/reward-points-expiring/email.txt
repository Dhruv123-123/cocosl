WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-09

"Your reward points expire tonight" texts

Arrives by: text · From: a store or card you use (fictional example)
"Reminder: your 12,400 reward points ($62.00) expire at midnight. Redeem now before they're gone: rewards-redeem-center.top"

3 TELLS
1. A specific dollar value to make it feel real.
2. A tonight-only deadline.
3. A link that isn't the store's real website.

DO THIS
1. Don't tap. Open the store's own app and check your points there.
2. No points there? Delete the text.
3. Forward it to 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Typed your password or card? Change the password, call your card company, and watch for small charges.
