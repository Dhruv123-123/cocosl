SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-14

An online partner whose gift is "stuck in customs"

Arrives by: dating app or DM · From: someone you've never met in person (fictional example)
"Baby, I sent you a gold necklace and $20,000 in cash for us, but customs is holding it. You only need to pay the $1,500 release fee."

3 TELLS
1. A big gift you never asked for.
2. A fee before you can get it.
3. You've never met, and never quite video-called.

DO THIS
1. Don't pay any fee.
2. Talk to a friend or family member about it.
3. Report the profile on the app.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Already paid? Contact your bank or the payment company, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
