Watch for this · 2027-01-21

Bank log-in links that are one letter off

Arrives by: text or email · From: your bank (fictional example)

Your account has been locked after 3 failed log-in attempts. Restore access now: secure-chasse-banking.com/login

3 tells

  1. A locked account you didn't lock.
  2. A link with an extra letter or word in the bank's name.
  3. A log-in page that looks exactly right.

Do this

  1. Don't tap. Open your bank's app or type its website.
  2. Check whether anything's really wrong there.
  3. Forward it to your bank's fraud email or 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Typed your password? Change it now, from the bank's app, and call the bank.
