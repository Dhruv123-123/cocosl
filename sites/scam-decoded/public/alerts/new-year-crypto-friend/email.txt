SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-30

A new online friend with a "sure thing" crypto plan for the new year

Arrives by: text or DM · From: someone who messaged you out of the blue (fictional example)
"My uncle's trading app made me $40,000 this year. Start 2027 right! Put in $500 and I'll show you how to double it in a month."

3 TELLS
1. A stranger who becomes a friend, then talks about money.
2. Promised big returns, fast.
3. A special app or site only they know about.

DO THIS
1. Don't invest through someone you met online.
2. Never move money to an app a stranger picked.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Already invested? Stop sending money; "fees" to withdraw are part of the scam. Report it and contact your bank.
