Disaster relief appeals from charities you've never heard of

Arrives by: text, call or social post · From: a charity with a familiar-sounding name (fictional example)

URGENT: Families lost everything in the storm. Donate $25 now by gift card or Zelle so help arrives tonight. Reply GIVE.

3 tells

  1. Pressure to give right now.
  2. Payment by gift card, crypto or app transfer.
  3. A name close to a well-known charity.

Do this

  1. Look up the charity yourself before you give.
  2. Give on its own website, typed in, by card or check.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid? Contact your card company or the gift card company right away.
