Watch for this · 2026-12-23

Last-minute shops that promise delivery by Christmas

Arrives by: ad or search result · From: a store you've never heard of (fictional example)

Delivery by Dec 24! Order in the next 2 hours. Everything 75% off, store closing. Pay by Zelle for an extra 10% off.

3 tells

  1. A countdown and a huge discount.
  2. A shop with no reviews or real address.
  3. A discount for paying by app transfer.

Do this

  1. Search the shop's name with "scam" or "complaints".
  2. Pay by credit card only.
  3. Buy from stores you already know.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid and nothing came? Dispute the charge with your card company.
