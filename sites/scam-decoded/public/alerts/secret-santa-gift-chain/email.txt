WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-03

The "send 1 gift, get 36 back" holiday exchange

Arrives by: social media post · From: a friend's post or a group invite (fictional example)
"Holiday gift exchange! Send ONE $10 gift to the name at the top, add your name, and get up to 36 gifts back. Only 6 spots, comment IN."

3 TELLS
1. You pay the person at the top of a list of strangers.
2. The payoff only works if new people keep joining, which is how a pyramid works.
3. Pressure to join fast, and you hand out your home address.

DO THIS
1. Skip it. BBB calls it a pyramid scheme.
2. Report the post to the platform.
3. Want a swap? Do one with people you actually know.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Already joined? Don't recruit anyone else, and keep an eye on mail or messages from strangers who now have your address.
