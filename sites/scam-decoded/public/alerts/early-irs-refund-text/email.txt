SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-04

"Your IRS refund is ready" texts, before you've even filed

Arrives by: text · From: the IRS (fictional example)
"IRS: You are eligible for a tax refund of $1,272.50. Verify your identity to receive it: irs-refund-claim.com"

3 TELLS
1. The IRS doesn't start contact by text about a refund.
2. A refund you haven't filed for yet.
3. A link that isn't irs.gov.

DO THIS
1. Don't tap. Check refunds only at irs.gov, typed in.
2. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).
3. Report it to the IRS as the page below explains.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Entered your SSN or bank details? Go to IdentityTheft.gov and the IRS identity theft page for next steps.
