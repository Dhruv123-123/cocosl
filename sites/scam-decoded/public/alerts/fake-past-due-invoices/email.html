Past-due invoices for services your business never ordered

Arrives by: email or mail · From: an SEO, tech support or domain company (fictional example)

FINAL NOTICE: Invoice #44871, Search Optimization Services, $489.00. This account is 30 days past due. Pay today to avoid collections.

3 tells

  1. A "final" or "past due" notice for a bill you never got a first copy of.
  2. Services like SEO, tech support or domain listings that nobody remembers buying.
  3. A deadline and a collections threat to rush whoever opens the mail.

Do this

  1. Match the invoice to a real order or contract. No match, no payment.
  2. Search the company's name with "scam" or "complaint".
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov; forward phishing emails to reportphishing@apwg.org.
