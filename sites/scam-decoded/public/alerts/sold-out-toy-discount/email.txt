WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-11

The sold-out toy everyone wants, at 60% off

Arrives by: marketplace listing or ad · From: a seller you don't know (fictional example)
"IN STOCK! The toy that's sold out everywhere, brand new in box, 60% off. Ships today. Pay by Zelle or gift card for fastest shipping."

3 TELLS
1. Sold out at every real store, but in stock here.
2. A huge discount on the one item everyone wants.
3. Payment by app transfer or gift card, which you can't get back.

DO THIS
1. Buy from the brand or a store you know.
2. Pay by credit card only.
3. Report the listing to the site.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid by app or gift card? Contact the app or card company right away and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
