WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-11-23

Black Friday ads for shops that don't exist

Arrives by: social media ad · From: an online store you've never heard of (fictional example)
"BLACK FRIDAY CLEARANCE! Everything 80% off, today only. Designer boots $29.99. Store closing forever. Shop now →"

3 TELLS
1. Prices far below what anyone else charges.
2. Pressure: today only, closing down, a countdown clock.
3. A shop you've never heard of, with a brand-new website and no real contact details.

DO THIS
1. Search the shop's name with "scam", "review" or "complaints" before you buy.
2. Pay by credit card, so you can dispute the charge if nothing arrives.
3. Report the ad to the platform.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid and nothing came, or something fake? Dispute the charge with your card company, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
