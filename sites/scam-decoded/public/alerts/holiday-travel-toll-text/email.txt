SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-22

Unpaid toll texts during holiday travel

Arrives by: text · From: a toll agency (fictional example)
"TOLL NOTICE: You have an unpaid toll of $4.15 from your recent trip. Pay today to avoid a $35 penalty: toll-roadpay-services.com"

3 TELLS
1. A small toll with a big penalty.
2. It arrives right when you've been traveling.
3. A link that isn't your toll agency's site.

DO THIS
1. Don't tap. Log in to your toll account yourself.
2. No balance there? It's fake.
3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid on that page? Call your card company today and dispute it.
