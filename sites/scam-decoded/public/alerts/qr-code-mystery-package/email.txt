SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-10

A package you didn't order, with a QR code to "see who sent it"

Arrives by: package · From: no sender, just your name (fictional example)
"A gift for you! This package was sent anonymously. Scan the QR code below to find out who sent it and say thank you."

3 TELLS
1. A package you didn't order, with no sender.
2. A note that asks you to scan a code.
3. The code opens a page that wants your details, or installs malware.

DO THIS
1. Don't scan it.
2. Keep the package if you like. The FTC says unordered items are yours.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
