Scam warning · 2026-11-24

"Your package is on hold" texts are spiking before Black Friday

Arrives by: text · From: a delivery company, from a number you don't know (fictional example)

USPS: Your package is on hold due to an incomplete address. Update within 12 hours and pay a $1.99 redelivery fee: usps-redeliver-help.com

3 tells

  1. A text you didn't sign up for, from a regular phone number, about a package you can't name.
  2. A tiny fee and a short deadline. The fee is the bait; the page wants your full card details.
  3. A link that only looks like the shipper's site. The real domain is right before the first single slash.

Do this

  1. Don't tap. Track packages in the shipper's app or by typing its website yourself.
  2. Expecting something? Check the order page on the store's own site or app.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Typed your card number? Call the number on the back of your card and ask for a new one. Then watch your statement for small test charges.
