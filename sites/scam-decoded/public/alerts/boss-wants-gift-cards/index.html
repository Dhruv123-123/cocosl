Scam warning · 2026-12-21

"The boss" needs gift cards for the team, today

Arrives by: text or email · From: your manager or a company owner (fictional example)

Hi, it's Mark. I'm in a meeting and can't talk. I need 5 x $100 gift cards for client holiday gifts. Send me the codes. Keep it quiet for now.

3 tells

  1. Your "boss" can't talk, only text.
  2. Gift cards, and the codes sent back.
  3. Secrecy and a rush.

Do this

  1. Don't buy anything. Call your manager on the number you already have.
  2. Tell your office manager or IT.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Sent codes? Call the gift card company right away with your receipt.
