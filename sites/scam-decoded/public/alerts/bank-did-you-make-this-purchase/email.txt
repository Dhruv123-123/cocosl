SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-08

"Did you make this $1,180 purchase?" bank texts

Arrives by: text, then a call · From: your bank's fraud department (fictional example)
"Fraud alert: did you attempt a purchase of $1,180.00 at an electronics store? Reply YES or NO. A specialist will call you to secure your account."

3 TELLS
1. A text that leads to a call from the "fraud team".
2. They ask for a code, your PIN, or to move money.
3. Caller ID can be faked to look like your bank.

DO THIS
1. Don't reply. Call the number on the back of your card.
2. Never read out a code or move money to "keep it safe".
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Moved money or shared a code? Call your bank on the number on your card right away.
