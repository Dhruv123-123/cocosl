WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-11

"Your new Medicare card is ready, confirm your number" calls

Arrives by: call · From: Medicare (fictional example)
"Hello, this is Medicare. Your new 2027 card is ready to mail, but we need to confirm your Medicare number and bank details first."

3 TELLS
1. A call you didn't ask for.
2. A new card that needs your number confirmed.
3. They ask for bank details too.

DO THIS
1. Hang up.
2. Call the number on your own Medicare card if you're worried.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Gave your number? Call 1-800-MEDICARE and check your statements for services you didn't get.
