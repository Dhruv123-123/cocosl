SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-27

Investment "clubs" in group chats with a professor and big wins

Arrives by: group chat · From: an investing club or "professor" (fictional example)
"Welcome to the VIP Investor Club! Our professor's picks gained 312% last month. Download our trading app to join the next round. Spots close tonight."

3 TELLS
1. Huge returns and screenshots from other "members".
2. A special app you've never heard of.
3. A deadline to get in.

DO THIS
1. Don't download their app or send money.
2. Leave the group and block it.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Already in? Don't pay "fees" or "taxes" to withdraw. Report it and contact your bank.
