"Your $1,299 order is confirmed" emails with a number to call

Arrives by: email · From: a big online store or payment app (fictional example)

Order #A-77812 confirmed: MacBook Pro, $1,299.00. Not you? Call our fraud desk within 24 hours at 1-555-0142 to cancel and get a refund.

3 tells

  1. A big purchase you never made, sent to scare you into acting fast.
  2. A phone number to call, instead of "check your account".
  3. A deadline to cancel. On the call, they'll want remote access to your computer or your bank login "to refund you".

Do this

  1. Don't call the number in the email. Open the store's app or type its website yourself and check your orders.
  2. No order there? Delete the email. It's fake.
  3. Forward it to reportphishing@apwg.org and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Called and let them onto your computer? Disconnect it from the internet, call your bank on the number on your card, and change your passwords from another device.
