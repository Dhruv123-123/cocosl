SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-11-18

Marketplace "buyers" who ask for a code to prove you're real

Arrives by: marketplace chat · From: a buyer for something you listed (fictional example)
"Hi, is the stroller still available? Before I drive over, I want to make sure you're real. I'm sending you a code, just tell me what it says."

3 TELLS
1. The buyer wants to "verify" you before they've even seen the item.
2. A code arrives by text that you never asked for.
3. They push you to read it back. That code lets them set up an account in your name or get into yours.

DO THIS
1. Never share a code someone sends you, with anyone.
2. Keep the chat inside the marketplace app, and sell in person, in a public place.
3. Report the buyer in the app, then block them.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Read out a code? Change the password on your email and phone accounts now, turn on two-step sign-in, and ask your carrier to add a PIN.
