Tax preparers who promise "the biggest refund in town"

Arrives by: flyer, ad or social post · From: a tax preparer (fictional example)

BIGGEST REFUNDS IN TOWN! Fees taken from your refund. Bring your W-2, we'll find credits others miss. No ID needed, sign today.

3 tells

  1. A promise about your refund before they've seen anything.
  2. Fees based on the size of your refund.
  3. They rush you to sign.

Do this

  1. Choose a preparer who will sign the return and has a PTIN.
  2. Never sign a blank or unfinished return.
  3. Check the free IRS options first.
