Real support won't ask for your card or a code in a DM.
Scam warning · Scam of the week · 2026-12-14

Fake "support" accounts that reply to your complaint

Arrives by: social media reply · From: an account using a big brand's logo (fictional example)

So sorry about your order! Please DM us your email, order number and the card you used so we can process your refund today.

3 tells

  1. A reply within minutes of a public complaint.
  2. A name one letter off the real brand.
  3. It asks for card details or a code to "refund" you.

Do this

  1. Don't DM replies. Use the help page on the brand's own website.
  2. Check the brand's real handles on its own site.
  3. Report the fake account.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Shared your card or a code? Call your card company and change the account's password.
