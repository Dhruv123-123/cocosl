SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-07

"Your Social Security number has been suspended" calls

Arrives by: call · From: Social Security or a federal agent (fictional example)
"This is Agent Ross with the Social Security Administration. Your SSN has been suspended due to suspicious activity. Press 1 to avoid arrest."

3 TELLS
1. A threat of arrest or a suspended number.
2. A caller ID that may look official.
3. They want payment or your number to "fix" it.

DO THIS
1. Hang up. Don't press anything.
2. Call the agency on a number from its real website if you're worried.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Gave your SSN or paid? Go to IdentityTheft.gov, and call your bank if you paid.
