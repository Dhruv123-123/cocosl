"Your refund failed, confirm your card" emails

Arrives by: email · From: a store you just returned something to (fictional example)

We couldn't process your refund of $86.40. Confirm your card details within 48 hours to receive it: returns-refund-verify.com

3 tells

  1. It arrives after the holidays, when lots of people have returns.
  2. It asks you to re-enter your card.
  3. A link that isn't the store's real site.

Do this

  1. Don't click. Check the return in the store's app or account.
  2. Delete the email.
  3. Forward it to reportphishing@apwg.org.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Typed your card? Call your card company and ask for a new card.
