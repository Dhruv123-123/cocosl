SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-25

Ghost preparers who won't sign your return

Arrives by: in person or online · From: a tax preparer (fictional example)
"We'll do your taxes for $50 cash. Just sign here and we'll e-file it from our office. We don't put our name on returns."

3 TELLS
1. They won't sign the return or give a PTIN.
2. Cash only, and the refund may go to their account.
3. You don't get to check the return.

DO THIS
1. Use a preparer who signs and has a PTIN.
2. Read the return before you sign it.
3. Make sure the refund goes to your own account.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Used one? Get a copy of the return, check it, and report the preparer to the IRS.
