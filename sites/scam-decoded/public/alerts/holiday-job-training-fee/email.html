The FTC's rule: anyone who asks you to pay to get a job is a scammer.
Scam warning · Scam of the week · 2026-12-07

Holiday warehouse jobs that make you pay for "training"

Arrives by: text · From: a recruiter for a big-name warehouse (fictional example)

You're hired! Seasonal warehouse role, $22/hr, start Monday. To activate your badge, pay the $49 safety training fee by today: hr-onboard-pay.com

3 tells

  1. Hired with no interview, by text.
  2. A fee to start: training, a uniform or a badge.
  3. A payment link that isn't the company's own careers site.

Do this

  1. Don't pay. Real employers pay you, not the other way round.
  2. Apply on the company's own careers page, typed in.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid a fee or sent your bank login? Call your bank now, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
