SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-18

"I'll set up your IRS online account for you" offers

Arrives by: social media or text · From: a "tax helper" (fictional example)
"Tax season tip! Send me your SSN, date of birth and a photo of your ID, and I'll set up your IRS online account and get your refund faster."

3 TELLS
1. A stranger offers to set up your IRS account.
2. They want your SSN and a photo of your ID.
3. A promise of a faster refund.

DO THIS
1. Set up your IRS account yourself at irs.gov.
2. Never share your SSN or ID photo with a "helper".
3. Report the post.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Shared your SSN or ID? Go to IdentityTheft.gov and get an IRS IP PIN.
