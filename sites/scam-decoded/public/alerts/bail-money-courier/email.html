"Your grandson needs bail, a courier will pick up the cash"

Arrives by: call · From: a grandchild, then a "lawyer" (fictional example)

This is his lawyer. Your grandson is in jail after an accident. Bail is $9,000. A courier will come to your house today to collect it. Don't tell anyone.

3 tells

  1. A family emergency, then a "lawyer" or "officer".
  2. A courier sent to your door for cash.
  3. Secrecy.

Do this

  1. Hang up and call your grandchild or their parents.
  2. Never hand cash to a courier.
  3. Call the police if a courier shows up.
