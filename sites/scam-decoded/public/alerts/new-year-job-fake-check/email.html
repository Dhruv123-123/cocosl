A job that sends a check and asks you to send money back is a scam.
Scam warning · Scam of the week · 2027-01-05

A new-year job that mails you a check to buy equipment

Arrives by: email or text · From: an employer you never met (fictional example)

Welcome to the team! We've mailed a $2,800 check. Deposit it, keep $300 for yourself, and send the rest to our equipment vendor by Zelle.

3 tells

  1. Hired with no real interview.
  2. A check before you've done any work.
  3. You're told to send part of it on.

Do this

  1. Don't deposit it or send money anywhere.
  2. Look the company up yourself and call it.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Deposited it and sent money? Call your bank now. When the check bounces, you'll owe that money.
