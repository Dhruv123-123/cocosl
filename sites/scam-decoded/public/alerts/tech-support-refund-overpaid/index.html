Urgent scam alert · 2027-01-13

"We refunded you too much" tech support calls

Arrives by: call or pop-up · From: a tech support company you once paid (fictional example)

Your $299 support plan was cancelled and we sent your refund, but our system added an extra zero. Please let us connect to your computer to fix it.

3 tells

  1. A refund you weren't expecting.
  2. An "overpayment" you must send back.
  3. They want to connect to your computer and your bank.

Do this

  1. Hang up. Don't let anyone connect to your computer.
  2. Check your bank account yourself, not with them.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Let them in or sent money? Disconnect from the internet, call your bank, and change passwords from another device.
