Watch for this · 2026-11-27

"Your personal info is for sale on the dark web" emails

Arrives by: email · From: a "security" or "identity" company (fictional example)

ALERT: We found your email, password and Social Security number for sale on the dark web. Click here to see the full report and remove your data.

3 tells

  1. A scary claim from a company you never signed up with.
  2. A link to "see what was found" or "remove your data".
  3. The page asks for your Social Security number, card or a payment to protect you.

Do this

  1. Don't click. If you're worried, change passwords on the sites you use and turn on two-step sign-in.
  2. Use our free data breach checklist for the steps that matter, including a free credit freeze.
  3. Report the email at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Entered your SSN or card? Freeze your credit at the 3 bureaus, call your card company, and go to IdentityTheft.gov.
