Fake charities ahead of Giving Tuesday

Arrives by: call, text or social post · From: a charity with a familiar-sounding name (fictional example)

Thank you for your past support! Give $50 today for families in need this holiday. Pay by gift card or crypto so 100% goes to the cause.

3 tells

  1. It thanks you for a donation you don't remember making.
  2. Pressure to give right now, on the call or in the text.
  3. It asks for gift cards, crypto, cash or a wire. Real charities don't.

Do this

  1. Look the charity up before you give: search its name plus "complaint", "review" or "scam".
  2. Give through the charity's own website, typed in, by credit card or check.
  3. Report the call or text at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid by card? Call your card company and dispute the charge. Paid by gift card? Call the gift card company now.
