SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-22

Buyers whose payment is "pending until you upgrade"

Arrives by: marketplace chat or email · From: a buyer for something you're selling (fictional example)
"I paid $450 for the couch, but the payment is pending until you upgrade to a business account. Send $150 to upgrade and it releases."

3 TELLS
1. An email that looks like it's from a payment app.
2. Money "on hold" until you pay.
3. A buyer who never wants to see the item.

DO THIS
1. Check your balance in the payment app itself.
2. Never pay to receive money.
3. Report the buyer in the marketplace app.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Sent the "upgrade"? Contact the payment app through the app itself, and your bank.
