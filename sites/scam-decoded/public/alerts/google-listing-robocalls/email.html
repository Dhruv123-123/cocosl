Verifying a Google listing is free, and Google doesn't call to threaten you.
Watch for this · Scam of the week · 2026-12-01

Robocalls saying your business will vanish from Google

Arrives by: robocall · From: a "Google listing specialist" (fictional example)

Final notice: your Google business listing is not verified and will be marked permanently closed. Press 1 now to speak with a listing specialist.

3 tells

  1. A recorded "final notice" about your listing.
  2. A threat that customers won't find you, or you'll be marked closed.
  3. A fee to verify or to rank first. The FTC says verifying a listing is free.

Do this

  1. Hang up. Don't press 1.
  2. Sign in to your Google Business Profile yourself to check it.
  3. Report the call at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
