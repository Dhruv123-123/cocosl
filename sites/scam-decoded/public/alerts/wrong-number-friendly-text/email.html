The "wrong number" text that turns into a friendship

Arrives by: text · From: a stranger who "meant to text someone else" (fictional example)

Hi Linda, it's Amy! Are we still on for lunch Thursday? … Oh sorry, wrong number! You seem nice though. Where are you from?

3 tells

  1. A text to someone else, from a number you don't know.
  2. They keep chatting after the "mistake".
  3. Weeks later: an investment or crypto tip.

Do this

  1. Don't reply, even to say wrong number.
  2. Block and delete.
  3. Forward it to 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Already chatting, and money came up? Stop, don't send any, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
