WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-02

Domain "expiration notices" that aren't from your registrar

Arrives by: letter or email · From: a company that looks like your domain registrar (fictional example)
"DOMAIN NAME EXPIRATION NOTICE for yourbusiness.org. Annual website address listing and search submission: $89. Failure to pay may result in loss of your online presence."

3 TELLS
1. The name is a close variant of yours, like .org instead of .com.
2. The small print sells a "listing" or "search submission", not your registration.
3. It warns you'll lose your website if you don't pay this sender.

DO THIS
1. Sign in to your real registrar account and check the renewal date there.
2. Turn on auto-renew with your registrar.
3. Tell whoever pays bills: domain bills only get paid inside the registrar account.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid? Call your card company or bank and dispute it. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
