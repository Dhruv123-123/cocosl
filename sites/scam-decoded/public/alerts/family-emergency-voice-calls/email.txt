SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-11-19

A family member's voice, crying for money

Arrives by: call · From: a grandchild, son or daughter, or someone "with" them (fictional example)
"Grandma? It's me. I've been in an accident and I'm in trouble. Please don't tell Mom. A lawyer is going to call you, and I need $3,000 in gift cards today."

3 TELLS
1. It's urgent and secret: don't tell anyone else in the family.
2. A second person takes over: a "lawyer", "officer" or "bail agent".
3. They want gift cards, cash, crypto or a wire. Those are hard to get back.

DO THIS
1. Hang up and call the family member on the number you already have for them.
2. Can't reach them? Call someone who's with them, or another relative.
3. Agree on a family code word now, said out loud, never texted.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Sent money? Call the gift card company, bank or wire service right away and ask them to stop it. Then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
