SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-11-20

"Rate products from home, earn $800 a week" job texts

Arrives by: text or WhatsApp · From: a recruiter you never contacted (fictional example)
"Hi! I'm Jenna, a recruiter. Part-time online work: rate products from home, 30 minutes a day, $800 a week. Reply YES to start. Small deposit to unlock higher-paying tasks."

3 TELLS
1. A recruiter you never contacted texts out of the blue.
2. Easy work for a lot of money, with no interview.
3. You have to pay, often in crypto, to unlock tasks or withdraw what you "earned".

DO THIS
1. Don't reply. Real employers don't recruit by surprise text and never ask you to pay to work.
2. Look up the company yourself, and apply on its own careers page.
3. Report the text: forward it to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid in? Stop sending money; there's no withdrawal coming. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and contact your bank or crypto exchange.
