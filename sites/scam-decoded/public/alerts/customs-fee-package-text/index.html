Scam warning · 2026-12-18

"Your package is held at customs" texts

Arrives by: text · From: a shipping company (fictional example)

Your international parcel is held at customs. Pay the $2.95 import duty within 24 hours or it will be returned: parcel-customs-release.com

3 tells

  1. A text about a package you can't name.
  2. A tiny fee and a deadline. The page wants your full card.
  3. A link that isn't the shipper's real site.

Do this

  1. Don't tap. Track it in the shipper's app or website, typed in.
  2. Check the order on the store's own site.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).

Already clicked, paid or shared? Typed your card? Call the number on the back of the card and ask for a new one.
