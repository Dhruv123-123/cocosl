WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-31

"You won the New Year sweepstakes" calls

Arrives by: call or letter · From: a sweepstakes or lottery (fictional example)
"Congratulations! You've won $750,000 and a new car in the New Year sweepstakes. Pay the $499 processing fee today to release your winnings."

3 TELLS
1. You won something you never entered.
2. A fee, tax or charge before you get it.
3. Pressure to pay today, often by gift card or wire.

DO THIS
1. Hang up. Real prizes never cost money to collect.
2. Don't share bank or card details.
3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid a fee? Contact your bank or the gift card company right away.
