WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2027-01-28

Fake IRS letters with a QR code to "claim your refund"

Arrives by: letter · From: the IRS (fictional example)
"Internal Revenue Service: Unclaimed refund of $1,450. Scan the QR code to claim it within 7 days. Have your SSN and bank details ready."

3 TELLS
1. A refund you didn't expect, with a deadline.
2. A QR code to scan.
3. It wants your SSN and bank details.

DO THIS
1. Don't scan it.
2. Check your account at irs.gov, typed in.
3. Report it to the IRS as the page below explains.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Entered your details? Go to IdentityTheft.gov and the IRS identity theft page.
