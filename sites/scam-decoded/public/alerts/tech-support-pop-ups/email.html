The loud "your computer is infected" pop-up

Arrives by: pop-up · From: "Microsoft", "Apple" or "Windows Defender" (fictional example)

WARNING! Your computer has been blocked. Pornographic spyware detected. Do not restart. Call Microsoft Support now: 1-555-0188

3 tells

  1. It's loud, scary and won't close.
  2. It tells you not to restart or turn off your computer.
  3. It gives you a phone number to call. Real security warnings don't.

Do this

  1. Don't call. Close the browser; if it won't close, restart the computer.
  2. Still worried? Run the security software you already have.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
