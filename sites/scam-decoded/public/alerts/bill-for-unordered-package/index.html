Watch for this · 2026-12-29

A bill for a package you never ordered

Arrives by: letter or email · From: a seller you don't know (fictional example)

Thank you for accepting your trial package. Your card will be charged $59.99 unless you call within 3 days to cancel.

3 tells

  1. A "trial" you never signed up for.
  2. A charge unless you call.
  3. A deadline so you call without checking.

Do this

  1. Don't call their number. Check your card statement.
  2. The FTC says unordered items are yours to keep, free.
  3. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
