WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-16

Celebrity "endorsements" in holiday ads

Arrives by: video ad · From: a famous face, maybe made with AI (fictional example)
"As seen on TV! A famous chef says these knives changed her life. Holiday deal: 70% off today only, free gift with every order."

3 TELLS
1. A celebrity pushing a deal they never mention anywhere else.
2. A huge discount, today only.
3. A shop you can't find outside the ad.

DO THIS
1. Search the star's name with the product. Real endorsements show up on their own pages.
2. Buy from stores you know.
3. Report the ad.
