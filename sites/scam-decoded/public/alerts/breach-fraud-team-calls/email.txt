WATCH FOR THIS · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-08

Fake "fraud team" calls after a data breach

Arrives by: call · From: your bank's "fraud department" (fictional example)
"Hi, this is the fraud team at your bank. Your account was included in the recent data breach. To secure it, please read me the 6-digit code we just sent you."

3 TELLS
1. It arrives right after breach news, and uses that news to sound believable.
2. It asks for a one-time code, a password or a PIN. That code signs them in as you.
3. It's urgent, and it wants you to stay on the line rather than call back.

DO THIS
1. Hang up and call the number on your card or statement.
2. Work through our free data breach checklist at scamdecoded.netlify.app/breached: passwords, two-step sign-in, a credit freeze.
3. Report the call at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Read out a code? Change that account's password now from a device you trust, turn on two-step sign-in, and call the bank on the number on your card.
