The "CEO" who emails HR for every employee's W-2

Arrives by: email · From: a company executive (fictional example)

Hi, I need copies of all employee W-2s for 2026 in PDF format for a meeting with the accountants. Please send them to me today. Thanks, CEO

3 tells

  1. An urgent request for everyone's W-2s.
  2. It's from an executive, but the reply-to address is off.
  3. It's January, when W-2s are ready.

Do this

  1. Call the executive on a known number to check.
  2. Have a rule: W-2s never go out by email.
  3. Tell IT and report it.
