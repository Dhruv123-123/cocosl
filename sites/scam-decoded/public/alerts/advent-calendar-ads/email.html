Advent calendar ads that take the money and never ship

Arrives by: social media ad · From: a shop you've never heard of (fictional example)

LAST CHANCE: Luxury beauty advent calendar, 24 full-size products, was $299 now $39.99. Only 12 left. Order now →

3 tells

  1. A famous-looking box at a tiny price.
  2. Pressure: last chance, only a few left.
  3. A shop you can't find anywhere except the ad.

Do this

  1. Go to the brand's own website instead of the ad's link.
  2. Search the shop's name with "complaints" or "scam".
  3. Pay by credit card so you can dispute it.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Paid and nothing came, or a fake tracking number? Dispute the charge with your card company and report the ad.
