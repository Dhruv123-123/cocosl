SCAM WARNING · SCAM OF THE WEEK · 2026-12-17

An online sweetheart who needs money to visit for the holidays

Arrives by: dating app or social DM · From: someone you've never met in person (fictional example)
"I can't wait to spend Christmas with you. My bank card is frozen overseas, can you send $850 for my ticket? I'll pay you back the day I land."

3 TELLS
1. You've never met, and video calls never quite happen.
2. A sudden problem that only your money can fix.
3. Requests for gift cards, a wire, crypto or an app transfer.

DO THIS
1. Don't send money, gift cards or crypto.
2. Tell a friend or family member what's going on.
3. Search their photos online to see if they're stolen.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Sent money? Contact your bank or the payment company, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
