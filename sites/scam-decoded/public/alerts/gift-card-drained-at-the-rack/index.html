Gift cards drained before you even give them

Arrives by: store rack · From: a gift card that was tampered with on the shelf (fictional example)

The card on the rack looks normal, but the PIN strip has been peeled and re-covered, and the barcode has a sticker over it. You load $100. A week later it's empty.

3 tells

  1. A scratched, peeled or re-stuck PIN strip on the back.
  2. A torn or resealed package, or a second barcode sticker on top of the real one.
  3. The balance is gone before the person you gave it to ever used it.

Do this

  1. Check the package and the PIN area before you pay. Pick one from the back of the rack.
  2. Keep the receipt with the card, and give both together.
  3. Empty card? Call the brand on the card right away with the receipt, then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Already clicked, paid or shared? Card already drained? Contact the gift card company now, using the number on the back, and ask about a refund. Keep the receipt.
