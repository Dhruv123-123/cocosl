Online marketplace scams

Scam explainer

What it looks like

Buying: a great deal on a phone, tickets or a puppy, but the seller wants a deposit by payment app before you see it. Selling: a buyer 'overpays' and asks you to send back the difference, sends a fake 'payment pending' email that says you must upgrade your account, or asks you to read them a code to 'verify' you're real.

The tell

Pressure to pay or move off the platform, payment methods with no protection, and anything involving codes or refunds of overpayments.

What to do

  1. Meet in person in a public place and pay or get paid on the spot, or use the platform's own checkout.
  2. Check your payment app or bank yourself before handing anything over. Ignore emails about pending payments.
  3. Never share a verification code, and never refund an overpayment.

Already paid or clicked?

Report the profile on the platform. Call your bank or payment app. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
