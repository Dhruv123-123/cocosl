Fake charity scams

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A call, text or social post asks you to donate to help families this holiday, disaster victims or veterans. The name sounds like a charity you know. They want the money now, by gift card, wire, crypto or payment app, and thank you for a donation you don't remember making.

The tell

Pressure to give right now, and payment by gift card, wire or crypto. Real charities take your money next week too.

What to do

  1. Don't give on the spot. Look up the charity yourself.
  2. Check it at Give.org or Charity Navigator, and use the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search if you want a tax deduction.
  3. Give on the charity's own website, by credit card or check.

Already paid or clicked?

If you paid by card, call your card issuer and dispute the charge. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
