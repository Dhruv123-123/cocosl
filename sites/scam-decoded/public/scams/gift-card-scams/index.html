Gift card scams

Scam explainer

What it looks like

Someone asks you to buy gift cards and read them the numbers on the back: your 'boss' by email, a 'utility' about to cut your power, the 'IRS', a grandchild in trouble, or a seller online. In the holidays, scammers also tamper with cards on the rack so the money lands on their card.

The tell

Gift cards are for gifts. Anyone who asks you to pay with one, for anything else, is scamming you. Once you share the numbers, the money is usually gone.

What to do

  1. Don't buy the card, or don't share the numbers.
  2. Check the request with the person or company on a number you found yourself.
  3. Buy gift cards from behind the counter or online direct, and check the packaging isn't torn.

Already paid or clicked?

Contact the gift card company right away (the number is on the card or its website) and ask for a refund. Keep the receipt and card. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
