Prize and lottery scams

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A call, letter or message says you've won a lottery, sweepstakes or a car, but you need to pay taxes, shipping or a processing fee first. Sometimes they send a check to cover the 'fees' that bounces after you've sent money back.

The tell

Real prizes don't make you pay to collect them. And you can't win a lottery you never entered.

What to do

  1. Don't pay anything or give bank details.
  2. Don't deposit a check from a 'prize' company and send money back.
  3. Hang up and block the number.

Already paid or clicked?

Call your bank if you sent money or deposited a check. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
