Fake bank fraud calls and the 'safe account'

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A text asks if you made a big purchase. You reply no, and someone calls from what looks like your bank's number. They know your name and the last four digits of your card. They say your account is compromised, maybe by someone at your branch, and ask you to move your money to a new 'secure account' while they fix it.

The tell

No real bank asks you to move money to keep it safe. Your bank can freeze your account itself. Caller ID can be faked, so the name on your screen proves nothing.

What to do

  1. Hang up.
  2. Call the number on the back of your card or on your statement.
  3. If there's real fraud, they'll see it.

Already paid or clicked?

Call your bank's real number straight away and ask them to try to recall the transfer. Then report it: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
