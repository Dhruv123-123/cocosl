Family emergency scams

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A text from a 'new number': Hi Mom, I dropped my phone, can you pay a bill for me? Or a call that sounds like a grandchild in trouble who needs bail money now and begs you not to tell anyone. Scammers can clone a voice from a short clip online.

The tell

Panic, urgent money and a reason you can't check. A new number, a request for secrecy, or 'I can't talk right now' are the whole scam.

What to do

  1. Call the person back on the number you already have.
  2. Ask something only they would know, that isn't on social media.
  3. Agree a family safe word tonight. Anyone calling in a panic for money has to say it.

Already paid or clicked?

Call your bank or payment app right away. If you paid by gift card, call the gift card company. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
