Unpaid toll texts

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A text says you owe a small toll, often under $10, and adds a late fee or a threat to your licence if you don't pay today. It ends with a link that looks almost like a toll agency's site.

The tell

A random number, a tiny amount, a same-day deadline and a link. Real toll agencies bill through your account or by mail. The small amount is the bait: the page wants your card number.

What to do

  1. Don't tap the link.
  2. Type your toll agency's website yourself, or open its official app. If you owe anything, it's there.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM) and delete it.

Already paid or clicked?

Call your card issuer and cancel the card. Watch for new charges. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. The FBI also takes reports at IC3.gov.
