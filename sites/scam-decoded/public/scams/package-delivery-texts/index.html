Package delivery texts

Scam explainer

What it looks like

A text or email says a package can't be delivered: the address is incomplete, or there's a small redelivery fee. A link takes you to a page that looks like a delivery company and asks for your details and a card. They spike in November and December, when everyone is expecting something.

The tell

Delivery companies don't text you out of the blue asking for a fee or an address update through a link. If you didn't give them your number for that package, the text isn't from them.

What to do

  1. Don't tap the link or reply.
  2. Track the package with the tracking number from the shop's own order email or app.
  3. Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM) and delete it.

Already paid or clicked?

If you entered a card number, call your card issuer. If you entered a password, change it everywhere you used it. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
