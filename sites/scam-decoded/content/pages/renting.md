# Renting or moving? Check this before you pay anything

Summer is moving season, and it's rental-scam season too. Fake listings, deposits before you've seen the place, and movers who change the price once your things are on the truck.

The free one-page checklist covers:

::: inside
- **Checking a listing:** the address search that catches most fake ones.
- **Seeing it first:** what to ask when you can't visit.
- **Paying safely:** the payment methods only scammers insist on, and the fees to get in writing.
- **Hiring movers:** the registration check, written estimates, and the moving-day red flags.
- **If it goes wrong:** who to call first, and where to report it.
:::
