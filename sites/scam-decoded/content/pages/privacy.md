**Short version:** we collect your email address when you ask for the checklist, use it to send the checklist and our emails, and never sell it.

- **What we collect:** the email address you type in, and which page you signed up from.
- **Where it goes:** our email provider, which sends the emails. It may record opens and clicks; you can block that in most email apps.
- **How to leave:** every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Email us to have your address deleted.
- **The scam checker** at /checker/ runs entirely in your own browser. What you paste is never sent to us or anyone else, and isn't saved.
- **Analytics:** if we turn them on, we use privacy-friendly analytics without advertising cookies.
