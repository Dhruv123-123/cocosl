<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

A code arrives by text. Seconds later someone calls or messages: 'I sent you a code by mistake, can you read it to me?' or 'Read me the code so I can verify it's you.' They might pose as your bank, tech support, or a buyer on a marketplace.

## The tell

**A one-time code is sent when someone tries to sign in or reset your password.** If anyone asks you for it, they're the one signing in.

## What to do

1. Never read out or forward a code. Codes are for typing into a screen, not for saying to a person.
2. Turn on sign-in alerts so you hear about attempts.
3. Use an authenticator app or passkey where you can.

## Already paid or clicked?

Change that account's password now, sign out of all devices, and check its recovery email and phone number. Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
