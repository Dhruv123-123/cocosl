<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

A pop-up with an alarm sound says your computer is infected and to call support now. Or someone calls saying they're from a big tech company and have spotted a problem. They ask to connect to your computer, then charge you to 'fix' it, or stage a fake refund.

## The tell

**Real tech companies don't put phone numbers in error messages, and don't call you out of the blue.**

## What to do

1. Don't call the number.
2. Close the browser. If it won't close, force quit it (Ctrl+Alt+Delete on Windows, Cmd+Option+Esc on a Mac) or restart.
3. Never let anyone who contacted you install remote access.

## Already paid or clicked?

Disconnect from the internet, run your security software, and change passwords from another device. Call your bank if you paid or logged in. Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
