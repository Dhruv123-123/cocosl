<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

It starts with a wrong-number text or a friendly stranger on social media. Weeks of chat later, they show you how much they're making on a crypto platform. The app shows your balance growing. When you try to withdraw, you're told to pay a fee or tax first.

## The tell

**Anyone who promises big, steady returns, or wants you to invest in crypto through a site they chose, is scamming you.** Fees to withdraw your own money mean there's no money.

## What to do

1. Don't invest through a link or app someone you met online sent you.
2. Check any adviser or firm at [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/).
3. Talk to someone you trust before moving money.

## Already paid or clicked?

Stop paying 'fees' to withdraw. Report it to the FBI at [IC3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/) and to the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/). Watch out for 'recovery' services that promise to get it back for a fee.
