<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

A call, text or letter says you owe back taxes, your Social Security number is suspended, or you need a new Medicare card. You'll be arrested or lose benefits unless you pay today, often by gift card, crypto or wire.

## The tell

**Government agencies don't demand immediate payment by gift card, crypto or wire, and don't threaten arrest by phone.** The IRS usually contacts you first by mail.

## What to do

1. Hang up or don't reply.
2. Contact the agency yourself, on a number from its real website or a letter you already have.
3. Never give your Social Security or Medicare number to someone who contacted you.

## Already paid or clicked?

If you paid, contact the payment company right away. Report it to the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/), and Social Security scams at [oig.ssa.gov](https://oig.ssa.gov/).
