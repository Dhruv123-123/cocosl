<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

An email confirms an order or a subscription renewal you didn't make, often for hundreds of dollars, and gives a number to call within 24 hours to cancel. On the phone, a friendly agent offers a refund, asks to connect to your computer, then claims they refunded too much and asks you to send the difference back.

## The tell

**The phone number in the email is the scam.** There's no order. The panic over a big charge is what gets you to call.

## What to do

1. Don't call the number or reply.
2. Log into the real account, or check your card statement. No charge there means no charge.
3. Mark the email as spam and delete it.

## Already paid or clicked?

If you gave remote access or card details, call your bank and get your computer checked. Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
