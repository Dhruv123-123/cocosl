<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

Someone you met on a dating app or social media is warm, attentive and quickly serious. They want to move to WhatsApp. They're always overseas, on a rig or deployed, so they can't video call or meet. Then comes an emergency, a plane ticket, or an investment they want you to try.

## The tell

**Someone you haven't met in person who asks for money, gift cards or crypto, or pushes an investment, is a scammer.**

## What to do

1. Keep talking on the dating app until you've met on video or in person.
2. Reverse search their photos.
3. Never send money to someone you haven't met, and talk to a friend before you do anything.

## Already paid or clicked?

Stop sending money. Report the profile to the app. Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
