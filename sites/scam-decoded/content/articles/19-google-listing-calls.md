<p class="kicker">Scam explainer · small business</p>

## What it looks like

A recording: *"Final notice. Your Google business listing has not been verified and will be marked permanently closed. Press 1 to speak to a listing specialist."* Press 1 and a salesperson offers to verify you, or to get you to the top of search results, for a few hundred dollars, then a monthly fee.

The FTC sued a group of companies for exactly this pitch. It says they weren't connected to Google, the threats were false, and they charged $300 to $700 for "verification", plus monthly fees for a ranking program.

## The tell

**Google doesn't call to threaten your listing.** The FTC says claiming and verifying a Google listing is free and takes minutes. A "final notice" by robocall, a fee to stay listed and promises of the top spot are the giveaways.

## What to do

1. Hang up. Don't press 1; it only marks your number as one that answers.
2. Sign in to your Google Business Profile yourself to check your listing. Verifying it is free.
3. Thinking about paying for marketing? Get the offer in writing, and search the company's name with "complaint" first. No one can promise the top search result.

## Already paid?

Call your card company and dispute the charge, and cancel any monthly billing. Report the call at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
