<p class="kicker">Scam explainer</p>

## What it looks like

An unexpected text or WhatsApp message offers easy remote work: rate products, like videos, 'boost' apps. Pay is high for an hour a day. Early payouts are real. Then your account goes negative and you have to 'top up', often in crypto, to unlock your earnings.

## The tell

**A job that asks you to pay, deposit or buy anything to get paid isn't a job.** Recruiters you never contacted, big pay for little work, and moving to WhatsApp or Telegram are the warning signs.

## What to do

1. Stop replying.
2. Look up the company yourself and apply only through its real careers page.
3. Never pay for training, equipment or 'unlocking' tasks.

## Already paid or clicked?

Stop paying, even if they say one more deposit will release your money. Report it: Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/).
