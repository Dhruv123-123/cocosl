---
alert_date: 2027-01-12
severity: urgent
scam: "Unpaid toll" texts are back
channel: text
sender: a toll agency, from a number or email address you don't know
subject: Scam alert: the $6.99 "unpaid toll" text
preheader: Toll agencies bill through your account or by mail. Don't tap the link.
example: NOTICE: You have an outstanding toll balance of $6.99. To avoid a $50 late fee and license suspension, pay by 11:59 PM today: ezdrive-tollpay.top
flags: $50 late fee and license suspension | 11:59 PM today | ezdrive-tollpay.top
tell_1: A small amount with a big threat attached: late fees, license suspension, today.
tell_2: A link that isn't your toll agency's real website, often with an odd ending like .top or .xyz.
tell_3: It arrives as a text from a number you don't know, sometimes even if you haven't driven a toll road.
do_1: Don't tap. Log in to your toll account by typing the agency's website yourself, or call the number on your statement.
do_2: Check the real balance there. No balance? It's fake.
do_3: Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM), and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid on that page? Call your card company today, ask for a new card, and dispute the charge.
source_title: FTC: Got a text about unpaid tolls? It's probably a scam
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/01/got-text-about-unpaid-tolls-its-probably-scam
explainer: toll-texts
---
Toll text scams come in waves. If one reaches you this week, it's likely reaching your parents too: forward them this alert.
