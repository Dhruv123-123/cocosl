---
alert_date: 2027-01-20
severity: watch
scam: Disaster relief appeals from charities you've never heard of
channel: text, call or social post
sender: a charity with a familiar-sounding name
subject: Scam alert: the disaster appeal that wants a gift card
preheader: Look the charity up before you give, and never pay by gift card.
example: URGENT: Families lost everything in the storm. Donate $25 now by gift card or Zelle so help arrives tonight. Reply GIVE.
flags: URGENT | by gift card or Zelle | help arrives tonight
tell_1: Pressure to give right now.
tell_2: Payment by gift card, crypto or app transfer.
tell_3: A name close to a well-known charity.
do_1: Look up the charity yourself before you give.
do_2: Give on its own website, typed in, by card or check.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid? Contact your card company or the gift card company right away.
source_title: FTC: Giving to charity
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/giving-charity
explainer: fake-charity-scams
