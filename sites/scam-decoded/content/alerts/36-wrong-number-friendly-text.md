alert_date: 2027-01-06
severity: watch
scam: The "wrong number" text that turns into a friendship
channel: text
sender: a stranger who "meant to text someone else"
subject: Scam alert: the friendly wrong number
preheader: Don't reply. These chats often end in an investment pitch.
example: Hi Linda, it's Amy! Are we still on for lunch Thursday? … Oh sorry, wrong number! You seem nice though. Where are you from?
flags: Hi Linda, it's Amy! | Oh sorry, wrong number! | You seem nice though
tell_1: A text to someone else, from a number you don't know.
tell_2: They keep chatting after the "mistake".
tell_3: Weeks later: an investment or crypto tip.
do_1: Don't reply, even to say wrong number.
do_2: Block and delete.
do_3: Forward it to 7726 (SPAM).
if_already: Already chatting, and money came up? Stop, don't send any, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: FTC: How to recognize and report spam text messages
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages
explainer: investment-and-crypto-scams
