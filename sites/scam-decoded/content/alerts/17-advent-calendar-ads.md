scam: Advent calendar ads that take the money and never ship
channel: social media ad
sender: a shop you've never heard of
subject: Scam alert: the advent calendar that never ships
preheader: Search the shop's name plus "complaints" before you pay.
example: LAST CHANCE: Luxury beauty advent calendar, 24 full-size products, was $299 now $39.99. Only 12 left. Order now →
flags: was $299 now $39.99 | Only 12 left | Order now
tell_1: A famous-looking box at a tiny price.
tell_2: Pressure: last chance, only a few left.
tell_3: A shop you can't find anywhere except the ad.
do_1: Go to the brand's own website instead of the ad's link.
do_2: Search the shop's name with "complaints" or "scam".
do_3: Pay by credit card so you can dispute it.
if_already: Paid and nothing came, or a fake tracking number? Dispute the charge with your card company and report the ad.
