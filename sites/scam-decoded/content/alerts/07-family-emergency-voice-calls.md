---
alert_date: 2026-11-19
severity: warning
scam: A family member's voice, crying for money
channel: call
sender: a grandchild, son or daughter, or someone "with" them
subject: Scam alert: the call that sounds just like family
preheader: Voices can be copied. Hang up and call them back on the number you know.
example: Grandma? It's me. I've been in an accident and I'm in trouble. Please don't tell Mom. A lawyer is going to call you, and I need $3,000 in gift cards today.
flags: Please don't tell Mom | A lawyer is going to call you | $3,000 in gift cards
tell_1: It's urgent and secret: don't tell anyone else in the family.
tell_2: A second person takes over: a "lawyer", "officer" or "bail agent".
tell_3: They want gift cards, cash, crypto or a wire. Those are hard to get back.
do_1: Hang up and call the family member on the number you already have for them.
do_2: Can't reach them? Call someone who's with them, or another relative.
do_3: Agree on a family code word now, said out loud, never texted.
if_already: Sent money? Call the gift card company, bank or wire service right away and ask them to stop it. Then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: FTC: Scammers use AI to enhance their family emergency schemes
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/scammers-use-ai-enhance-their-family-emergency-schemes
explainer: family-emergency-scams
