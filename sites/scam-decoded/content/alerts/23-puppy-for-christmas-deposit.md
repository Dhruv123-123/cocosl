scam: Puppies for Christmas that don't exist
channel: website or social ad
sender: a breeder you found online
subject: Scam alert: the Christmas puppy that doesn't exist
preheader: See the puppy in person, or on a live video, before paying anything.
example: Two puppies left for Christmas delivery! $400 deposit holds yours. We ship nationwide. Pay by Zelle or gift card, no visits due to holiday rush.
flags: $400 deposit holds yours | Pay by Zelle or gift card | no visits
tell_1: You can't visit or see a live video.
tell_2: A deposit by app transfer or gift card.
tell_3: Then extra fees: shipping, a crate, insurance.
do_1: Don't pay until you've seen the puppy in person or on live video.
do_2: Search the breeder's photos and name online.
do_3: Pay by credit card if you pay at all.
if_already: Paid a deposit? Stop paying any extra fees, contact the payment app, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
