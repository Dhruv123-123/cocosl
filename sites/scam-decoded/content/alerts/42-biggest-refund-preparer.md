alert_date: 2027-01-15
severity: warning
scam: Tax preparers who promise "the biggest refund in town"
channel: flyer, ad or social post
sender: a tax preparer
subject: Scam alert: the preparer who promises a huge refund
preheader: Never sign a blank return, and pick a preparer who signs with a PTIN.
example: BIGGEST REFUNDS IN TOWN! Fees taken from your refund. Bring your W-2, we'll find credits others miss. No ID needed, sign today.
flags: BIGGEST REFUNDS IN TOWN | Fees taken from your refund | sign today
tell_1: A promise about your refund before they've seen anything.
tell_2: Fees based on the size of your refund.
tell_3: They rush you to sign.
do_1: Choose a preparer who will sign the return and has a PTIN.
do_2: Never sign a blank or unfinished return.
do_3: Check the free IRS options first.
if_already: Already filed with one? Get a copy of your return and check it. Report the preparer to the IRS.
source_title: IRS: Dirty Dozen tax scams for 2026
