alert_date: 2026-12-14
severity: warning
scam: Fake "support" accounts that reply to your complaint
channel: social media reply
sender: an account using a big brand's logo
subject: Scam alert: the fake support reply
preheader: Real support won't ask for your card or a code in a DM.
example: So sorry about your order! Please DM us your email, order number and the card you used so we can process your refund today.
flags: DM us | the card you used | process your refund today
tell_1: A reply within minutes of a public complaint.
tell_2: A name one letter off the real brand.
tell_3: It asks for card details or a code to "refund" you.
do_1: Don't DM replies. Use the help page on the brand's own website.
do_2: Check the brand's real handles on its own site.
do_3: Report the fake account.
if_already: Shared your card or a code? Call your card company and change the account's password.
source_title: BBB: 12 scams of the holidays
source_url: https://www.bbb.org/all/holiday-hq/scams/12-scams-of-christmas
