---
alert_date: 2026-12-01
severity: watch
scam: Robocalls saying your business will vanish from Google
channel: robocall
sender: a "Google listing specialist"
subject: Scam alert: "your business will be removed from Google"
preheader: Verifying a Google listing is free, and Google doesn't call to threaten you.
example: Final notice: your Google business listing is not verified and will be marked permanently closed. Press 1 now to speak with a listing specialist.
flags: Final notice | marked permanently closed | Press 1 now
tell_1: A recorded "final notice" about your listing.
tell_2: A threat that customers won't find you, or you'll be marked closed.
tell_3: A fee to verify or to rank first. The FTC says verifying a listing is free.
do_1: Hang up. Don't press 1.
do_2: Sign in to your Google Business Profile yourself to check it.
do_3: Report the call at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid? Dispute the charge with your card company and cancel any monthly billing.
source_title: FTC: Robocallers' false threat that small businesses will be "removed" from Google
source_url: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2018/05/ftc-challenges-robocallers-false-threat-small-businesses-will-be-removed-google
explainer: google-listing-calls
