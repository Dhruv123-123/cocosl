---
alert_date: 2027-03-03
severity: watch
scam: Free trials that only cost shipping, then charge every month
channel: ad
sender: an online ad for skin cream, supplements or an app
subject: Scam alert: the $4.95 free trial that keeps charging
preheader: If you pay for shipping, it isn't free. Find the cancel terms first.
example: Try it FREE, just pay $4.95 shipping. Offer ends tonight. By ordering you agree to monthly deliveries of $89.95.
flags: just pay $4.95 shipping | ends tonight | monthly deliveries of $89.95
tell_1: "Free" still needs your card, for shipping or a fee.
tell_2: A pre-checked box or tiny line signs you up to repeat charges.
tell_3: No clear way to cancel.
do_1: Find the terms and the cancel steps before you sign up. No terms, no order.
do_2: Check your card statement for repeat charges you didn't expect.
do_3: Still charged after cancelling? Dispute it with your card company.
if_already: Cancel in writing and keep a copy. If charges continue, call the number on the back of your card and dispute them, then send a letter to the billing disputes address.
source_title: FTC: Getting in and out of free trials, auto-renewals and subscriptions
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/getting-and-out-free-trials-auto-renewals-and-negative-option-subscriptions
