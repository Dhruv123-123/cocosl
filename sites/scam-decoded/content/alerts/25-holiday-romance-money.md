alert_date: 2026-12-17
severity: warning
scam: An online sweetheart who needs money to visit for the holidays
channel: dating app or social DM
sender: someone you've never met in person
subject: Scam alert: the sweetheart who needs a plane ticket
preheader: Never send money to someone you've only met online.
example: I can't wait to spend Christmas with you. My bank card is frozen overseas, can you send $850 for my ticket? I'll pay you back the day I land.
flags: spend Christmas with you | My bank card is frozen overseas | send $850 for my ticket
tell_1: You've never met, and video calls never quite happen.
tell_2: A sudden problem that only your money can fix.
tell_3: Requests for gift cards, a wire, crypto or an app transfer.
do_1: Don't send money, gift cards or crypto.
do_2: Tell a friend or family member what's going on.
do_3: Search their photos online to see if they're stolen.
if_already: Sent money? Contact your bank or the payment company, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: FTC: What to know about romance scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams
