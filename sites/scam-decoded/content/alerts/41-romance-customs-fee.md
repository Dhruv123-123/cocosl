---
alert_date: 2027-01-14
severity: warning
scam: An online partner whose gift is "stuck in customs"
channel: dating app or DM
sender: someone you've never met in person
subject: Scam alert: the gift stuck in customs
preheader: If you've never met in person, never send money.
example: Baby, I sent you a gold necklace and $20,000 in cash for us, but customs is holding it. You only need to pay the $1,500 release fee.
flags: I sent you a gold necklace and $20,000 | customs is holding it | pay the $1,500 release fee
tell_1: A big gift you never asked for.
tell_2: A fee before you can get it.
tell_3: You've never met, and never quite video-called.
do_1: Don't pay any fee.
do_2: Talk to a friend or family member about it.
do_3: Report the profile on the app.
if_already: Already paid? Contact your bank or the payment company, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: FTC: What to know about romance scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams
