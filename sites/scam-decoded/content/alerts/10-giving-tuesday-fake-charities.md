---
alert_date: 2026-11-25
severity: watch
scam: Fake charities ahead of Giving Tuesday
channel: call, text or social post
sender: a charity with a familiar-sounding name
subject: Scam alert: the charity that wants a gift card
preheader: Real charities take a check or card, and you can look them up first.
example: Thank you for your past support! Give $50 today for families in need this holiday. Pay by gift card or crypto so 100% goes to the cause.
flags: Thank you for your past support | Give $50 today | Pay by gift card or crypto
tell_1: It thanks you for a donation you don't remember making.
tell_2: Pressure to give right now, on the call or in the text.
tell_3: It asks for gift cards, crypto, cash or a wire. Real charities don't.
do_1: Look the charity up before you give: search its name plus "complaint", "review" or "scam".
do_2: Give through the charity's own website, typed in, by credit card or check.
do_3: Report the call or text at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid by card? Call your card company and dispute the charge. Paid by gift card? Call the gift card company now.
source_title: FTC: Giving to charity
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/giving-charity
explainer: fake-charity-scams
