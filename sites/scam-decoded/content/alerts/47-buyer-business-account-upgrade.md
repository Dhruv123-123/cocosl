---
alert_date: 2027-01-22
severity: warning
scam: Buyers whose payment is "pending until you upgrade"
channel: marketplace chat or email
sender: a buyer for something you're selling
subject: Scam alert: the payment that's "pending"
preheader: Payment apps don't hold money until you pay to upgrade.
example: I paid $450 for the couch, but the payment is pending until you upgrade to a business account. Send $150 to upgrade and it releases.
flags: the payment is pending | upgrade to a business account | Send $150 to upgrade
tell_1: An email that looks like it's from a payment app.
tell_2: Money "on hold" until you pay.
tell_3: A buyer who never wants to see the item.
do_1: Check your balance in the payment app itself.
do_2: Never pay to receive money.
do_3: Report the buyer in the marketplace app.
if_already: Sent the "upgrade"? Contact the payment app through the app itself, and your bank.
source_title: FTC: Selling stuff online? Here's how to avoid a scam
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2022/07/selling-stuff-online-heres-how-avoid-scam
explainer: online-marketplace-scams
