alert_date: 2027-01-27
severity: warning
scam: Investment "clubs" in group chats with a professor and big wins
channel: group chat
sender: an investing club or "professor"
subject: Scam alert: the investment group chat
preheader: Screenshots of wins prove nothing. Don't move money to their app.
example: Welcome to the VIP Investor Club! Our professor's picks gained 312% last month. Download our trading app to join the next round. Spots close tonight.
flags: gained 312% last month | Download our trading app | Spots close tonight
tell_1: Huge returns and screenshots from other "members".
tell_2: A special app you've never heard of.
tell_3: A deadline to get in.
do_1: Don't download their app or send money.
do_2: Leave the group and block it.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Already in? Don't pay "fees" or "taxes" to withdraw. Report it and contact your bank.
source_title: FTC: What to know about cryptocurrency scams
