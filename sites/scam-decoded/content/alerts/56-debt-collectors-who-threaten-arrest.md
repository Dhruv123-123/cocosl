---
alert_date: 2027-03-04
severity: warning
scam: Debt collectors who threaten arrest and won't give an address
channel: call
sender: a debt collector or "legal department"
subject: Scam alert: fake debt collectors threatening arrest
preheader: A real collector must tell you who they are and what you owe. Threats of arrest are a red flag.
example: This is the legal department about your unpaid loan of $2,180. A warrant will be issued today unless you pay now. We can't mail anything.
flags: unpaid loan of $2,180 | A warrant will be issued | can't mail anything
tell_1: A debt you don't recognize.
tell_2: Threats of arrest or the police.
tell_3: They won't give a mailing address or phone number.
do_1: Ask for the collector's name, address and the creditor, in writing. Pay nothing yet.
do_2: Don't recognize it? Dispute it within 30 days of getting that information.
do_3: Report fake collectors at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid or shared bank details? Call your bank now, then check your free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com for debts you don't know.
source_title: FTC: Fake and abusive debt collectors
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/fake-abusive-debt-collectors
explainer: 
---
Real collectors must tell you the creditor and the amount within 5 days of first contact, the FTC says, and stop collecting while you dispute a debt you don't recognize.
