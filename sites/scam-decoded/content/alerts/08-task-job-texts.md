---
alert_date: 2026-11-20
severity: warning
scam: "Rate products from home, earn $800 a week" job texts
channel: text or WhatsApp
sender: a recruiter you never contacted
subject: Scam alert: the job that pays you to "rate products"
preheader: A job that makes you deposit money to unlock your pay is a scam.
example: Hi! I'm Jenna, a recruiter. Part-time online work: rate products from home, 30 minutes a day, $800 a week. Reply YES to start. Small deposit to unlock higher-paying tasks.
flags: I'm Jenna, a recruiter | $800 a week | Small deposit to unlock
tell_1: A recruiter you never contacted texts out of the blue.
tell_2: Easy work for a lot of money, with no interview.
tell_3: You have to pay, often in crypto, to unlock tasks or withdraw what you "earned".
do_1: Don't reply. Real employers don't recruit by surprise text and never ask you to pay to work.
do_2: Look up the company yourself, and apply on its own careers page.
do_3: Report the text: forward it to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.
if_already: Paid in? Stop sending money; there's no withdrawal coming. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and contact your bank or crypto exchange.
source_title: FTC: Paying to earn money? Gamified online job task scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/09/paying-earn-money-gamified-online-job-task-scams
explainer: job-and-task-scams
