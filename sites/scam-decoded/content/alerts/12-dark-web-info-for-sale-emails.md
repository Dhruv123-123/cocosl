alert_date: 2026-11-27
severity: watch
scam: "Your personal info is for sale on the dark web" emails
channel: email
sender: a "security" or "identity" company
subject: Scam alert: the dark web warning that's the real trap
preheader: Don't click to "see what was found". Check your accounts yourself.
example: ALERT: We found your email, password and Social Security number for sale on the dark web. Click here to see the full report and remove your data.
flags: ALERT | Social Security number for sale | Click here to see the full report
tell_1: A scary claim from a company you never signed up with.
tell_2: A link to "see what was found" or "remove your data".
tell_3: The page asks for your Social Security number, card or a payment to protect you.
do_1: Don't click. If you're worried, change passwords on the sites you use and turn on two-step sign-in.
do_2: Use our free data breach checklist for the steps that matter, including a free credit freeze.
do_3: Report the email at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Entered your SSN or card? Freeze your credit at the 3 bureaus, call your card company, and go to IdentityTheft.gov.
source_title: FTC: Did you get an email saying your personal info is for sale on the dark web?
