alert_date: 2026-11-26
severity: warning
scam: The loud "your computer is infected" pop-up
channel: pop-up
sender: "Microsoft", "Apple" or "Windows Defender"
subject: Scam alert: the pop-up that says "call now"
preheader: Real security warnings never ask you to call a number. Close the browser.
example: WARNING! Your computer has been blocked. Pornographic spyware detected. Do not restart. Call Microsoft Support now: 1-555-0188
flags: Your computer has been blocked | Do not restart | Call Microsoft Support now
tell_1: It's loud, scary and won't close.
tell_2: It tells you not to restart or turn off your computer.
tell_3: It gives you a phone number to call. Real security warnings don't.
do_1: Don't call. Close the browser; if it won't close, restart the computer.
do_2: Still worried? Run the security software you already have.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Called and gave them access or paid? Disconnect from the internet, run your security software, change passwords from another device, and call your card company.
source_title: FTC: How to spot, avoid and report tech support scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-spot-avoid-and-report-tech-support-scams
