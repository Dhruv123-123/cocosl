severity: watch
scam: The "send 1 gift, get 36 back" holiday exchange
channel: social media post
sender: a friend's post or a group invite
subject: Scam alert: the "send 1 gift, get 36" exchange
preheader: BBB calls it a pyramid scheme. Skip it and swap gifts with people you know.
example: Holiday gift exchange! Send ONE $10 gift to the name at the top, add your name, and get up to 36 gifts back. Only 6 spots, comment IN.
flags: Send ONE $10 gift to the name at the top | get up to 36 gifts back | Only 6 spots
tell_1: You pay the person at the top of a list of strangers.
tell_2: The payoff only works if new people keep joining, which is how a pyramid works.
tell_3: Pressure to join fast, and you hand out your home address.
do_1: Skip it. BBB calls it a pyramid scheme.
do_2: Report the post to the platform.
do_3: Want a swap? Do one with people you actually know.
if_already: Already joined? Don't recruit anyone else, and keep an eye on mail or messages from strangers who now have your address.
source_title: BBB: The secret sister gift exchange is illegal
