---
alert_date: 2026-12-18
severity: warning
scam: "Your package is held at customs" texts
channel: text
sender: a shipping company
subject: Scam alert: the customs fee text
preheader: Track packages in the shipper's own app, never through a text link.
example: Your international parcel is held at customs. Pay the $2.95 import duty within 24 hours or it will be returned: parcel-customs-release.com
flags: held at customs | $2.95 import duty within 24 hours | parcel-customs-release.com
tell_1: A text about a package you can't name.
tell_2: A tiny fee and a deadline. The page wants your full card.
tell_3: A link that isn't the shipper's real site.
do_1: Don't tap. Track it in the shipper's app or website, typed in.
do_2: Check the order on the store's own site.
do_3: Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).
if_already: Typed your card? Call the number on the back of the card and ask for a new one.
source_title: FTC: Scammers are delivering phishing messages this holiday season
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/12/scammers-are-delivering-phishing-messages-holiday-season
explainer: package-delivery-texts
