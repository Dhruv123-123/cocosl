---
alert_date: 2026-12-30
severity: warning
scam: A new online friend with a "sure thing" crypto plan for the new year
channel: text or DM
sender: someone who messaged you out of the blue
subject: Scam alert: the crypto friend with a sure thing
preheader: A stranger promising big, sure returns is a scam sign.
example: My uncle's trading app made me $40,000 this year. Start 2027 right! Put in $500 and I'll show you how to double it in a month.
flags: made me $40,000 this year | Put in $500 | double it in a month
tell_1: A stranger who becomes a friend, then talks about money.
tell_2: Promised big returns, fast.
tell_3: A special app or site only they know about.
do_1: Don't invest through someone you met online.
do_2: Never move money to an app a stranger picked.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Already invested? Stop sending money; "fees" to withdraw are part of the scam. Report it and contact your bank.
source_title: FTC: What to know about cryptocurrency scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-cryptocurrency-scams
explainer: investment-and-crypto-scams
