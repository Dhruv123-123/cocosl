alert_date: 2027-03-02
severity: warning
scam: Calls from "the FTC" about a prize, a virus or your account
channel: call
sender: the Federal Trade Commission
subject: Scam alert: the FTC isn't calling you
preheader: The FTC never calls to demand money, threaten arrest or promise a prize.
example: This is the Federal Trade Commission. Your identity was used in a crime. Pay the $1,500 bond in Bitcoin today or you will be arrested.
flags: Federal Trade Commission | in Bitcoin | will be arrested
tell_1: The caller uses the FTC's name, sometimes a real employee's.
tell_2: They want money, often in crypto, gift cards or a wire.
tell_3: They threaten arrest, or promise a prize.
do_1: Hang up. The FTC never calls to demand money.
do_2: Don't move money or read out any code, whatever they say.
do_3: Report the call at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid already? Call your bank, or the crypto ATM or app company, right away. Then report it, so it gets counted.
source_title: FTC consumer alert: The FTC won't demand money, threaten you, or promise you a prize
