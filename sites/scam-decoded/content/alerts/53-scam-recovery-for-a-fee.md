---
alert_date: 2027-03-01
severity: urgent
scam: Recovery offices that will get your scam losses back, for a fee
channel: call or email
sender: a "fraud recovery" office, a law firm or a government agency
subject: Scam alert: paid "recovery" of money you lost
preheader: Nobody real charges you upfront to get your money back. This is the second scam.
example: This is the Federal Fraud Recovery Unit. We located $8,400 you lost last year. A $350 processing fee by gift card releases the refund today.
flags: Federal Fraud Recovery Unit | $350 processing fee | by gift card
tell_1: They know you lost money before, and offer to get it back.
tell_2: The refund needs a fee first: processing, retainer or tax.
tell_3: They want gift cards, crypto or a wire.
do_1: Hang up. Look up the agency or firm's number yourself and call it.
do_2: Never pay upfront for a refund, or give account numbers to "deposit" one.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid already? Call the gift card company or your bank on the number on the card right away and ask them to stop or reverse it.
source_title: FTC: Refund and recovery scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/refund-and-recovery-scams
explainer: government-impostor-scams
---
It's National Consumer Protection Week (Feb 28 to Mar 6), the FTC's week to talk about frauds, scams and consumer rights. People who've already lost money are the main target here: scammers trade lists of them.
