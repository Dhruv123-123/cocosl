---
alert_date: 2027-01-13
severity: urgent
scam: "We refunded you too much" tech support calls
channel: call or pop-up
sender: a tech support company you once paid
subject: Scam alert: "we refunded you too much"
preheader: Nobody needs remote access to your computer to give you a refund.
example: Your $299 support plan was cancelled and we sent your refund, but our system added an extra zero. Please let us connect to your computer to fix it.
flags: Your $299 support plan was cancelled | added an extra zero | let us connect to your computer
tell_1: A refund you weren't expecting.
tell_2: An "overpayment" you must send back.
tell_3: They want to connect to your computer and your bank.
do_1: Hang up. Don't let anyone connect to your computer.
do_2: Check your bank account yourself, not with them.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Let them in or sent money? Disconnect from the internet, call your bank, and change passwords from another device.
source_title: FTC: How to spot, avoid and report tech support scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-spot-avoid-and-report-tech-support-scams
explainer: tech-support-scams
