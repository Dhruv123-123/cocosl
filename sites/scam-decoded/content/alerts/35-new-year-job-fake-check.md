alert_date: 2027-01-05
severity: warning
scam: A new-year job that mails you a check to buy equipment
channel: email or text
sender: an employer you never met
subject: Scam alert: the job that sends you a check first
preheader: A job that sends a check and asks you to send money back is a scam.
example: Welcome to the team! We've mailed a $2,800 check. Deposit it, keep $300 for yourself, and send the rest to our equipment vendor by Zelle.
flags: mailed a $2,800 check | keep $300 for yourself | send the rest to our equipment vendor
tell_1: Hired with no real interview.
tell_2: A check before you've done any work.
tell_3: You're told to send part of it on.
do_1: Don't deposit it or send money anywhere.
do_2: Look the company up yourself and call it.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Deposited it and sent money? Call your bank now. When the check bounces, you'll owe that money.
source_title: FTC: How to spot, avoid and report fake check scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-spot-avoid-and-report-fake-check-scams
explainer: job-and-task-scams
