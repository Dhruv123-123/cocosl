---
alert_date: 2026-12-31
severity: watch
scam: "You won the New Year sweepstakes" calls
channel: call or letter
sender: a sweepstakes or lottery
subject: Scam alert: the prize you have to pay for
preheader: If you have to pay to get a prize, it isn't a prize.
example: Congratulations! You've won $750,000 and a new car in the New Year sweepstakes. Pay the $499 processing fee today to release your winnings.
flags: You've won $750,000 | Pay the $499 processing fee | today
tell_1: You won something you never entered.
tell_2: A fee, tax or charge before you get it.
tell_3: Pressure to pay today, often by gift card or wire.
do_1: Hang up. Real prizes never cost money to collect.
do_2: Don't share bank or card details.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Paid a fee? Contact your bank or the gift card company right away.
source_title: FTC: Fake prize, sweepstakes and lottery scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/fake-prize-sweepstakes-and-lottery-scams
