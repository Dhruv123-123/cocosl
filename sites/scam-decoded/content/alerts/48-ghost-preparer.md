---
alert_date: 2027-01-25
severity: warning
scam: Ghost preparers who won't sign your return
channel: in person or online
sender: a tax preparer
subject: Scam alert: the preparer who won't sign
preheader: A paid preparer must sign the return and include a PTIN.
example: We'll do your taxes for $50 cash. Just sign here and we'll e-file it from our office. We don't put our name on returns.
flags: $50 cash | Just sign here | We don't put our name on returns
tell_1: They won't sign the return or give a PTIN.
tell_2: Cash only, and the refund may go to their account.
tell_3: You don't get to check the return.
do_1: Use a preparer who signs and has a PTIN.
do_2: Read the return before you sign it.
do_3: Make sure the refund goes to your own account.
if_already: Used one? Get a copy of the return, check it, and report the preparer to the IRS.
source_title: IRS: Dirty Dozen tax scams for 2026
source_url: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/dirty-dozen-tax-scams-for-2026-irs-reminds-taxpayers-to-watch-out-for-dangerous-threats
explainer: government-impostor-scams
