alert_date: 2026-12-07
severity: warning
scam: Holiday warehouse jobs that make you pay for "training"
channel: text
sender: a recruiter for a big-name warehouse
subject: Scam alert: the holiday job that charges a training fee
preheader: The FTC's rule: anyone who asks you to pay to get a job is a scammer.
example: You're hired! Seasonal warehouse role, $22/hr, start Monday. To activate your badge, pay the $49 safety training fee by today: hr-onboard-pay.com
flags: You're hired! | pay the $49 safety training fee | hr-onboard-pay.com
tell_1: Hired with no interview, by text.
tell_2: A fee to start: training, a uniform or a badge.
tell_3: A payment link that isn't the company's own careers site.
do_1: Don't pay. Real employers pay you, not the other way round.
do_2: Apply on the company's own careers page, typed in.
do_3: Forward the text to 7726 (SPAM).
if_already: Paid a fee or sent your bank login? Call your bank now, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: FTC: Spot holiday job scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/12/spot-holiday-job-scams
explainer: job-and-task-scams
