alert_date: 2026-12-11
severity: watch
scam: The sold-out toy everyone wants, at 60% off
channel: marketplace listing or ad
sender: a seller you don't know
subject: Scam alert: the sold-out toy at 60% off
preheader: If the real stores are sold out, a stranger's bargain usually isn't real.
example: IN STOCK! The toy that's sold out everywhere, brand new in box, 60% off. Ships today. Pay by Zelle or gift card for fastest shipping.
flags: sold out everywhere | 60% off | Pay by Zelle or gift card
tell_1: Sold out at every real store, but in stock here.
tell_2: A huge discount on the one item everyone wants.
tell_3: Payment by app transfer or gift card, which you can't get back.
do_1: Buy from the brand or a store you know.
do_2: Pay by credit card only.
do_3: Report the listing to the site.
if_already: Paid by app or gift card? Contact the app or card company right away and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
source_title: BBB: 12 scams of the holidays
