---
alert_date: 2026-11-30
severity: warning
scam: Past-due invoices for services your business never ordered
channel: email or mail
sender: an SEO, tech support or domain company
subject: Scam alert: the past-due invoice nobody ordered
preheader: No purchase order, no payment. Match every bill to a real order first.
example: FINAL NOTICE: Invoice #44871, Search Optimization Services, $489.00. This account is 30 days past due. Pay today to avoid collections.
flags: FINAL NOTICE | Search Optimization Services | 30 days past due
tell_1: A "final" or "past due" notice for a bill you never got a first copy of.
tell_2: Services like SEO, tech support or domain listings that nobody remembers buying.
tell_3: A deadline and a collections threat to rush whoever opens the mail.
do_1: Match the invoice to a real order or contract. No match, no payment.
do_2: Search the company's name with "scam" or "complaint".
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov; forward phishing emails to reportphishing@apwg.org.
if_already: Paid it? Call your bank or card company now and ask to stop or dispute the payment.
source_title: FTC: Run a small business? Pay your bills, not scammers
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/05/run-small-business-pay-your-bills-not-scammers
explainer: fake-invoices
