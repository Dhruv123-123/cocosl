---
alert_date: 2026-12-21
severity: warning
scam: "The boss" needs gift cards for the team, today
channel: text or email
sender: your manager or a company owner
subject: Scam alert: the boss who needs gift cards
preheader: Anyone who asks you to pay with gift cards is a scammer.
example: Hi, it's Mark. I'm in a meeting and can't talk. I need 5 x $100 gift cards for client holiday gifts. Send me the codes. Keep it quiet for now.
flags: I'm in a meeting and can't talk | Send me the codes | Keep it quiet
tell_1: Your "boss" can't talk, only text.
tell_2: Gift cards, and the codes sent back.
tell_3: Secrecy and a rush.
do_1: Don't buy anything. Call your manager on the number you already have.
do_2: Tell your office manager or IT.
do_3: Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
if_already: Sent codes? Call the gift card company right away with your receipt.
source_title: FTC: Gift card scams
source_url: https://consumer.ftc.gov/gift-card-scams
explainer: gift-card-scams
