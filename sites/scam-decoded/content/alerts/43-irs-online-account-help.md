---
alert_date: 2027-01-18
severity: warning
scam: "I'll set up your IRS online account for you" offers
channel: social media or text
sender: a "tax helper"
subject: Scam alert: the IRS account "helper"
preheader: Only set up your IRS account yourself, at irs.gov.
example: Tax season tip! Send me your SSN, date of birth and a photo of your ID, and I'll set up your IRS online account and get your refund faster.
flags: Send me your SSN, date of birth and a photo of your ID | I'll set up your IRS online account | get your refund faster
tell_1: A stranger offers to set up your IRS account.
tell_2: They want your SSN and a photo of your ID.
tell_3: A promise of a faster refund.
do_1: Set up your IRS account yourself at irs.gov.
do_2: Never share your SSN or ID photo with a "helper".
do_3: Report the post.
if_already: Shared your SSN or ID? Go to IdentityTheft.gov and get an IRS IP PIN.
source_title: IRS: Dirty Dozen tax scams for 2026
