# Fiverr and Upwork listing copy

Marketplaces bring buyers to you, which matters while your own outreach warms up. They take a cut
(Fiverr charges sellers 20% of each order; Upwork charges freelancers a service fee that has changed over
time, so check the current rate) and both have rules about AI work.

## Before you post: AI and platform rules to respect

Policies change; read the current versions before publishing (Fiverr: Terms of Service and Community
Standards, including its AI-services guidance; Upwork: Terms of Service and AI-related help articles).
As of writing, the safe approach on both is:

1. **Say clearly that the work uses AI**, in the title or first line of the description *and* in the
   deliverables. Both marketplaces expect sellers to be transparent with buyers about AI-generated work;
   Fiverr has AI-specific service categories, so list under the AI video category if one fits.
2. **Don't sell fake reviews or testimonials**, or "UGC" that pretends an AI presenter is a real customer.
   Marketplaces ban services that create fake reviews or deceive consumers, and so does the FTC.
3. **No real people's likeness/voice** (celebrity lookalikes, voice clones) without a licence.
4. **Stay on platform.** No email, phone, website or other contact details in gigs, profiles or messages
   before an order, and never take payment off platform for a client you met there. (Link your portfolio
   only in the places each platform allows, e.g. Upwork's portfolio section.)
5. **Only showcase work you have rights to.** Use your 5 portfolio ads from `SAMPLE_ADS.md` (fictional
   brands, labeled as concepts). Don't upload client work without written permission.
6. **No fake engagement** on your own listing: no buying reviews, no review swaps, no asking friends to
   place fake orders. It gets accounts banned.

## Fiverr gig

**Title (max ~80 chars):**
`I will create AI presenter video ads for your product, labeled as AI`

Alt: `I will make vertical AI spokesperson ads for tiktok, reels and shorts`

**Category:** AI Services → AI video (or Video & Animation → UGC/Spokesperson-style videos if no AI
video category fits; choose the one where AI use is expected)

**Search tags (5):** ai video ad, tiktok ad, product video, spokesperson video, reels ad

**Description:**

```
Short vertical video ads for your product, made with an AI presenter and voice and clearly labeled as AI.

Great for small brands that want to test lots of hooks on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
without paying for a shoot.

WHAT YOU GET
- 15-30 second vertical ads (9:16, 1080x1920 MP4)
- Script drafted with AI help from your product facts, edited by a person, and approved by you before production
- AI-generated presenter and/or AI voice, plus your own product photos or footage
- Burned-in captions and licensed music
- An on-screen "AI-generated" label on every ad

IMPORTANT, PLEASE READ
- Presenters are AI-generated, not real people, and are labeled as such.
- These are spokesperson / product-demo ads. I don't make fake customer testimonials or reviews,
  and the presenter will never claim to have used your product.
- I only script claims you can back up. No health cures, guaranteed results or income claims.
- No celebrity or real-person likeness or voice cloning.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
Product link, 5+ product photos or short phone clips, your offer, and key product facts.

Message me before ordering if you're unsure which package fits.
```

**Packages:**

| | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Starter | Growth | Growth+ (paid usage) |
| Description | 4 AI presenter ads, 1 hook each | 8 ads x 3 hooks each for A/B testing | 8 ads x 3 hooks + 90 extra days paid-ads usage |
| Price | $249 | $449 | $549 |
| Delivery | 7 days | 10 days | 10 days |
| Revisions | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| Video length | 30s | 30s | 30s |

**Gig extras:** Fast delivery 48h +$75 · Extra hook per ad +$25 · Translated subtitles +$15/ad ·
Source files +$75. For the monthly retainer, use Fiverr's subscription option if available on your gig
(12 ads/month, $799).

> **Pricing tip:** many new Fiverr sellers start lower to get the first reviews. If you do, change the
> *price*, never the honesty: e.g. a launch Basic at $149 for 3 ads. Raise it once you have real reviews.

**FAQ (Fiverr gig FAQ section):**
- *Are the presenters real people?* No. They're AI-generated and every ad is labeled as AI.
- *Can the presenter say they love/use my product?* No. That would be a fake testimonial. They present and demonstrate it.
- *Can I run these as paid ads?* Yes. Paid-ads use is included for 30 days (Basic) or 90 days (Standard), 180 days (Premium). Organic use on your own channels has no time limit.
- *Do you guarantee sales?* No one honest can. You get well-made, on-brief ads built for testing.
- *What if I have no footage?* Product photos work; phone clips of the product in use work even better.

**Gig images:** 3 stills from your portfolio ads (each showing the AI label) + one image of the package
table. Gig video: a 30–45s reel of the 5 portfolio ads with the "concept ads for fictional brands" label.

## Upwork

### Profile title
`AI Video Ads for DTC Brands | TikTok, Reels & Shorts | Clearly Labeled AI Presenters`

### Profile overview
```
I make short vertical video ads for small brands using AI-generated presenters and voices, always
labeled as AI, plus your real product footage.

Why brands use them: paid social rewards testing lots of creative. AI ads let you test 10+ hooks for
the cost of one traditional shoot, then double down on what works.

What you get on every project:
- Scripts drafted with AI help from your product facts, edited by a person, and approved by you before production
- 15-30s vertical MP4s with captions and licensed music
- 1-3 hook variations per ad for A/B testing
- An on-screen AI label on every ad. I don't make fake testimonials or reviews.

Typical projects: 4 ads ($249), 8 ads x 3 hooks ($449), or 12 ads/month on retainer ($799).
See my portfolio below for concept ads made for fictional brands.
```

### Project Catalog listing (fixed-price "project")
**Title:** `You will get 4 AI presenter video ads for TikTok, Reels and Shorts (labeled AI)`

Tiers: Starter $249 (4 ads, 1 hook, 7 days) · Standard $449 (8 ads, 3 hooks, 10 days) ·
Advanced $549 (Standard + 90 extra days paid-ads usage). Same description and "Important" block as the
Fiverr gig. Upwork's Project Catalog requires the description to match what's delivered; keep the AI
disclosure in the first paragraph.

### Proposal template (for job posts asking for "UGC video ads")

```
Hi {client first name if shown},

{One line showing you read the post, e.g. "You mentioned testing new hooks for your {product}
launch on TikTok, so hook variety seems like the priority."}

Upfront so there are no surprises: I make these with AI-generated presenters and voices, labeled
as AI on screen. They work as spokesperson / product-demo ads rather than real-customer
testimonials. If you specifically need real creators filming themselves using the product, I'm
not the right fit, and that's fine.

If AI ads work for you, I'd suggest {package}: {N} ads with {N} hook variants each, delivered
in {N} days, for {price}. You approve every script before I produce anything.

Concept examples (fictional brands) are in my portfolio. Happy to make one short sample from your
product page if that helps you decide.

{Your first name}
```

> Apply only to posts where AI is acceptable or not ruled out. If a post says "no AI" or "real creators
> only", skip it. Don't try to slip AI work past a client who asked for humans.
