# Portfolio: 5 sample ad scripts (fictional brands)

Make these five first: they're your portfolio before you have clients. Every brand and product below is
**made up**. Before you produce one, search the name quickly to make sure it isn't a real brand you'd be
confused with; rename it if so. Put the finished videos in one unlisted showcase (Google Drive folder, Vimeo
showcase or a simple page) and paste that link into `STUDIO.portfolio` in `index.html`.

**House rules for every script (matches `docs/COMPLIANCE.md`):**
- Spokesperson or demo only. The presenter **presents** the product; they never say "I bought / I use /
  it changed my life / my skin cleared up". No invented customers, reviews, star ratings or results.
- On-screen label for the whole ad, top-left, readable: **"AI-generated presenter · Ad"** (or
  "AI-generated voice · Ad" for voiceover-only demos).
- Portfolio pieces also carry a bottom label: **"Concept ad for a fictional brand"**.
- Claims are limited to product features you'd verify with a real client (dimensions, materials, what it does).
- When posting: tick the platform's AI-content label (TikTok "AI-generated", YouTube "altered or synthetic
  content", Meta "AI info") and the paid-partnership/ad label where relevant.

Format for all: 9:16, 1080×1920, 15–30s, burned-in captions, licensed music bed at -20 dB under voice.

## 1. TrailSip collapsible bottle (outdoor gear, demo + spokesperson, 22s)

**Brief:** silicone bottle that collapses to 1/3 height when empty. 600 ml. Fictional price $24.
**Audience:** hikers, commuters, travellers.

| Time | Visual | Voice (AI presenter) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook A:** Presenter holds a full-size bottle, then squashes it flat in one push | "This is a full-size water bottle. Watch." | Full size → pocket size |
| 3–8s | Close-up: bottle collapses, slides into a jacket pocket | "When it's empty, it folds down to a third of its height." | Folds to 1/3 |
| 8–14s | Hand fills it at a tap; carabiner clip on a backpack | "When you need it, pop it open, 600 millilitres, and clip it anywhere." | 600 ml · clip-on |
| 14–19s | Presenter taps the leak-proof cap, turns bottle upside down | "The cap seals, so it's bag-safe. And it's dishwasher safe." | Leak-proof cap · dishwasher safe |
| 19–22s | Product on white, price card | "TrailSip. Link below." | TrailSip · $24 |

**Alt hooks:** B: "Your bag is full of air. Specifically, your water bottle." C: "Airport security tip nobody tells you: bring it empty, fold it flat."
**Label:** AI-generated presenter · Ad / Concept ad for a fictional brand

## Production checklist (per ad)

- [ ] Script approved (by you for portfolio; by the client for real work)
- [ ] No first-person usage, results or review claims from the presenter
- [ ] AI label visible for the full duration; "concept / fictional brand" label on portfolio pieces
- [ ] Presenter is generated (or a licensed stock avatar), not a real person's likeness
- [ ] Voice is AI/your own/licensed. Never a cloned real person without a signed licence
- [ ] Music and stock footage licensed for ads; note the source in the project folder
- [ ] Captions checked for typos; prices and specs match the brief exactly
- [ ] Exported 1080×1920 MP4; filenames like `trailsip_hookA_v1.mp4`
