# Portfolio part 2: 10 more sample ad scripts (fictional brands)

These continue `launch-pack/ai-ad-studio/SAMPLE_ADS.md` (scripts 1 to 5). They cover the niches in
`leads.csv`, so whatever niche a lead is in, you can show them a portfolio piece that looks like their
product. Each one also uses a different ad format, so the portfolio shows range, not ten copies of one idea.

Every brand and product here is **made up**. Search each name before producing it; rename it if a real
brand already uses it.

**Same house rules as part 1:** spokesperson or demo only, no "I use it / I love it / it changed my life",
no invented reviews, ratings or results, no health or skin-result claims. Label top-left for the whole ad:
**"AI-generated presenter · Ad"** (or **"AI-generated voice · Ad"**), plus bottom label
**"Concept ad for a fictional brand"** on portfolio pieces. 9:16, 1080×1920, burned-in captions, music at -20 dB.

## 6. Hollowick wooden-wick candle (candles, sensory close-up, 20s)

**Brief:** soy wax candle with a wooden wick that crackles, 8 oz amber jar, scent "Cedar & Smoke". Fictional price $28.
**Audience:** people buying for cosy evenings and gifts.

| Time | Visual | Voice (AI voice) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook A:** Extreme close-up, match strikes, wooden wick catches | "Turn your sound on for this one." | 🔊 sound on |
| 3–8s | Flame flickers wide and low, faint crackle audible | "That's a wooden wick. It crackles a little while it burns." | Wooden wick · soft crackle |
| 8–13s | Slow pan across the amber jar and label | "Hand-poured soy wax in an eight-ounce amber jar." | Soy wax · 8 oz |
| 13–17s | Candle on a side table, book and blanket, lamp off | "The scent is Cedar and Smoke." | Cedar & Smoke |
| 17–20s | Product on linen, price card | "Hollowick. Link below." | Hollowick · $28 |

**Alt hooks:** B: "A candle you can hear." C: "If your candles tunnel, look at the wick."
**Label:** AI-generated voice · Ad / Concept ad for a fictional brand
**Note:** only state burn time or "clean burning" if the client gives you their own test figures.

## Turning a portfolio script into a free sample for a real lead

1. Pick the script above whose format fits the lead's product (see the table at the top).
2. Swap in only facts from their product page: materials, size, price, what it does. If the page doesn't
   say it, the ad doesn't say it.
3. Use their hook ideas from `pitches/` (each lead's pitch file has three), and make the strongest one Hook A.
4. Replace the bottom label with **"SAMPLE"** in a corner and keep the AI label.
5. Run the production checklist from part 1 before you send it.
