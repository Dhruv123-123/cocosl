## Free sample ad (lead offer)

- One ad, one hook, up to 20 seconds, for one product, built from the prospect's public product page and photos.
- Delivered with a visible "SAMPLE" watermark plus the AI label. Organic use allowed; not for paid ads.
- Credited ($62) against the first package if they order within 30 days.
- Cap: 3 free samples per week, so they don't eat your week. Only offer to leads that pass `LEAD_CRITERIA.md`.

## Payment terms

- 50% upfront to book; 50% on delivery before final un-watermarked files are released.
- Retainer: billed monthly in advance.
- Payment by card or bank transfer through Stripe invoice or PayPal invoice.
- Late payments: usage rights start when the invoice is paid in full.

## What's not included (quote separately or decline)

- Real people's faces or voices (only with a written licence the client supplies).
- First-person testimonials or "I used it and…" scripts with AI presenters. Not offered, at any price.
- Claims the client can't substantiate (health cures, guaranteed results, income claims).
- Media buying, ad account management, or performance guarantees.
