Sample ads for brands that don’t exist.

Every brand, product and price on this page is fictional. These are concept ads made to show the format: vertical 9:16, a hook in the first three seconds, burned-in captions, one clear call to action, and hook variants (A/B/C) for testing.

  • AI-made and labeled. Each video carries an on-screen “AI-generated” label for its full length, plus “Concept ad for a fictional brand · Sample”.
  • No fake reviews. Scripts are spokesperson and demo style. Nobody claims to have bought or used anything.
  • These are caption-led cuts with generated abstract visuals. Ads for a real client use your own product photos or footage, an AI voice or presenter, and licensed music.
