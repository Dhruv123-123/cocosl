# Service agreement template: AI video ads

> **This is a template, not legal advice.** It's written in plain English for small, low-value projects.
> Laws differ by country and state. For larger retainers, or if a client sends you their own contract,
> have a local lawyer review it. Fill in every `[bracket]`, delete options that don't apply, and send
> as a PDF (or via a free e-signature tool) with the first invoice.

---

**SERVICE AGREEMENT**

This agreement is between:

- **Studio:** [Your full name], trading as [Studio Name], [address], [email] ("Studio"), and
- **Client:** [Client legal business name], [address], represented by [name, title], [email] ("Client").

Effective date: [date]

### 1. Services and scope

1.1 Studio will produce the following short-form video advertisements ("Ads"):

| Package | [Starter / Growth / Monthly retainer] |
|---|---|
| Number of ads | [4 / 8 / 12 per month] |
| Hook variants per ad | [1 / 3] |
| Length and format | 15–30 seconds, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 MP4, burned-in captions |
| Products featured | [product name(s) and URLs] |
| Revision rounds | [1 / 2] (per batch for retainers) |
| Delivery | [7 / 10] days after Client approves scripts and supplies materials; retainer batches on [dates] |
| Add-ons | [e.g. paid-ads usage +90 days, rush 48h, translated subtitles, raw files, or "none"] |
| Total fee | $[amount] [per month for retainers] |

1.2 **Client supplies** product facts, any claims to be made and evidence supporting them, logos, brand
guidelines, and product photos/footage, by [date]. Delivery dates move by the same number of days that
materials or approvals are late.

1.3 **Script approval.** Studio sends scripts and hooks for written approval (email is fine) before
production. Client is responsible for confirming that every factual claim in an approved script is true
and can be substantiated.

1.4 **Out of scope:** media buying, ad account management, posting, new concepts after script approval,
and performance results. Studio does not guarantee sales, views, ROAS or any business outcome.

### 2. Revisions

2.1 A revision round is one consolidated list of changes, sent within [7] days of delivery, covering edits
to script wording, captions, pacing, music, on-screen text and visuals within the approved script.

2.2 A new concept, new product, or rewritten script after approval counts as a new Ad, billed at the
package's per-ad rate ($[62]) or quoted separately.

2.3 If Client doesn't request revisions within [7] days of delivery, the Ads are accepted.

### 3. AI disclosure and content standards

3.1 Client understands and agrees that **Ads use AI-generated presenters and/or AI-generated or synthetic
voices**, which are not real people.

3.2 Every Ad includes an on-screen label stating that the presenter and/or voice is AI-generated. Client
agrees **not to remove, cover or crop out** that label, and to use each platform's AI-content disclosure
setting where available when publishing or running the Ads.

3.3 Ads are spokesperson or product-demonstration ads. **Studio will not produce**, and Client will not
present the Ads as: testimonials or reviews from customers or users; statements that the presenter has
bought, used or obtained results from the product; or endorsements by any real person.

3.4 Studio will not use any real person's likeness or voice unless Client supplies a written licence from
that person covering advertising use. Client warrants any such licence is valid.

3.5 Studio may decline or stop work on content it reasonably believes is misleading, unlawful, infringes
third-party rights, or breaks advertising platform policies (including health, financial or income claims
that can't be substantiated). Fees for completed work remain payable.

### 4. Fees and payment

4.1 **50% of the total fee is due upfront** to book production; the remaining **50% is due on delivery**,
before final un-watermarked files are released. Retainers are billed monthly in advance on the [1st].

4.2 Invoices are payable within [7] days by [card / bank transfer / PayPal] via the invoice link.

4.3 Upfront payments are non-refundable once script work has begun, except where Studio fails to deliver.
If Studio cancels, unused fees are refunded in full.

4.4 Retainers continue month to month; either party may end them with [30] days' written notice.

### 5. Ownership and intellectual property

5.1 On **payment in full**, Studio assigns to Client ownership of the final edited Ads as delivered, subject
to section 6 (usage) and section 5.3.

5.2 Client keeps all rights in its own materials (logos, product photos, footage, trademarks) and grants
Studio a licence to use them only to produce the Ads.

5.3 Some components are licensed, not owned: stock music, stock footage, fonts, and outputs of AI tools
are provided under their providers' licences. Studio will use only components licensed for commercial
advertising use and keep a record of sources. Copyright protection for purely AI-generated material may be
limited in some countries; Client's ownership covers what Studio can lawfully assign.

5.4 Studio keeps ownership of its scripts templates, project files, prompts and working methods. Raw project
files are delivered only if purchased as an add-on.

5.5 **Portfolio:** Studio may show the Ads in its portfolio [unless Client opts out here: ☐ Client opts out].
Studio will not publish Client's confidential information or performance data.

### 6. Usage rights

6.1 **Organic use:** Client may post the Ads on its own website, email and organic social media accounts
with no time limit.

6.2 **Paid-ads use** (running the Ads as paid advertisements on any platform, including boosting, Spark Ads,
or partnership/whitelisted ads): permitted for **[30 / 90 / 180] days** from delivery, extendable with the
paid-usage add-on ([+$100 per extra 90 days / 12 months $400 / perpetual $750]).

6.3 Usage rights start when payment is received in full.

### 7. Client responsibilities and warranties

Client warrants that (a) product information and claims supplied are accurate and substantiated; (b) Client
owns or has permission to use all materials it supplies; and (c) Client will run the Ads in line with
applicable law and platform policies, including advertising disclosure rules.

### 8. Liability

8.1 Each party is responsible for claims arising from its own breach of this agreement. Client is
responsible for claims arising from product claims, materials or approvals Client supplied.

8.2 To the extent the law allows, Studio's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees
Client paid under it. Neither party is liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or ad spend.

### 9. General

9.1 Studio is an independent contractor, not an employee or agent of Client.

9.2 Either party may end this agreement by written notice if the other materially breaches it and doesn't
fix the breach within [10] days. Client pays for work completed up to the end date.

9.3 Confidential information each party shares stays confidential, except as needed to do the work.

9.4 This agreement is governed by the laws of [state/country]. It is the entire agreement between the
parties and can be changed only in writing (email counts).
