FAQ

Questions

Does it work on Etsy?

Yes. The listing video is 10–15 seconds and silent, because Etsy listing videos must be 3–15 seconds and play without sound. You upload it in the listing editor under Photos and video.

Will AI change how my product looks?

No. The product is always your real product from your own photos. AI only helps with motion, backgrounds, transitions and captions, never with the product itself. Buyers should get exactly what they saw.

Is it labeled as AI?

If the social video uses an AI voice, it carries an on-screen "AI-generated" label. The silent listing video is made only from your photos, and your delivery note says so, so you can answer buyers honestly.

Do you write reviews or "customer" lines?

No fake reviews, customer quotes, ratings or sales numbers. If you have real reviews you're allowed to share, I can show one as on-screen text.

Can I run these as paid ads?

These packages cover your listings, website and social accounts with no time limit. For paid ads, see the studio packages. Your $49 or $99 is credited in full if you upgrade within 30 days.

What if I'm not happy?

You approve the plan before I start, and one revision is included. If I can't make a video that works from your photos, I'll tell you before starting and refund you.
