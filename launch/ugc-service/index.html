How we use AI (read this)

Honest ads are the only kind worth running.

  • Presenters and voices are AI-generated. They are not real people and are not your customers.
  • Every ad is labeled with an on-screen "AI-generated" notice. We also recommend using each platform's own AI-content label when you post.
  • No fake testimonials or reviews. Presenters never say they bought, used or were cured by your product. They present it, like a spokesperson.
  • No real people's likeness or voices unless you supply a written licence from that person.
  • Claims must be true. We only script claims you can back up. We won't write health cures, guaranteed results or income claims.
  • Your product is real. Demos use your own footage or photos, so viewers see what they'll actually get.
FAQ

Questions

Are the people in the ads real?

No. Presenters and voices are generated with AI tools, and every ad shows an on-screen "AI-generated" label. We don't present them as real customers, and they never claim to have used your product.

Why not "UGC" from real creators?

Real creator content is great, and if you can afford lots of it, do it. AI ads are a faster, cheaper way to test many angles and hooks. Many brands use AI ads to find winning messages, then brief human creators on what works.

Will platforms allow AI ads?

Yes, when they're disclosed and follow normal ad policies. TikTok, Meta and YouTube all have rules for AI-generated or altered content; labeling the ad and using the platform's AI label when you upload keeps you on the right side of them. You're responsible for your ad account and final targeting.

What do you need from me?

Your product page, a few photos or short phone clips of the product (unboxing, in use, close-ups), your offer, and the claims you can substantiate. If you have no footage, we can work from product photos.

Can I run these as paid ads?

Yes. Organic use on your own channels is included with no time limit. Paid-ads use is included for 30 days (Starter), 90 days (Growth) or 180 days per batch (Retainer), and you can extend it 90 days at a time for $100.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Results depend on your product, offer, targeting and budget. What we promise is on-time, on-brief, well-made creative that's built for testing.

What's the free sample?

One short ad (one hook, up to 20 seconds) for one of your products, made from your public product page. It's yours to keep for organic use. If you like it, it's credited against your first package. No obligation.

How do payment and revisions work?

50% upfront to book your slot, 50% on delivery via invoice (card or bank transfer). Revisions cover edits to script, captions, pacing and visuals within the approved brief; a brand-new concept counts as a new ad.

Who owns the ads?

Once paid in full, you own the final edited videos, subject to the usage terms of your package and the licences of the tools and music used. Details are in our simple service agreement, which you'll see before paying.
