# 1099 Money: affiliate placeholders and disclosures

Every affiliate URL in `brands/1099-money.yaml` is a placeholder (`https://example.com/go/<slug>`).
Replace each one with your real tracking link when the program approves you, then run
`factory links sync` so clicks are tracked. Terms below are the published terms as of Nov 2026
(`research/02-affiliate-programs.md`); confirm them in each program's dashboard.

| Slug | Program | Network | Commission | Used in |

| freshbooks | FreshBooks (invoicing + accounting) | Impact | $88 per sale, $8 per trial | "Three lines that get your invoices paid", "The invoice checklist" |
| quickbooks | QuickBooks Self-Employed / Solopreneur | Impact | 10% per sale | "Your car is a deduction if you log it" (mileage tracking) |
| novo | Novo (business checking) | PartnerStack | not published | "Stop paying clients into your personal account", long video 1 |
| lively | Lively (HSA) | Impact | $10 per sale | "Your bronze plan might unlock an HSA" |
| gusto | Gusto (payroll, once you hire) | Gusto network | $50–$100 per lead | not used yet (for a later "first hire" piece) |
| taxact | TaxAct (self-employed filing) | CJ | 15% per sale | not used yet (save for Jan–Apr filing season) |

**Order to apply:** Impact (FreshBooks, QuickBooks, Lively) and CJ (TaxAct) first, since one approval
covers several programs, then PartnerStack (Novo). Most programs want a live account with ~10 posts.

## Disclosure wording (already in the captions)

- Caption: "The [product] link in our bio is an affiliate link: we may earn a commission if you
  [sign up / open an account], at no extra cost to you. Compare options yourself first."
- YouTube description: "Some links in the description are affiliate links: we may earn a commission
  if you sign up, at no extra cost to you."
- Link-in-bio page: an affiliate note is printed above the links automatically.
- Spoken/visual: when a video names the product, also use the platform's paid-partnership or
  "includes paid promotion" toggle if the company paid for the placement (not needed for plain
  affiliate links, but the caption disclosure is always needed).

## Rules

- Never post an example.com link. Until approved, hold the affiliate pieces or post them with the
  affiliate paragraph removed and no link.
- Only recommend a product category the video actually explains (invoicing, business checking, HSA).
  No "best X" rankings we haven't tested.
- Tax software and HSA links: keep "not tax advice" in the caption. Banking: name FDIC insurance and,
  for fintech apps, the partner bank.
- No income claims ("save $X on taxes with...").
