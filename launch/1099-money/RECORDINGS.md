# Recordings: the 14 long videos waiting for your footage

Each of these long videos has an `original_element` that only you can make: a screen recording or a short on-camera
segment. Until the clip is in, the pack's original-element check blocks the video. Every other long video already
passes with its chart.

**One session, about 90 minutes:** do all 9 screen recordings first (same browser setup), then the 5 on-camera clips
(same camera and light). Each clip only needs to cover one chapter (15–45 seconds); record about 10 seconds extra.

## Setup

- **Screen:** 1920×1080, browser zoom 125%, a private window, logged out of everything, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off.
  Never type real personal data; nothing gets submitted.
- **Camera:** landscape 1920×1080, eye level, window light from the side, a quiet room. Keep it plain and talk the way you would to a friend.
- **Honesty:** say only what's true about your own routine. Where the shot list says "say truthfully", either describe what you actually do or present it as a suggestion.
- **Files:** save each clip as `footage/1099-money/<file name below>.mp4` inside your factory home folder.
- **Wire it in:** in the video's `content.json`, find the segment with the chapter heading below and set
  `"footage": "footage/1099-money/<file name>.mp4"`. Then re-render that month's pack. The block clears.
