The Tax Jar Tracker

The rule: from every client payment, move your set-aside percentage into a separate tax account the same day. Colour in a band each time the jar reaches another 10% of your goal.

One step a week

  • Day 1 · Nov 16 Start: 25% of every payment, the same day it arrives
  • Week 1 · Nov 23 Open a separate savings account for tax. Nickname it
  • Week 2 · Nov 30 Find your own %: federal from the table + your state's rate
  • Week 3 · Dec 7 Backfill: your % of payments since Sep 1
  • Week 4 · Dec 14 Decide: % of the whole payment (simple) or of profit
  • Week 5 · Dec 21 Automate: invoice reminder, Friday transfer or bank rule
  • Week 6 · Dec 28 Check the jar against your Jan 15 payment
  • Week 7 · Jan 4 Set up IRS Direct Pay: 1040-ES, tax year 2026
  • Week 8 · Jan 11 Leftover beyond the payment stays for April 15
  • Jan 15 Pay from the jar. Save the confirmation number
