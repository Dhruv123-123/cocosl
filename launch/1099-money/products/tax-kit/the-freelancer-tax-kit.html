Your first 30 minutes

  1. Tracker, Setup tab (5 min). Filing status, state set-aside, and last year's total tax from your return.
  2. Pick your set-aside percentage (5 min). The tracker's set-aside check gives you a starting point. Write it at the top of the Tax Jar Tracker.
  3. Log this year so far (15 min). Income and expenses by month is enough to start; add detail later.
  4. Put the next deadline in your calendar (5 min). Use the Tax Calendar, and schedule the payment in IRS Direct Pay if you know the amount.

The three printables are also free. They're the same files 1099 Money gives away to email subscribers. This kit puts them in one download with the tracker so everything lives in one folder.

The yearly rhythm

How it runs all year

Every client payment

Move your set-aside percentage into a separate tax savings account the day the money lands, and log it on the Tax Jar Tracker.

Once a month (15 minutes)

  • Log income, expenses and business miles in the tracker.
  • Check the set-aside line: is your tax savings keeping up with the estimate?
  • Tick the monthly items on the checklist.

The checklist's filing section lists what to gather. The tracker's totals give you your income, expenses by category and miles for Schedule C.
