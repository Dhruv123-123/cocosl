## Full description

> Freelancers don't have tax withheld, so the IRS expects you to pay four times a year. Most people either guess, overpay, or find out in April with a bill and a penalty.
>
> **The Quarterly Tax Tracker** turns it into a 15-minute routine. Log your income, expenses and business miles as you go. Before each deadline, open the Quarterly tab: it estimates your full-year federal tax (self-employment tax plus income tax, with 2026 brackets, the standard deduction and the QBI deduction) and shows a **suggested payment**, the lesser of the two IRS penalty-avoidance methods, minus what you've already paid.
>
> **What's inside**
>
> - **The spreadsheet (.xlsx):** Excel, Google Sheets and Apple Numbers. Yellow cells are inputs; everything else calculates. Pre-filled with a fictional freelancer so you can see it working.
>   - **Quarterly:** profit so far, full-year tax at your pace, current-year vs. prior-year safe harbor, suggested payment, and a set-aside check that tells you if your tax savings are enough
>   - **Income / Expenses / Mileage logs:** with business-use % for mixed items and the 2026 mileage rate
>   - **Deadlines:** every date for the year, ready for your calendar
>   - **Tax Tables:** all 2026 federal figures in one place, easy to update
> - **The guide (PDF, 10 pages):** why quarterly, a $60,000 worked example, how much to set aside, the two calculation methods and the safe harbor, the pre-deadline routine, records to keep, FAQ.
>
> **Who it's for:** US freelancers, creators, gig workers and side hustlers filing Schedule C, especially in your first or second year.
>
> **Who it's not for:** S corps, people who itemise or have large credits or capital gains, and incomes above about $200,000. Use Form 1040-ES or a tax professional for those.
>
> Instant download. Educational tool, not tax advice.

## FAQ

**Does it do state taxes?** Only a rough flat-rate estimate you choose. Use your state's estimated tax worksheet for the real number.
**I also have a W-2 job.** Enter expected wages and withholding on Setup; the calculation accounts for both.
**Will it match my return exactly?** No. It's a planning estimate built to keep you clear of the penalty.
**Is this tax advice?** No. It's an educational tool. For your situation, talk to a qualified tax professional.
