## Description

Find the hourly rate that actually covers your taxes, costs and unbilled time, then get it in writing.

Most freelancers set a rate by guessing what clients will pay. This kit starts from the take-home pay you want and works backward through 2026 federal tax, self-employment tax, your business costs and your real billable hours. Then it gives you the paperwork to quote, sign and invoice at that rate.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Rate & Invoice spreadsheet (.xlsx: Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers)
  – Rate Calculator: your hourly floor, with 2026 federal tax built in
  – Project Pricer: hours, revision buffer, deposit and balance
  – Invoice template + invoice log that flags overdue clients
• 4 editable Word / Google Docs templates (in one zip)
  – Proposal that ends in one clear next step
  – Freelance services agreement, 13 plain-English clauses
  – Change order for out-of-scope requests
  – Invoice
• 7-page PDF guide with a worked example

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download from your Etsy Purchases page and unzip the templates
2. Rate Calculator: enter take-home goal, costs and billable hours
3. Price the project, send the proposal
4. Sign the agreement, invoice the deposit, invoice the balance on delivery

GOOD TO KNOW
• Digital download. Nothing is shipped.
• The agreement is a starting template, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it for big contracts or your state's rules.
• Tax figures are federal, 2026 tax year, plus a flat state rate you enter.
