## Description

Everything a freelancer needs to set aside the right amount, pay the IRS on time each quarter, and be ready at filing time. In one download.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The Quarterly Tax Tracker: spreadsheet + 10-page guide. Log income, expenses and miles; it shows a suggested payment before each IRS deadline, with the safe harbor built in.
• The 2027 Freelancer Tax Calendar (printable PDF): every federal deadline, month by month.
• The Freelancer Tax Checklist (printable PDF): first weeks, every month, each quarter and filing time.
• The Tax Jar Tracker (printable PDF): a one-page log for setting aside a share of every client payment.
• 3-page Start Here guide: set it all up in about 30 minutes.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download both files from your Etsy Purchases page and unzip the kit
2. Open 1-Start-Here.pdf
3. Fill in the tracker's Setup tab (about 5 minutes)
4. Print the calendar, checklist and jar tracker, or use them on screen
