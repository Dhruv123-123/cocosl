# Compliance note: Scam Watch for libraries and senior centers

**This is not legal advice.** It lists what a library director, senior center manager or Area Agency
on Aging is likely to ask, and what we already do about it.

## 1. Our content rules

| Rule | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Sources cited** | Each issue lists its sources: FTC, US Department of Justice, Social Security, Medicare. |
| **Real numbers only** | Help numbers are the official ones (Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-372-8311, Social Security 1-800-772-1213, Medicare 1-800-633-4227, FTC 1-877-382-4357). We re-check them every quarter. |
| **Made-up examples, clearly labelled** | Example calls and messages are marked as made up and use no real company's number or logo. |
| **No selling to patrons** | No products, ads, affiliate links or links to our sites. Public institutions shouldn't appear to endorse a business, and this never asks them to. |
| **No patron data** | We never receive names, emails or sign-in sheets. |
| **Kind, not shaming** | Every issue says it's not the victim's fault and encourages telling someone. |

## 2. Issues that matter

### Large print and accessibility
- Handouts use 18 pt body text, high contrast and short lines, in line with common large-print
  guidance (16 to 18 pt minimum). The screen slide and flyer use very large type. The newsletter blurb
  has a plain-text version and every image has alt text.

### Staff and volunteers aren't investigators
- The talk guide is for awareness only. If a patron says they've lost money, staff point them to their
  bank, the Elder Fraud Hotline and ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Staff shouldn't collect account details or
  give legal advice. Any reporting duties under state law are the organization's own.

### Public-sector buying
- Small libraries and county agencies often need a W-9, an invoice and a purchase order, and can't
  auto-renew. Offer annual invoicing and say plainly that nothing renews without their OK.

### Copying and licence
- The organization may print unlimited copies for its patrons, members or residents at the locations
  in its plan, show the slides, and post the image on its own social pages while subscribed. Annual
  plans also include the Scam Decoded for Seniors group print license.

## 3. Fact-check list (before every issue ships)

1. Every official phone number checked against the agency's own page that quarter.
2. Body text at least 18 pt in the handout; nothing important only in color.
3. No instruction to tap or scan a link.
4. Example messages labelled made up, with no real company names or numbers.
5. No unfilled `{{TOKENS}}`.
6. Read it aloud to someone. Shorten any sentence over about 20 words.
