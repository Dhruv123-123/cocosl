# Cold-email pack: Scam Watch for libraries and senior centers

**You send every one of these yourself, by hand, from your own inbox. Nothing has been sent.**

Same sending rules as `../credit-union-alert/COLD_EMAILS.md`. Senior centers often have only a phone
number (the Idaho list does); call script below. Libraries usually list an adult-services or
programming contact on their own site. Never guess an email address.

Tokens: `{First name}`, `{Organization}`, `{City}`, `{Personal line}`, `{Your name}`, `{Your mailing address}`.

## Email 1: library (day 0)

**Subject options:** `A monthly large-print scam alert for {Organization} patrons` · `Scam-awareness handouts for your older patrons`

> Hi {First name},
>
> {Personal line}
>
> I make Scam Watch, a monthly scam alert written for older adults: a large-print handout, a bulletin
> board flyer, a screen slide and a 10-minute talk guide your staff or volunteers can run. Each month
> has your library's name and help line on it, and you can copy it freely for patrons.
>
> This month's sample covers the "grandchild in trouble" call, which now uses AI-copied voices.
>
> Can I send you the free 4-month sample to look at?
>
> Thanks,
> {Your name}
> Scam Decoded · {Your mailing address}
> If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks".

## Phone script: senior center (when only a phone number is listed)

> "Hi, my name is {Your name}. I make a free-to-copy, large-print scam alert for seniors, one scam a
> month with a short talk guide for your group. Who would be the right person to email a free sample
> to?"

Write down the name and email they give you, then send Email 1 that day. If they say no, thank them
and mark it.

## Follow-up 1 (day 4)

## Replies

- **"Send the sample."** Attach `out/sample-county-library.zip` and `out/sales-sheet.pdf`. Offer a
  branded version: ask for their logo, color and the help line to print.
- **"What does it cost?"** $19 a month for one location, $59 for a system (up to 10 branches or a whole
  AAA region), or yearly with 2 months free. Talks are $150 each.
- **"We need a purchase order / W-9."** "No problem. I can invoice yearly, and nothing renews without
  your OK."
- **"We get free materials from AARP / the FTC already."** "Those are great; we point to them too.
  This is a fresh topic every month, ready to print with your name on it, plus a talk guide, so staff
  don't have to put it together."
- **"Can you come and give a talk?"** Quote $150 (speaking kit), or include one free talk with an
  annual System plan if you want to close the sale. **Your call.**
- **"No thanks."** "Understood, I won't write again." Mark it and stop.
