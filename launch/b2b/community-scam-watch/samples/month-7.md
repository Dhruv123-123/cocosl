**This month's scam**

### The text about a package you're not expecting

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "USPS: Your package is on hold due to an incomplete address. Pay a $1.99 redelivery fee within 24 hours: [link]"

The text says a package couldn't be delivered and asks you to tap a link to reschedule or pay a small fee. The link goes to a fake website that asks for your card number.

The text may say it's from the Postal Service, UPS, FedEx or Amazon. These texts go out by the millions, so many people really are waiting for a package.

**The warning sign**

**Delivery companies don't text you a link asking for payment to deliver a package. Don't tap it.**

**What to do**

1. **Don't tap the link** or reply. Delete it.
2. **Expecting something?** Check the tracking number from your order email, or on the shipper's own website.
3. **Entered your card?** Call your bank right away to block it.

**Also this month**

- **Forward scam texts to 7726** (SPAM). It helps your phone company block them.
- **The Postal Service doesn't text you** unless you signed up for tracking texts with a tracking number.

**One thing to do this month**

Practise forwarding a text: ask a family member or library staff to show you how to forward a message to 7726.

> Questions? {{HELP_LINE}} If you're 60 or older and lost money to a scam, call the free National Elder Fraud Hotline: **1-833-372-8311**.

## Group leader's 10-minute talk

- **Open with:** "Has anyone had a text about a package they didn't order?"
- **Show:** Show the flyer's example text. Point out the fee and the link.
- **Practise:** Show how to forward a text to 7726, on your own phone or a volunteer's.
- **Close with:** "If a text has a link and asks for money, don't tap. Ask someone first."
