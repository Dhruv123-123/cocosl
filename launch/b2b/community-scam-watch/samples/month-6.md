**This month's scam**

### The call that says your power goes off today

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "This is the power company. Your account is past due and a crew is on the way to disconnect you in 60 minutes. Pay $386 now by gift card to stop it."

In the heat of summer, scammers call pretending to be the electric, gas or water company. They say your bill is late and your service will be cut off within the hour unless you pay right now.

They ask for payment by gift card, a payment app, cryptocurrency or a cash machine. Some even send a fake "technician" to your door.

**The warning sign**

**A real utility company sends written notices before it shuts off service, and doesn't demand gift cards or crypto.**

**What to do**

1. **Hang up.** Then call the number on your own bill to check.
2. **Never pay with a gift card, payment app or crypto** because a caller says so.
3. **Someone at the door?** Ask for ID, and call the utility on the number on your bill before letting them in.

**Also this month**

- **Behind on a bill for real?** Ask your utility about payment plans and help programs.
- **Caller ID can lie.** It can show your utility's real name.

**One thing to do this month**

Find your latest utility bill and circle the phone number. That's the one to call if anyone threatens a shutoff.

## Group leader's 10-minute talk

- **Open with:** "Has anyone had a call saying a bill was late and they had to pay right away?"
- **Show:** Hold up a utility bill and show where the real phone number is.
- **Practise:** Have everyone find the phone number on a bill they carry or have at home.
- **Close with:** "A real company gives you time. A scammer gives you an hour."
