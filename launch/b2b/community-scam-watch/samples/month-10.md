**This month's scam**

### The Medicare call you didn't ask for

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Hello, this is your Medicare benefits office. You qualify for extra benefits this year. I just need to confirm your Medicare number to send your new card."

Medicare open enrollment runs from **October 15 to December 7**. It's when you can change your Medicare health and drug plans. It's also when callers say you need a new card, a better plan, or extra benefits, if you just confirm your Medicare number.

Medicare won't call you to sell a plan or ask for your number.

**The warning sign**

**Choose a plan on your own terms. Don't sign up or give your Medicare number on a call you didn't make.**

**What to do**

1. **Hang up** on calls you didn't expect, even if they say "Medicare".
2. **Get free, unbiased help** from your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) at shiphelp.org, or call 1-800-MEDICARE.
3. **Compare plans yourself** at Medicare.gov with the Plan Finder.

**Also this month**

- **Your Medicare card doesn't expire**, and there's no fee for a new one.
- **Ask about help with costs:** SHIP counselors can check if you qualify for programs that lower drug costs.

**One thing to do this month**

Write down the dates, Oct 15 to Dec 7, and your local SHIP phone number from shiphelp.org.
