**This month's scam**

### The free offer that wants your Medicare number

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Hi, I'm calling about a free cancer gene test that Medicare covers. We just need your Medicare number to mail the kit today."

A caller, ad or person at a health fair offers something free: a genetic test, a back brace, a cancer screening. All they need is your Medicare number.

Then Medicare is billed for things you never needed, and your number can be used again and again. Some people get boxes of equipment they never asked for.

**The warning sign**

**Only give your Medicare number to your own doctor, pharmacy or plan. Free offers that ask for it are scams.**

**What to do**

1. **Don't give your Medicare number** to callers, door-to-door sellers or ads.
2. **Read your Medicare statements.** Look for tests or equipment you didn't get.
3. **Report it** to 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227), or to your state's Senior Medicare Patrol.

**Also this month**

- **Got a box you didn't order?** Don't sign for it, and tell Medicare.
- **Medicare doesn't call you** to sell anything or ask for your number.

**One thing to do this month**

Find your last Medicare statement, or look it up at Medicare.gov, and check it for anything you don't recognize.
