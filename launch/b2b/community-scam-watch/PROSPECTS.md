**Why these:** an Area Agency on Aging buys once for a whole region (System tier) and has senior

need handouts. Montana Regions III and X weren't shown on the state page; look them up if you want them.

**Before you write:** fill `website` and `contact_route` from each organization's own site. Senior
centers often list only a phone: use the phone script in `COLD_EMAILS.md`. For libraries, look for
"adult services", "outreach" or "programming" staff.

Credit unions and banks in the same towns often sponsor senior programs; the credit union product
(`../credit-union-alert/`) pairs well if a credit union wants to sponsor Scam Watch for its local
library.
