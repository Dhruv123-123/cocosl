# B2B outreach dates (inside the ad studio's daily cap)

Made by `outreach_dates.py` from `OUTREACH_CALENDAR.md`. **Nothing has been sent.** Dhruv sends every email by hand.

The ad studio calendar already uses most of the 10-a-day cap (5 new first emails a day). These dates use only the room left over, so the combined total never goes over the cap. Follow-ups land about 4 and 10 days after Email 1, on the next day with room.

**Why so late:** from 10 Nov to 12 Apr every ad studio day that has spare sends already uses all 5 new-first-email slots, so the spare room can only take follow-ups, and a B2B prospect can't get a follow-up before an Email 1. Accounting firms therefore miss the pre-January window and start after tax season.

**181 prospects, 543 emails.** 4 prospects finish their 3 emails while the ad studio calendar runs (to Mon 26 Apr 2027); the other 177 start after it ends, when the full cap is free. Last email: Mon 26 Jul 2027.

**Want them sooner?** The only way without breaking the cap is a second sending inbox for B2B (about $6 to $7 a month for a Google Workspace or similar seat, plus warm-up time). That's a spending decision for Dhruv, so it isn't assumed here. Accounting firms are skipped from mid-January to Apr 19 (tax season).

Templates: each product's `COLD_EMAILS.md` (freelancer emails are in its `README.md`). Stop a prospect's later rows the moment they reply or say no.
