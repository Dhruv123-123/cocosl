# Cold-email pack: Money Made Simple at Work

**You send every one of these yourself, by hand, from your own inbox. Nothing has been sent.**

Same sending rules as `../credit-union-alert/COLD_EMAILS.md`: 5 to 8 new prospects a day, plain text,
no attachment or link in the first email, your name, mailing address and an opt-out line in every
email, and stop at the first "no". Never guess an email address or scrape a directory. Use the
fictional Sample Outfitters pack for first contact, never a real employer's logo.

Tokens: `{First name}`, `{Company}`, `{City}`, `{Personal line}` (one true sentence, such as their Top
Workplaces award), `{Your name}`, `{Your mailing address}`.

## Who to write to

1. **Employers:** HR manager, people/benefits manager, or the owner at companies under about 100
   people. Find the name on the company's own site or award write-up.
2. **SHRM chapters and HR associations:** the chapter president or programs chair listed on the
   chapter site. Ask for a 10-minute slot at a meeting, or a mention in their newsletter.
3. **Benefits brokers:** the employee benefits lead. Brokers want low-cost extras for their clients.

## Replies

Every reply below goes out with the same signature as email 1: your name, `{Your mailing address}` and the "no thanks" line.

- **"Send the sample."** Attach `out/demo-packet.zip`, `out/sales-sheet.pdf` and `out/COMPLIANCE_NOTE.pdf`.
  Offer a branded version: ask for their logo, colors, benefits contact, portal name and exact match wording.
- **"What does it cost?"** Starter $59 a month (up to 100 employees), Growth $129 (100 to 499; adds
  the breakroom flyer and manager talking points), Plus $249 (500+; adds Spanish and 2 custom issues a
  year). First 3 employers: 2 months free.
- **"Our 401(k) provider already sends stuff."** "Good. This is broader (tax season, credit, saving)
  and comes from you, not a vendor, which usually gets read more. Use both."
- **"Is this investment advice?"** "No. It's general education and never recommends funds or amounts.
  The compliance note explains how we keep it that way; your adviser or counsel has final say."
- **"We have an EAP with financial counseling."** "Great; every issue points people to it. Most
  employees forget it exists, and a monthly reminder helps."
- **Broker partner rate:** propose 20% off for broker-referred clients, or $39 a client a month for
  5+ clients billed to the broker. **These are proposals: decide before you quote them.**
- **"No thanks."** "Understood, I won't write again." Mark it and stop.
