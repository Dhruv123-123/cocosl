**This month's money topic**

### Your mid-year paycheck checkup

Halfway through the year is a good time to check that the right amount of tax is coming out of your pay. A big life change, like marriage, a new baby, a second job or side income, can throw it off.

The IRS has a free online Tax Withholding Estimator. With your latest pay stub, it tells you whether to change your W-4.

**The one thing to remember**

**Fix your withholding in August, not in April. A small change now can avoid a big bill later.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Grab your latest pay stub** and run the IRS [Tax Withholding Estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator).
2. **Need to change it?** Submit a new W-4 in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}} or ask {{BENEFITS_CONTACT}}.
3. **Check your credit reports too.** They're free every week at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com), the official site.

**Good to know**

- **Side income?** You may need to raise withholding or make estimated payments.
- **Wrong item on a credit report?** You can dispute it with the credit bureau for free.

**Your 10-minute task**

Run the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator with this month's pay stub. It takes about 15 minutes.
