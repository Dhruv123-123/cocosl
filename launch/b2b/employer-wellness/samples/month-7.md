**This month's money topic**

### Avalanche or snowball?

If you have more than one debt, you'll pay off all of them faster with a plan than by spreading extra money around. There are two common plans.

**The avalanche:** pay the minimum on everything, and put every extra dollar on the debt with the highest interest rate. It saves the most money. **The snowball:** put the extra on the smallest balance first. You get quick wins, which helps many people keep going.

**The one thing to remember**

**The best plan is the one you'll stick with. Always pay at least the minimum on every debt.**

**Do it this month**

1. **List your debts** with the balance, the interest rate and the minimum payment.
2. **Pick avalanche or snowball**, and choose one debt to put extra money on.
3. **When one is paid off,** move its payment to the next one. That's the part that speeds up.

**Good to know**

- **Be careful with offers to "settle" your debt for a fee up front.** That's often a scam. A nonprofit credit counselor is a safer place to start.
