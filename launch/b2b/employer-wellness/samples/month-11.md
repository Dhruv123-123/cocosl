**This month's money topic**

### Set your holiday number

The easiest way to avoid a January hangover is to decide on one number for the season before the sales begin: gifts, travel, food and all.

Buy now, pay later can feel like free money. It's still a loan. Several plans at once are easy to lose track of, and a missed payment can mean fees.

**The one thing to remember**

**Decide what the holidays cost before the stores decide for you.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Pick your holiday number** and write it down. Divide it between people and events.
2. **Track it** in a note on your phone as you go.
3. **Using buy now, pay later?** Keep a list of every plan and payment date, and make sure the payments fit next month's budget.

**Good to know**

- **Start a holiday fund for next year** in January: a small amount each payday makes next December easier.
- **Gift card or package text you weren't expecting?** It may be a scam. Don't click the link.

**Your 10-minute task**

Write your holiday number on a note in your phone today, before Black Friday.
