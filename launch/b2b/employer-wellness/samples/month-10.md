**This month's money topic**

### Open enrollment in 20 minutes

Open enrollment is the one time a year most people can change their health, dental and other benefits without a life event. It's easy to click "keep the same". Twenty minutes can be worth a lot more.

**HSA or FSA?** A Health Savings Account is only for people on a qualifying high-deductible plan; the money is yours and carries over year to year. A Flexible Spending Account is usually use-it-or-lose-it, though some plans allow a grace period or a small carryover. Your plan documents say which rules apply.

**The one thing to remember**

**Check what you used this year before you choose next year. Last year's choice may not fit anymore.**

> **From HR:** {{HR_NOTE}}

**Do it this month**

1. **Look back:** how many doctor visits, prescriptions or big bills did you have this year?
2. **Compare the total cost**, not just the paycheck deduction: premiums plus the deductible and what you'd pay out of pocket.
3. **Check the dates and details** in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}}, and ask {{BENEFITS_CONTACT}} anything that isn't clear.

**Good to know**

- **Using an FSA?** Plan for what you'll actually spend. Money left over may be lost.
- **Update your beneficiaries** for life insurance and your {{RETIREMENT_PLAN}} while you're logged in.

**Your 10-minute task**

Write down how much you spent on health care this year, from your statements or insurer's app. Use it to compare plans.
