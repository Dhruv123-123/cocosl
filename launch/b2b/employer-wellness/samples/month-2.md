**This month's money topic**

### Tax season, minus the surprises

Your W-2 should arrive by the end of January. Before you file, three quick checks can save you from a surprise bill next year, or a refund that's much smaller than you expected.

Tax rules changed for 2025 and 2026, so last year's habits may not fit this year. The IRS updated its free withholding estimator to include the changes.

**The one thing to remember**

**Your withholding is a choice you can change any time, and side income needs its own tax plan.**

> **From HR:** {{HR_NOTE}}

**Do it this month**

1. **Run the IRS [Tax Withholding Estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator)** once you've filed or have your W-2. If it says to change your W-4, update it in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}}.
2. **Earn money on the side** (rideshare, freelancing, reselling)? No tax is taken out of that money. A common rule of thumb is to set aside 25 to 30% of what you earn on the side, and the IRS may expect [estimated tax payments](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes) during the year.
3. **File for free if you can.** Check the IRS's free filing options at IRS.gov before you pay for software.

**Good to know**

- **The IRS doesn't text or email you out of the blue** about refunds or your account. Delete those messages and type IRS.gov yourself.
- **Payroll will never ask** you to confirm your direct deposit details through a link in an email. If you get one, report it to {{BENEFITS_CONTACT}}.

**Your 10-minute task**

Find your W-2 (paper or in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}}) and put it in one folder with any 1099 forms. Everything in one place is half the work of filing.
