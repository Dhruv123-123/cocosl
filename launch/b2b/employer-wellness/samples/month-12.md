**This month's money topic**

### Five money moves before the year ends

A few things have a December 31 deadline, and a few are just easier to do before the new year gets busy.

None of this takes long. Pick the ones that apply to you.

**The one thing to remember**

**A little end-of-year tidying makes January easier.**

> **From HR:** {{HR_NOTE}}

**Do it this month**

1. **Use your FSA money** if your plan is use-it-or-lose-it. Check the deadline and any grace period in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}}.
2. **Raise your {{RETIREMENT_PLAN}} contribution** for the new year, even by 1%. {{MATCH_LINE}} The IRS publishes next year's contribution limits each fall.
3. **Gather your records** for tax season: an envelope or folder for the forms that arrive in January.

**Good to know**

- **Moved or changed your name this year?** Update your address with payroll so your W-2 reaches you.
- **Beneficiaries:** check them once more, especially after a marriage, divorce or new baby.

**Your 10-minute task**

Check your address in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}} so your W-2 goes to the right place. It takes 2 minutes.

> Questions about your benefits? Contact **{{BENEFITS_CONTACT}}**. Something weighing on you? {{EAP_LINE}}
