**This month's money topic**

### Find the subscriptions you forgot about

Free trials that turn into monthly charges, streaming services you stopped watching, apps that renew every year: they're easy to forget because they're small.

Summer adds its own pressure: trips, kids home from school, sales everywhere. Two habits help without making you give anything up.

**The one thing to remember**

**Small charges you don't notice add up to real money. Look once, cancel what you don't use.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Do a subscription audit.** Scroll through the last two months of your bank and card statements and list every repeating charge.
2. **Cancel what you don't use**, and turn off auto-renew on anything you only want for a while.
3. **Try the 30-day list** for non-essential buys: write it down, and if you still want it in 30 days, buy it.

**Good to know**

- **Free trial?** Put the end date in your phone the day you sign up.
- **Your phone keeps a list too:** the subscriptions page in your app store shows what renews and when.

**Your 10-minute task**

Open your bank or card app and search for the word "subscription" or "monthly". Cancel one you don't use.
