**This month's money topic**

### A 20-minute money reset for the new year

New year, new plan year. Before January gets busy, take 20 minutes for three checks. None of them need a budget app or a spreadsheet, and each one keeps working for you all year.

The idea is simple: decide once, then let automatic transfers do the work. Money you never see in your checking account is money you don't have to resist spending.

**The one thing to remember**

**Decide once, automate it, and stop relying on willpower.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Check your {{RETIREMENT_PLAN}} contribution** in {{BENEFITS_PORTAL}}. {{MATCH_LINE}} If you're putting in less than the match, you're leaving part of your pay on the table.
2. **Set up one automatic transfer** to savings on payday, even $25. Name the account "Emergency fund". A common first goal is $1,000, then one month of essential bills.
3. **Pull your free credit reports** at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com), typed in yourself. They're free every week, and checking your own credit never lowers your score.

**Good to know**

- **Your benefits elections took effect January 1.** Look at your first paycheck of the year and make sure the deductions match what you picked.
- **Free trials:** when you sign up for one, set a reminder to cancel the same day.

**Your 10-minute task**

Open your first 2027 pay stub and find three numbers: take-home pay, your {{RETIREMENT_PLAN}} contribution, and your health plan deduction. If any look wrong, contact {{BENEFITS_CONTACT}} this week.
