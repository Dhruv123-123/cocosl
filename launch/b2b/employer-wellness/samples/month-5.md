**This month's money topic**

### Start a small emergency fund

A surprise bill, a car repair or a vet visit is often what pushes people onto a credit card or a payday loan. A small cushion breaks that chain.

You don't need three months of expenses to start. A first goal of $500, saved a little at a time, covers a lot of the surprises that cause the most stress.

**The one thing to remember**

**Small and automatic beats big and someday. Start with a first goal you can reach.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Pick a first goal**, like $500, and give it its own savings account so it isn't mixed with spending money.
2. **Automate it.** Ask payroll if your pay can be split between two accounts, or set an automatic transfer for payday. {{BENEFITS_CONTACT}} can help with direct deposit.
3. **Refill it after you use it.** That's what it's for.

**Good to know**

- **Tax refund or bonus?** Putting part of it in the cushion gets you there fast.
- **Struggling right now?** {{EAP_LINE}}

**Your 10-minute task**

Open or rename a savings account "Emergencies only" and set up one automatic transfer, even $10 a payday.
