**This month's money topic**

### What actually moves your credit score

Your credit score affects more than loans. It can change what you pay for car insurance, whether you need a deposit for utilities, and what a landlord thinks of your application.

A lot of what people believe about credit scores is wrong. Checking your own score doesn't lower it. Your income isn't part of it. And a high balance hurts only while it's high.

**The one thing to remember**

**Pay on time, keep card balances low compared with your limits, and check your reports for mistakes.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Set up autopay for at least the minimum** on every card and loan. Late payments are the single biggest drag on a score, and they can stay on your report for years.
2. **Pay the statement balance in full** if you can. You'll pay $0 interest on purchases, and lower balances help your score.
3. **Check your free credit reports** at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com) and dispute anything that isn't yours with the credit bureau.

**Good to know**

- **Your report and your score aren't the same thing.** The report is the record; the score is a number calculated from it.
- **A credit freeze is free** at each of the three bureaus. It stops new accounts being opened in your name, and you can lift it when you need credit.

**Your 10-minute task**

Turn on autopay for the minimum payment on every card you have. It takes about 2 minutes a card.
