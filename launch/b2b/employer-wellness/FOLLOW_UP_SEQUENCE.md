For prospects who **said yes to the sample** (or downloaded it) and then went quiet. This is different from the
cold sequence in `COLD_EMAILS.md`: that one is for people who haven't answered yet. **Nothing here has been sent.** Dhruv sends
each one by hand, in the same thread as the sample.

- **Sample page:** `{SAMPLE_URL}` = `https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/samples/employer/` once the landing page is published
  (`site/build_site.py` builds it). Until then, leave the link out and attach `out/demo-packet.pdf` instead.
- **Timing:** day 3, day 10 and day 21 after the sample went out, Tuesday to Thursday mornings. These count toward the
  daily cap in `../OUTREACH_DATES.md` (4 B2B sends a day from Dec 1).
- **Stop** at any reply. "No thanks" or "unsubscribe" means never again: log it in `PROSPECTS.csv`.
- Plain text, from your own inbox, with the same signature every time (name, mailing address, opt-out line).
- Fill `{First name}` and `{Org}` by hand. Never claim results you don't have.
