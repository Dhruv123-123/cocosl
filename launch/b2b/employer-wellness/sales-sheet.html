Why now

Money stress follows people to work. A monthly nudge to use the benefits you already pay for (the retirement match, the EAP, the withholding check) is the cheapest wellness program there is.

Each month you get

  • Employee email: HTML and plain text, about 350 words
  • Intranet, Teams or Slack post with a 1080×1080 image
  • Breakroom screen slide (1920×1080)
  • Sources page: IRS, DOL, CFPB, FTC and SEC links

How it works

  1. Send your logo, colors, benefits contact, portal name and your exact match wording, once.
  2. We deliver each issue 2 weeks before it goes out.
  3. HR or your broker reviews and edits anything.
  4. You send it and post it on your own channels.
