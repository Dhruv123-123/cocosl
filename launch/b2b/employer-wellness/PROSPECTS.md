**Why these:** companies that enter "best place to work" awards already invest in employee experience,
and the award gives you an honest opening line. Partners first: one SHRM chapter or broker can
introduce you to dozens of employers.

**Fit score (1–5):** 5 = local HQ with many hourly or frontline staff (they gain most from a plain-language
newsletter) or a partner who resells; 4 = local award winner with 35–499 staff; 3 = small team (Starter only);
1–2 = banks, lenders, insurers, law firms and other finance-savvy or centrally run employers. Employers are
sorted by score. It's a guess from public facts, so adjust it after looking at their site.

**Before you write:** fill `website` and `contact_route` from each organization's own site (HR or
people contact, chapter president, or the broker's benefits lead). Employee sizes come from the award
category, not a headcount; confirm before quoting a tier. The award pages showed only part of each
list, so there are more winners to add later.

Left out: banks, credit unions and financial firms on the lists (they have their own content or
compete), and national companies whose HR sits outside the region.
