Why it pays for itself

Freelance clients who make their estimated payments, keep a mileage log and upload everything in February are faster to prepare and less likely to call in April with a penalty notice. One month of reminders that does that for a handful of clients covers the year.

Each month you get

  • Client email: HTML and plain text, about 350 words, with your booking link
  • Social post with a 1080×1080 image
  • Printable client handout (Standard and Plus)
  • Notes for your team: the question clients will ask and what to say
  • Sources page: IRS links for every rule we mention

How it works

  1. Send your logo, colors, phone, booking link and client portal name, once.
  2. We deliver each issue 2 weeks before it goes out.
  3. You review it, edit anything, and approve.
  4. You send it from your own email platform. Your client list never comes to us.
