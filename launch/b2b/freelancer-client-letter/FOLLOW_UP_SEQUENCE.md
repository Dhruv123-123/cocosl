cold sequence in `README.md` (Cold emails): that one is for people who haven't answered yet. **Nothing here has been sent.** Dhruv sends
each one by hand, in the same thread as the sample.

  daily cap in `../OUTREACH_DATES.md` (4 B2B sends a day from Dec 1).
