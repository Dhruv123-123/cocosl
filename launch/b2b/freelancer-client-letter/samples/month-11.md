**This month for freelancers**

### Health insurance for next year

If you buy your own health insurance, open enrollment for next year's Marketplace plans starts **November 1**. End dates vary by state, and they've changed in recent years, so check yours now at HealthCare.gov or your state's marketplace.

Self-employed people can often deduct health insurance premiums for themselves and their family, with some limits. And your expected income matters: it's used to work out any help with premiums.

**The one thing to remember**

**Check your state's deadline this week, and use a realistic income estimate.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Look up your state's open enrollment dates** at HealthCare.gov.
2. **Estimate next year's income** carefully. Ask us if you're unsure: {{FIRM_PHONE}}.
3. **Keep your premium records** and Form 1095-A, if you get one. We'll need them for your return.

**Good to know**

- **Income changed during the year?** Update your Marketplace application so your premium help stays right.
- **HSA-eligible plan?** A Health Savings Account may be worth asking about.

**Your 10-minute task**

Put your state's open enrollment deadline in your calendar today.
