**This month for freelancers**

### Third payment, and two scams to skip

Your third 2027 estimated payment is due **September 15**. Pay the amount from your plan, unless your income has changed a lot.

Around payment dates, scammers pretend to be the IRS: a call, text or email says you owe and must pay now, by gift card, payment app or crypto. Others send fake invoices for services nobody ordered.

**The one thing to remember**

**The IRS usually contacts you first by mail, and never demands payment by gift card, payment app or crypto.**
