**This month for freelancers**

### Quarterly taxes, without the guessing

The first estimated payment for 2027 is due **April 15**, the same day as your 2026 return. The other three are **June 15**, **September 15** and **January 15, 2028**.

There's a rule that makes the amount easier. It's called the safe harbor: if you pay at least **100% of last year's total tax** (110% if last year's adjusted gross income was over $150,000), split across the four dates and paid on time, you generally avoid the underpayment penalty, even if you earn more this year. You still owe any balance in April.

**The one thing to remember**

**Pay on time, pay enough of last year's tax, and set the rest aside. Quarterly taxes should be boring.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Let us work out your 2027 payments** when we finish your 2026 return. We'll give you four amounts and four dates.
2. **Open a separate savings account** for taxes, if you don't have one, and move a share of every payment you receive into it.
3. **Put the four dates in your calendar** now: Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15 and Jan 15.

**Good to know**

- **Income changed a lot?** If this year will be much lower or higher, tell us. The safe harbor isn't always the cheapest choice.
- **Pay online** at [IRS.gov/payments](https://www.irs.gov/payments) and keep the confirmation. Your state may want its own payments too.

**Your 10-minute task**

Find last year's total tax on your 2025 return (Form 1040) and write it down. That number is where your 2027 plan starts.

## Notes for your team

- **Clients may ask:** "Can't I just pay it all in April?"
- **What to say:** "You can, but you may owe an underpayment penalty. Four on-time payments based on last year's tax usually avoid it. We'll work out the amounts with your return."
- **Watch for:** High earners (AGI over $150,000, or $75,000 married filing separately) need 110%, not 100%.
