**This month for freelancers**

### April 15 is two deadlines

For self-employed people, April 15 is the deadline to file your 2026 return and pay what you owe, **and** the date of your first estimated payment for 2027.

If you need more time, an extension (Form 4868) moves the filing deadline to October 15. It doesn't move the payment deadline. Tax not paid by April 15 can bring interest and penalties.

**The one thing to remember**

**Extend the paperwork if you need to, but pay what you expect to owe by April 15.**
