**This month for freelancers**

### The home office deduction

If you use part of your home for your business, you may be able to deduct some of the costs. The space generally has to be used **regularly and exclusively** for business, and be your main place of business or where you meet clients.

**Exclusively** matters: a desk in a guest room you also use for guests, or the kitchen table, usually doesn't count. There are two ways to figure the deduction: the simplified method ($5 a square foot, up to 300 square feet) or the regular method, based on actual expenses.

**The one thing to remember**

**A space used only for work, regularly, can count. A space you share with daily life usually doesn't.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Measure the space** you use only for work, and the total size of your home.
2. **Keep a few photos** of the space and a note of how you use it.
3. **Keep your home bills** (rent or mortgage interest, utilities, insurance) in your records. We'll work out which method is better.

**Good to know**

- **Employees working from home** generally can't take this deduction. It's for self-employed income.
- **Selling your home later?** The regular method can have tax effects then. We'll factor that in.
