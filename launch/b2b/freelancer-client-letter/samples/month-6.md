**This month for freelancers**

### June 15 comes fast

Your second 2027 estimated payment is due **June 15**. It covers income from April and May, which is why it feels like it comes right after the first one.

If you're using the safe harbor amount we worked out, pay the same as in April. If your income has changed a lot, let us know and we'll check the number.

**The one thing to remember**

**Same amount, on time. Put it in your calendar now.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Pay by June 15** at IRS.gov/payments, using the amount from your plan.
2. **Check your state:** many states have their own estimated payments on similar dates.
3. **Income up or down a lot?** Call {{FIRM_PHONE}} and we'll update the next two amounts.

**Good to know**

- **Set a reminder** for September 15 while you're at it.
- **Keep a tax savings account** and move a share of each payment you get into it, so the money is there.

**Your 10-minute task**

Put June 15, September 15 and January 15 in your phone calendar with the payment amount.

## Notes for your team

- **Clients may ask:** "Can I skip June and pay double in September?"
- **What to say:** "Each payment is due on its own date. Paying late can bring an underpayment penalty, even if you catch up later."
- **Watch for:** Clients whose income jumped this year. They may want to raise payments even if safe harbor protects them from the penalty.
