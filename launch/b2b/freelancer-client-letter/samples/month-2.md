**This month for freelancers**

### Your return, minus the scramble

A self-employed return takes longer than a W-2 return because your expenses are your own records, not a form someone sends you. The more you bring in one go, the faster and cheaper it is to prepare.

Deductions need records. A car, a home office or a phone bill can all count, but only the business part, and only if you can show how you worked it out.

**The one thing to remember**

**If you can't show it, you can't deduct it. A rough log now beats a perfect guess in April.**

- **The 8 things:** your income list and any 1099s; business bank and card statements; receipts for equipment and software; mileage log; home office size and your home's total size; health insurance premiums you paid; retirement contributions (SEP, Solo 401(k), IRA); and any estimated payments you made in 2026, with dates.
- **Phone and internet:** only the business share counts. Tell us your rough percentage and how you got it.

## Notes for your team

- **Clients may ask:** "Can I just deduct my whole phone bill and my car?"
- **What to say:** "Only the business share, and we need to see how you worked it out. A mileage log or a month of notes is enough to start."
- **Watch for:** Missing estimated-payment dates: ask clients to check IRS Online Account rather than guess.
