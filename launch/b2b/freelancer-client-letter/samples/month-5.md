**This month for freelancers**

### Make next tax season easier now

With last year's return done, May is the best month to make next year easier. Two habits do most of the work.

**Keep business money separate.** A separate checking account (and card, if you use one) for your freelance income and expenses makes your records clear. **Keep records of what you spend.** The IRS expects you to be able to show what each deduction was for.

**The one thing to remember**

**If it's business, it goes through the business account and the receipt gets saved.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Open a separate account** for your freelance money, if you don't have one. Many banks offer free accounts.
2. **Save receipts as you go:** snap a photo and put it in one folder, with a note on what it was for.
3. **Every month or so,** upload your statements to {{CLIENT_PORTAL}} or keep them in one place.

**Good to know**

- **How long to keep records?** Generally at least 3 years after you file, longer in some cases. Ask us what applies to you.
- **Paid by app?** Payment app records count, but they don't say what the purchase was for. Add a note.

**Your 10-minute task**

Make one folder on your phone called "Receipts 2027" and add the first one today.
