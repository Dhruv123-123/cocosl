**This month for freelancers**

### Retirement when you're your own boss

Without an employer plan, it's up to you. The good news: self-employed people have options that can lower this year's tax and build savings.

Common choices include a **SEP IRA**, a **solo 401(k)** and a **SIMPLE IRA**. They differ in how much you can put in, the paperwork, and the deadlines for setting them up. Some must be set up before the end of the year to count for this year.

**The one thing to remember**

**Ask in October, not April. Some plans have year-end deadlines.**

**Do it this month**

1. **Tell us if you want to save for retirement** this year, and roughly how much you could put in.
2. **We'll compare the plans** for your income and situation.

**Good to know**

- **Already have a plan?** Check that the contributions you make match what we agreed.
