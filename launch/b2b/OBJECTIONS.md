# Objection handling: the top 10 per offer

Short, honest answers for the replies you'll actually get. Answer in two or three sentences, then ask one
question or offer one next step. If an answer here isn't true for a prospect, don't use it. Never invent a
client, a result or an endorsement. Don't argue with a no: thank them, log it in `PIPELINE.csv`, and stop.

Prices are in each offer's README and sales sheet. "Sample" means the 3-issue `out/demo-packet.pdf` (or
`.zip`) with their logo only if they ask for it.

**Answers that apply to every offer**

- **"Is this written by AI?"** "Partly. Drafts are written with AI help, and a person checks every fact
  against the source linked under it. Each issue says so in its footer."
- **"Who else uses it?"** Tell the truth. Before the first client, say: "You'd be one of the first, which is
  why the first three get two months free and keep the founding price."
- **"Unsubscribe" / "remove me"**: Don't reply with a pitch. Mark them Do not contact the same day.

## 1. Credit unions: Weekly Scam Alert

| # | Objection | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "We already send fraud alerts." | "Good, then you know the hard part is doing it every week. This takes the writing and fact-checking off your plate. If yours are working, keep them; if they're irregular, this fills the gaps." |
| 2 | "Our compliance team has to approve everything." | "That's built in. Each pack arrives 7 days early, every claim links to a .gov source, and edits come back within a business day. The compliance note is a one-page summary for them." |
| 3 | "No budget this year." | "Essentials is $49 a month, often under a single sign-off limit, and the first 3 credit unions get 2 months free. Would a pilot timed to Consumer Protection Week help make the case?" |
| 4 | "Our members don't read email." | "Each issue also comes as a social post, a lobby slide and a branch poster, so it reaches members who never open email." |
| 5 | "We use our core/marketing vendor for this." | "Then ask whether they send a sourced scam alert weekly. If they do, you don't need this. If not, it drops straight into the email tool you already use." |
| 6 | "How do we know it's accurate?" | "Every claim links to the FTC, FBI, CFPB, IRS or another official source on its sources page, and we never promise members reimbursement." |
| 7 | "Will you see our member data?" | "No. You send it from your own platform. We never get a list, and there are no tracking pixels." |
| 8 | "Can we change the wording or topics?" | "Yes. Edit anything. Plus includes 2 custom issues a year on a scam your members are reporting." |
| 9 | "Send pricing." | Send the sales sheet PDF and one line: "Most credit unions your size pick Standard at $99 a month. Want a free sample with your logo?" |
| 10 | "Not now." | "Understood. National Consumer Protection Week (Feb 28 – Mar 6) is a natural start. May I check back in January?" Log a revisit date. |
