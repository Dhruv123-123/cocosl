### The $499 credit "fix"

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "We'll raise your score 100 points in 30 days and wipe your late payments. One-time setup fee: $499, due today."

Credit repair companies can't legally charge you before they've done the work. And no one can legally remove negative information from your credit report if it's accurate and current.

If your report has a mistake, you can dispute it yourself with the credit bureaus, for free.

**The tell**

**Anyone who wants payment before they help, or promises to erase accurate history, is breaking the rules.**

**What to do**

1. **Never pay up front** for credit repair.
2. **Get your free credit reports** at AnnualCreditReport.com and check them for mistakes.
3. **Dispute errors yourself** with the credit bureau, free. Want help reading your report or building credit? Call us at {{CU_PHONE}}.
