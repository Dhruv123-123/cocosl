### The second scam: "we can get your money back"

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Our records show you lost $3,200 to an investment scam. As a fund recovery office we can return it within 10 days. A $350 processing fee is due today by gift card."

It's National Consumer Protection Week, and all week we're covering one scam a day and the rights that protect you.

People who have already lost money to a scam often get a second call: someone who knows what happened offers to get the money back, for a fee up front. Scammers share lists of past victims, and the caller may claim to be a government office, a law firm or a nonprofit.

**The tell**

**Government agencies and real organizations don't ask for money to help you get a refund.**

> **Our promise:** {{CU_NEVER_ASK}}

**What to do**

1. **Never pay upfront** for a refund or help with a refund. Hang up.
2. **Check for yourself:** look up the agency's number on its own website and call it. Don't use the number the caller gave you.
3. **Lost money to a scam?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}}, and report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov). Reporting is free.

**Quick hits**

- **Payment by gift card, crypto or wire** for a "fee" is a sure sign.
- **This week:** one alert a day, Monday to Friday. Share them with someone you care about.
