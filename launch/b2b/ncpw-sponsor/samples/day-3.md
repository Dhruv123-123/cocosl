**National Consumer Protection Week**

### The free trial that isn't

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Try it free! Just pay $4.95 shipping." Fourteen days later: $89.95. Then $89.95 every month after that.

Some "free trial" offers for creams, supplements or gadgets sign you up for monthly charges, buried in the fine print. Canceling can be hard on purpose.

You have options: you can dispute charges you didn't agree to, and we can help you stop them.

**The tell**

**If a free trial asks for your card, find the cancel terms before you enter it.**

**What to do**

1. **Read the terms before you enter your card:** how long the trial lasts, what it costs after, and how to cancel.
2. **Put the trial's end date in your phone** the day you sign up.
3. **Seeing a charge you didn't agree to?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}}. We can help you dispute it and block future charges.

**Quick hits**

- **Check your statements** once a month for repeating charges you don't recognize.
- **Turn on card alerts** in our app, so a surprise charge shows up right away.

**Your 2-minute job today**

Open our app and look for any repeating charge you don't recognize.
