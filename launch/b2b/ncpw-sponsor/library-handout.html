Five scams to know, and the one rule for each

1

"We can get your lost money back"

Never pay upfront for a refund or help with a refund.

2

A call from "the FTC" or another agency

The FTC will never call to demand money, threaten arrest or promise a prize. Hang up.

3

A "free" trial that only costs shipping

Read how to cancel before you give your card. Check your statement every month.

4

A debt collector who threatens jail

That's not allowed. Ask for the debt details in writing. Don't pay on the first call.

5

Credit repair for a fee up front

They can't charge first. Your credit reports are free at AnnualCreditReport.com.

Lost money, or not sure? Call your bank or credit union using the number on your card. Report scams free at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
