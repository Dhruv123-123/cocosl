Questions

Do you need our member or employee list?

No. You send everything from your own systems. We never receive names, emails, account or client data.

Is this legal or investment advice?

No. It's general education, checked against official sources, and it never recommends a product, fund or amount. Your team reviews every issue before it goes out.

Can we change the wording?

Yes, anything. Send edits before the deadline on each issue and we return updated files within a business day.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans cancel with 30 days' notice. The founding pilot (2 months free for the first 3 organizations in each offer) has no contract.

Do you use AI?

Drafting is AI-assisted. A person checks every issue against the sources listed in it before it ships.
