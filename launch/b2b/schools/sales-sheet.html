Why schools use it

Montana now requires a half-credit of financial education or economics for graduation, starting with the class of 2026, and most states have a personal finance requirement. Full curricula exist. What teachers and advisors are short of is a current, ready-to-teach 10 minutes for advisory, homeroom, an economics or math class, or a first-year seminar.

Each week you get

  • Class slide (1920×1080) and a student handout (PDF)
  • Lesson page to post or email: example, activity, discussion questions, exit ticket
  • Teacher notes: the standard, timing, answer key and what to watch for
  • LMS post with an image for Google Classroom or Canvas
  • Sources: IRS, CFPB and FTC links for every fact

How it works

  1. Send your logo, colors and who students should ask, once.
  2. Each lesson arrives 2 weeks before its week.
  3. Teachers review it, adapt anything, and teach it.
  4. No student data, logins or tracking. Ever.
