## What each issue says

**Week 5, Feb 2 (`2027-02-a`): Fake data breach settlements, and 3 free locks**  
A breach letter or call that asks for your account number or a fee to claim a settlement. Turn it into 3 locks: credit freeze, 2-step login on online banking, card alerts in the app.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.identitytheft.gov/> · <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-credit-freezes-and-fraud-alerts>

**Week 6, Feb 9 (`2027-02-b`): Romance scams and the gift card favor**  
The new online friend asks for gift cards, then a photo of the back of the card. Staff angle: tellers spotting large gift-card buys and repeated wires.  
Sources to start from: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams> · <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoiding-and-reporting-gift-card-scams>

**Week 7, Feb 16 (`2027-02-c`): "Hi Mom, this is my new number"**  
Text from a "child" with a new number who needs a bill paid by P2P. Fix: call the old number, agree a family code word.  
Sources to start from: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/scammers-use-fake-emergencies-steal-your-money>

**Week 8, Feb 23 (`2027-02-d`): Ghost tax preparers**  
Preparers who won't sign the return, take a cut of the refund, or route it to their own account. Tell members to deposit refunds only into their own account here.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/be-informed-not-fooled-by-ghost-preparers-and-tax-credit-scams> · <https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/choosing-a-tax-professional>

**Week 9, Mar 2 (`2027-03-a`): Government imposters: Social Security numbers don't get "suspended"**  
Calls from "SSA", "Medicare" or "the sheriff" that end with moving money to a "safe account". Promise box: we will never ask you to move money to keep it safe.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.ssa.gov/scam/> · <https://oig.ssa.gov/>

**Week 10, Mar 9 (`2027-03-b`): Spring break rentals and March Madness "sure wins"**  
Half-price rental that wants a wire, Zelle or gift card deposit. Pay for travel by card, never P2P to a stranger.  
Sources to start from: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/rental-listing-scams>

**Week 11, Mar 16 (`2027-03-c`): Student loan forgiveness fees**  
"You qualify for forgiveness, $499 to apply." The help is free at studentaid.gov; never share an FSA ID. Good for young members and parents.  
Sources to start from: <https://studentaid.gov/resources/scams>

**Week 12, Mar 23 (`2027-03-d`): Unpaid toll texts**  
A text says you owe a small toll and links to a pay page that steals card details. Fix: don't tap; check your toll account by typing the address yourself; if you paid, call us to block the card.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/01/got-text-about-unpaid-tolls-its-probably-scam> · <https://www.ic3.gov/>

**Week 13, Mar 30 (`2027-03-e`): The fake IRS letter that wants a phone call**  
Paper letters with a callback number. Three checks: notice number, look up the number on irs.gov, log in to your IRS online account.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-irs-notice-or-letter> · <https://www.irs.gov/help/tax-scams/recognize-tax-scams-and-fraud>

**Week 14, Apr 6 (`2027-04-a`): The "government grant" a friend sent you**  
A hacked friend's account offers free grant money for a fee. Call the friend; real grants don't charge fees.  
Sources to start from: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/government-grant-scams> · <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recover-your-hacked-email-or-social-media-account>

**Week 15, Apr 13 (`2027-04-b`): After you file: the "refund adjustment" text**  
Texts about a changed refund that link to a fake IRS page. One place to check: Where's My Refund on irs.gov.  
Sources to start from: <https://www.irs.gov/wheres-my-refund> · <https://www.irs.gov/help/tax-scams/recognize-tax-scams-and-fraud>

**Week 16, Apr 20 (`2027-04-c`): Payment app scams: "send it back" and "send it to yourself"**  
Overpayment by P2P then "send it back", and fake fraud staff who say to Zelle money "to yourself". Promise box. Reg E note for the staff brief: defer to the credit union's own dispute process.  
Sources to start from: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/mobile-payment-apps-how-avoid-scam-when-you-use-one>

## Before each issue ships

- **Open every source link on fact-check day.** These are starting points picked on Nov 7 2026; official
  pages move. Replace any dead link and add a second source where only one is listed (the rule is 2 per issue).
- **Check the season hooks.** Slam the Scam Day's date is set by SSA's inspector general each year; confirm it
  before writing week 9. If a big new FTC or FBI warning lands, swap it in and push the planned topic back a
  week; keep the swap in this file and the CSV.
- **Client requests win.** A Plus client's custom issue replaces the planned topic for that client only;
  everyone else gets the calendar issue.
- **Stay 2 issues ahead.** By the first client's first send, weeks 5 and 6 should already be written.
- When an issue is written, add it to `issues.json` and set its `status` in the CSV to `written`, then
  `checked`, then `delivered`.
