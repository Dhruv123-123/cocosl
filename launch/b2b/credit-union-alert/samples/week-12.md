**This week's scam**

### The toll you don't owe

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "FINAL NOTICE: You have an unpaid toll balance of $6.99. Pay now to avoid a $50 late fee and suspension of your license: [link]"

The amount is small on purpose. The link goes to a fake payment page that takes your card number and sometimes a code from your phone.

These texts go to people who have never driven a toll road.

**The tell**

**A text that threatens fees and asks you to pay a toll through a link is a scam. Check your toll account by typing the real website yourself.**

**What to do**

1. **Don't tap the link** and don't reply. Delete it.
2. **Check for yourself:** if you use toll roads, type your toll agency's website into your browser, or call the number on your statement.
3. **Already entered your card?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}} to block the card and watch for charges.

**Quick hits**

- **Forward scam texts to 7726** (SPAM) to help your carrier block them.
- **Same trick, different name:** "undelivered package" and "unpaid parking" texts work the same way.

**Your 5-minute job this week**

Turn on card alerts in our app if you haven't, so any charge from a fake page shows up on your phone right away.
