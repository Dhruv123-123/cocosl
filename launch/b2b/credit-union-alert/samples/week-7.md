**This week's scam**

### The new number that needs a bill paid

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Hi Mom, I dropped my phone in the sink 😩 this is my new number. Can you save it? Also I need to pay a bill today and my banking app won't work on the new phone, can you send it for me? I'll pay you back tonight."

It starts friendly and familiar. Within a few texts there's a bill that has to be paid today, by a payment app or transfer to an account you don't recognize.

Money sent by a person-to-person app or transfer is very hard to get back once it's gone.

**The tell**

**A "new number" plus an urgent payment to someone else's account is a scam until you've heard your child's real voice on their old number.**

**What to do**

1. **Call the old number.** If they really have a new phone, they can still prove it by calling or texting from a number you know, or by a video call.
2. **Agree a family code word** that only your family knows, for any text or call asking for money.
3. **Already sent money?** Call us right away at {{CU_PHONE}}. The sooner we know, the more we can try to do.

**Quick hits**

- **The same trick works on grandparents and on bosses:** "It's me, I'm in a meeting, I need gift cards for a client."
- **Only send P2P payments to people you know**, on a number or email you already had.

**Your 5-minute job this week**

Pick a family code word tonight and text it to your kids and parents. Don't use anything that's on social media.
