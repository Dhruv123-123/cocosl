**This week's scam**

### The wrong number that becomes an investment

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Hi Emma, are we still on for dinner Saturday? ... Oh sorry, wrong number! You seem nice though. Where are you based?"

This one is patient. Weeks of friendly chat later, your new friend mentions how well they're doing on a crypto trading site and offers to show you how.

The site shows your balance growing. When you try to take money out, there's a "tax" or "fee" to pay first. The balance was never real, and the fees keep coming.

**The tell**

**Anyone you've only met online who brings up investing, on a site or app they picked, is running a scam. A fee to withdraw your own money means there's no money.**

**What to do**

1. **Don't invest** through a link or app sent by someone you met online. Delete and block wrong-number texts instead of replying.
2. **About to buy crypto or send a wire for an investment?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}} first, or stop by a branch. Talking it through with someone you trust is the best protection there is.
3. **Already invested?** Stop paying fees. Report it to the FBI at [IC3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov) and the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

**Quick hits**

- **"Recovery" services** that promise to get your crypto back for a fee are often the same scammers coming back for more.
- **Check before you invest:** you can look up an adviser or firm for free at [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov).

**Your 5-minute job this week**

Don't reply to any wrong-number texts this week. Delete and block. It's the cheapest scam protection there is.
