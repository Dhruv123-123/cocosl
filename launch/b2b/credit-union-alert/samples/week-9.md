**This week's scam**

### The government call that ends at a "safe account"

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "This is Officer Daniels with the Social Security Administration. Your number was linked to a crime and has been suspended. To protect your savings, you must move them to a secure government account today."

Scammers pretend to be Social Security, the IRS, Medicare, the FTC or a local sheriff. They threaten arrest or frozen benefits, then tell you to move money to a "safe" account, buy gift cards, or hand cash to a courier.

No government agency will ever do that. And neither will we.

**The tell**

**Nobody real tells you to move your money to keep it safe. Not the government, not your bank, not us.**

> **Our promise:** {{CU_NEVER_ASK}}

**What to do**

1. **Hang up.** Then call the agency yourself on a number you look up, like Social Security at 1-800-772-1213.
2. **Before moving money because someone told you to,** call us at {{CU_PHONE}}. We'll help you check.
3. **Report it** to the Social Security inspector general at [oig.ssa.gov](https://oig.ssa.gov) and at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

**Quick hits**

- **Caller ID lies.** It can show a real government name or number.
- **Slam the Scam Day** is run each March by Social Security's inspector general; it's a good day to tell one person this rule.
