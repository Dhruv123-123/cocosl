**This week's scam**

### The job that asks you to pay

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Hi! I'm a recruiter with a digital marketing agency. Flexible remote work, 60 to 90 minutes a day rating products, $300 to $800 a day. Interested? Message me on WhatsApp."

The first small payouts are real, to build trust. Then your "account" goes negative and you have to deposit money, often in crypto, to unlock your earnings. That money doesn't come back.

Another version mails you a check to buy equipment from their "vendor". The money can show up in your account before the check clears. When it bounces days later, you owe back what you spent or sent.

**The tell**

**A real job never asks you to pay, deposit or buy anything to get paid, and never sends you a check to pass money on to someone else.**

**What to do**

1. **Don't reply.** Look the company up yourself and apply through its real careers page.
2. **Got a check from a new employer, buyer or stranger?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}} before you spend or send any of it. We'd much rather answer a question than help clean up a bounced check.
3. **Already paid?** Stop, even if they say one more deposit releases your money. Report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

**Quick hits**

- **Interviews by text only:** real employers meet you by video or in person.
- **"Receive money and forward it":** a job that has you move money through your account is moving stolen money, and you can be held responsible.

## Staff huddle brief

- **Members may ask:** "When will this check clear? My new job sent it for equipment."
- **What to say:** "Funds showing as available doesn't mean the check is good. Can you tell me about the job? A check you're asked to send part of somewhere else is a common scam."
- **Escalate:** Follow our deposit hold and fraud procedures. If the member already sent money, note who it went to and how.
