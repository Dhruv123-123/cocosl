**This week's scam**

### The forgiveness fee

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Great news! Under the new program your student loans qualify for forgiveness. Spots are limited. Pay the $499 processing fee today and send us your FSA ID so we can file for you."

Student loan rules change often, and every change brings a wave of calls, texts and ads offering to "handle it for you" for a fee.

The help is real, but it's free from your loan servicer and the Department of Education. Scammers also ask for your FSA ID, which lets them change your contact details and redirect your account.

**The tell**

**You never have to pay for help with federal student loans, and you should never give anyone your FSA ID.**

**What to do**

1. **Go to StudentAid.gov** or call your loan servicer directly. Both are free.
2. **Never share your FSA ID** or sign a form giving a company power of attorney over your loans.
3. **Paid a company already?** Call us at {{CU_PHONE}} to stop future payments, then report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

**Quick hits**

- **Monthly "service" fees** taken from your account are a common next step. Check your statements for new recurring charges.
- **Parents:** this targets you too if you have Parent PLUS loans.

**Your 5-minute job this week**

Look up who your federal loan servicer is on StudentAid.gov and save their real number in your phone.
