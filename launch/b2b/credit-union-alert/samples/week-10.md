**This week's scam**

### The rental that's too good to be true

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Beach house, sleeps 8, spring break week, $900! Owner is out of the country so we can't show it, but send the deposit by Zelle today to hold it. Lots of interest!"

Scammers copy real listings, lower the price, and ask for a deposit by P2P app, wire or gift card. You arrive to find the house belongs to someone else, or doesn't exist.

March Madness brings its own version: paid "insider picks" or betting groups that guarantee wins, then ask for more money to unlock bigger payouts.

**The tell**

**A deal that must be paid today, by P2P, wire or gift card, to someone you can't meet is a scam. Nobody can guarantee a win.**

**What to do**

1. **Pay for travel with a credit card** through the booking site, never by P2P or wire to a stranger.
2. **Search the address** and the owner's name. If the same photos show up under another name or price, walk away.
3. **Already sent a deposit?** Call us right away at {{CU_PHONE}} and report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

**Quick hits**

- **"Owner is overseas, can't show it"** is the classic line.
- **Guaranteed picks** don't exist. If someone sells certainty, they're selling a scam.

**Your 5-minute job this week**

If you're booking a trip this spring, use a card with fraud protection and keep every receipt and message.
