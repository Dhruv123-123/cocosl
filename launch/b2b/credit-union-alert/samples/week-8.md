**This week's scam**

### The preparer who takes a cut and vanishes

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "Bigger refund guaranteed! We file for you, no paperwork needed. Fee comes out of your refund. Just sign here, we'll handle the rest."

The IRS calls them ghost preparers. They prepare your return for a fee but won't sign it as the paid preparer, sometimes invent credits to inflate your refund, or route the refund through their own account.

If the return is wrong, you're the one who owes the tax, the penalties and the interest.

**The tell**

**A real paid preparer signs your return with their PTIN and has your refund go to your own account. If they won't, walk away.**

**What to do**

1. **Ask for their PTIN** (Preparer Tax Identification Number). Every paid preparer must have one and must sign your return.
2. **Have your refund deposited into your own account here.** Never into the preparer's account, and never on a prepaid card they hand you.
3. **Read before you sign.** Never sign a blank return or one with numbers you don't understand. Get a copy.

**Quick hits**

- **Free help exists:** IRS Free File and VITA sites help many people file for free. Search for them on IRS.gov.
