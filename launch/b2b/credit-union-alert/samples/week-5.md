**This week's scam**

### The breach settlement that pays scammers

*What it looks like (made-up example):* "You may be owed up to $1,200 from a data breach settlement. Confirm your identity and the account where you'd like your payment deposited: [link]"

Real data breaches happen, and some do end in real settlements. Scammers copy them: a text or email says you're owed money, then asks for your account number, card number or Social Security number "to send your payment".

A real settlement never needs your password, PIN or a one-time code, and never charges a fee to pay you.

**The tell**

**Anyone who asks for your login, a code or a fee before paying you a settlement isn't paying you anything.**
