2. **No provider is named or ranked** on screen or in captions: "Opción A / B / C".
3. Repeat the "¿Cuánto llega este mes?" carousel monthly after this run, same amount, corridor and delivery method.

Nothing goes out until a fluent Spanish speaker has signed off the piece (`REVIEW_CHECKLIST_ENVIOS_2027.md`) and the facts under CHECK BEFORE POSTING are confirmed. Never name a bank, lender or remittance company on camera. Times America/New_York.
