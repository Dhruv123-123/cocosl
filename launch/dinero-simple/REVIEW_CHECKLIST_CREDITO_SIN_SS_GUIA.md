# Review checklist: Crédito sin Seguro Social: guía para empezar (lead magnet)

Nothing in `GUIA_CREDITO_SIN_SS.md`, its PDF or the `/credito-sin-seguro-social/` page is shared until every box is ticked. Until then, keep the page out of the build with `hold` in `sites/dinero-simple/site.json`. Edit `credito/guia.py`, never the outputs, then run `python3 launch/dinero-simple/credito/build_credito.py --pdf`.

- [ ] **Native-speaker review** of the whole guide (machine-written Spanish).
- [ ] **CREDIT EXPERT REVIEW:** a HUD-approved housing counselor, NFCC counselor or credit union lender checks every credit fact below.
- [ ] **ATTORNEY REVIEW:** a licensed immigration attorney or DOJ-accredited representative reads the 7 sections marked below (every section that mentions the ITIN).
- [ ] **Ask two local credit unions** whether they open accounts, secured cards and credit-builder loans with an ITIN, and note what ID they take.

### 1. Por qué importa tu crédito
- [ ] Uses of credit reports: rental screening, auto loans, phone plans, and (in most states) auto insurance pricing (CFPB)

### 2. Paso 1: el ITIN · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] ITIN: 9-digit IRS number for people who must file and are not eligible for an SSN; for federal tax purposes only
- [ ] W-7 options: with a return by mail, IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center by appointment, Certifying Acceptance Agent; no IRS fee
- [ ] ITINs not used on a federal return for 3 consecutive years expire Dec 31 of the third year

### 3. Paso 2: una cuenta de banco o cooperativa · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] Some banks and credit unions accept an ITIN plus a foreign passport or consular ID (requirements vary)
- [ ] FDIC/NCUA insurance: up to $250,000 per depositor, per institution, per ownership category

### 4. Paso 3: la tarjeta asegurada · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] Secured card deposit usually equals the limit; some issuers accept ITIN applications; not all report to all three bureaus
- [ ] Paying the full statement balance by the due date avoids purchase interest
- [ ] Graduation to an unsecured card is issuer-specific (6-12 months is common, not guaranteed)

### 5. Paso 4: el préstamo para construir crédito · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] Credit-builder loans: funds held in savings or a CD until repaid (CFPB); offered by many credit unions and CDFIs, some to ITIN holders

### 6. Paso 5: cómo cuidar tu puntaje
- [ ] FICO factors: payment history (~35%), amounts owed (~30%), length of history (~15%), new credit (~10%), credit mix (~10%)
- [ ] A FICO score generally needs at least one account open 6 months and one reported in the last 6 months

### 7. Tus reportes de crédito, gratis · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] Free weekly reports from all three bureaus at AnnualCreditReport.com, 1-877-322-8228 or by mail
- [ ] How ITIN holders request reports in practice (online identity check may require an SSN): confirm with each bureau

### 8. Si después recibes número de Seguro Social · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] After getting an SSN, notify the IRS so ITIN records are combined; stop using the ITIN (IRS)
- [ ] Ask creditors to update the identifier; bureaus generally keep the history under the same name and address (confirm with a credit expert)

### 9. Las trampas que debes evitar · **ATTORNEY REVIEW**
- [ ] Advance-fee loans are a scam signal (FTC); Credit Repair Organizations Act bars charging before services are performed
- [ ] FTC/CFPB: CPNs are often stolen SSNs; using one on a credit application can be a federal crime

### 10. Ayuda gratis
- [ ] HUD housing counselor line 1-800-569-4287; CFPB 855-411-2372 (Spanish available); NFCC member agencies offer Spanish service
