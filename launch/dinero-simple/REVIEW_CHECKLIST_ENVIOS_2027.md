# Dinero Simple "Mandar dinero a casa" (Tuesdays, Mar 2 – Apr 6, 2027): review checklist

Nothing in `content-factory/dinero-simple/envios-2027/` goes out until both boxes for that piece are ticked: a fluent Spanish speaker has read every word (on screen, caption, narration) and fixed anything that sounds translated, and every fact listed under it has been checked. Each folder's `caption.txt` has the same facts under CHECK BEFORE POSTING.

Highest-risk facts (check these first): the 1% remittance excise tax rules (cash, money order, cashier's check; exempt when funded from a US account or US-issued card), the CFPB 30-minute cancellation and 180-day error rights, and every [LLENAR] in the monthly carousel. Native-speaker review matters most for money words that vary by country (envío, giro, remesa, tipo de cambio).

### 03. El impuesto de 1% a los envíos en efectivo (short, videos/)
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] 1% excise tax on remittance transfers paid in cash, money order, cashier's check or similar physical instrument, for transfers made after Dec 31, 2025 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, IRC sec. 4475)
- [ ] Exempt when funded from an account at a US financial institution or with a US-issued debit or credit card
- [ ] Check for any IRS guidance issued since November 2026
