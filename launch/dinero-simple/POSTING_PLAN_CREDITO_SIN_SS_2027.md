Six Shorts, one step each, from the ITIN to a growing credit history, then a summary carousel. Every post offers the free guide (lead magnet, `credito/`, also on the site at `/credito-sin-seguro-social/`).

7 posts: 6 Shorts and 1 carousel(s), one a week on a day no other Dinero Simple plan uses. 7 mention the ITIN and carry ATTORNEY REVIEW.

## Rules for this series

1. **Every piece mentions the ITIN**, so every piece needs ATTORNEY REVIEW the week it posts, and a credit expert (HUD-approved housing counselor, NFCC counselor or credit union lender) checks the credit facts.
2. **No bank, card or lender is named.** "Una cooperativa de crédito o un prestamista comunitario" is enough.
3. Nothing promises approval or a score. The ITIN never appears as a path to work authorization or status.

Nothing goes out until a fluent Spanish speaker has signed off the piece (`REVIEW_CHECKLIST_CREDITO_SIN_SS_2027.md`) and the facts under CHECK BEFORE POSTING are confirmed. Never name a bank, lender or remittance company on camera. Times America/New_York.
