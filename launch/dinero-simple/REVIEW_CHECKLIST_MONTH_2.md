# Dinero Simple month 2: review checklist

Nothing in `content-factory/dinero-simple/month-2/` goes out until both boxes for that piece are ticked: a fluent Spanish speaker has read every word (on screen, caption, narration) and fixed anything that sounds translated, and every fact listed under it has been checked against its source. Highest-risk facts this month (check these first): the 2027 filing-season opening date, the Feb 1, 2027 W-2 deadline, the ITIN expiration and W-7 rules, the VITA / Free File income limits (this year's figures), the PATH Act refund hold for EITC/ACTC, the 21-day refund line, and the April 15, 2027 IRA deadline for 2026. Tax rules and dates change every year; check irs.gov the week you post.

### 08. Todavía puedes aportar a tu IRA de 2026
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)

- [ ] Traditional IRA deductibility depends on income and workplace plan coverage ('a veces')
- [ ] Roth IRA eligibility has income limits; not mentioned, consider adding

### 09. ¿Tu ITIN venció? Revísalo antes de declarar
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] ITIN expires if not used on a federal tax return at least once in three consecutive tax years (expires Dec 31 of the third year)
- [ ] Filing with an expired ITIN: return processed but delays and some credits disallowed until renewed
- [ ] Form W-7 is free; can be filed with the return, by mail, in person at a TAC, or through a Certifying Acceptance Agent
- [ ] Confirm wording on renewing 'antes' (IRS generally requires renewal with a return unless an exception applies)

### 10. Tu W-2 tiene que llegar antes del 1 de febrero
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] W-2 due to employees by Jan 31; when it falls on a weekend the deadline is the next business day (Mon Feb 1, 2027)
- [ ] IRS Topic 154: contact the IRS if the W-2 hasn't arrived by the end of February

### 11. Hay quien te hace los impuestos gratis
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] VITA: free prep for people generally earning up to an income limit set each year (around $69,000 recently; check the 2027 season figure before posting if you add a number)
- [ ] TCE serves taxpayers 60 and older
- [ ] Many sites offer Spanish-speaking volunteers (not all)

### 12. ¿Trabajaste por tu cuenta? Eso también se declara
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Self-employment income is taxable whether or not a 1099 is received
- [ ] 1099-K reporting thresholds changed (OBBBA restored $20,000 / 200 transactions); no threshold is quoted, keep it that way
- [ ] Self-employment tax counts toward Social Security credits
- [ ] 20-30% set-aside is a common rule of thumb, not advice; check wording

### 13. ¿Cuándo llega tu reembolso?

- [ ] PATH Act: refunds claiming EITC/ACTC cannot be issued before mid-February

- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
