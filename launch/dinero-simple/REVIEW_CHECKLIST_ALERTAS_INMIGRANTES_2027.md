Nothing in `content-factory/dinero-simple/alertas-inmigrantes-2027/` goes out until both boxes for that piece are ticked: a fluent Spanish speaker has read every word (on screen, caption, narration) and fixed anything that sounds translated, and every fact listed under it has been checked. Highest-risk facts (check these first): the H-2A/H-2B recruitment-fee rule, how USCIS fees are paid, and that no example names a program whose status is in flux. If a program in the news changes the week a piece posts, swap the example rather than the rule.
