# Dinero Simple month 3: review checklist

Nothing in `content-factory/dinero-simple/month-3/` goes out until both boxes for that piece are ticked: a fluent Spanish speaker has read every word (on screen, caption, narration) and fixed anything that sounds translated, and every fact listed under it has been checked against its source. Each folder's `caption.txt` has the same facts under CHECK BEFORE POSTING.

Highest-risk facts this month (check these first): anything about the IRS sharing information with immigration authorities (the 2025 IRS-DHS agreement and its court cases: get a licensed immigration attorney to read the ITIN myths carousel and the 'Privacidad' segment of the ITIN guide the week they post, and cut them if in doubt), the 2025 changes to SSN rules for the Child Tax Credit, ITIN documents and W-7 rules for dependents, ITIN processing times, CalEITC for ITIN filers, and the IRS penalty rates. Tax rules and dates change every year; check irs.gov the week you post.


## Shorts (videos/01–14)

### 01. Cómo pedir tu primer ITIN
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] W-7 generally must be attached to a federal return (exceptions exist) and mailed
- [ ] A valid passport is the only stand-alone document; otherwise two documents from the IRS list, one with a photo
- [ ] Certifying Acceptance Agents and some IRS Taxpayer Assistance Centers can verify originals
- [ ] Processing takes about 7 weeks, longer in filing season (IRS says 9-11 weeks Jan-Apr); check current figure

### 02. Lo que tu ITIN es, y lo que no es
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] IRS: ITIN is for federal tax reporting only; does not authorize work, provide Social Security benefits, or qualify for EITC; does not change immigration status
- [ ] Many (not all) banks accept an ITIN to open an account

### 03. ITIN para tu pareja y tus hijos
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Every person listed on a return needs an SSN or ITIN
- [ ] Dependent ITIN applicants must prove US residency (school/medical records) unless from Mexico or Canada or dependents of military
- [ ] Dependents abroad: generally only residents of Mexico/Canada can be claimed (plus some US nationals); confirm wording
- [ ] OBBBA changed some credit rules for dependents; no credit claims made here

### 04. ¿Por qué declarar si tienes ITIN?
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Filing requirement depends on income, status and age; self-employment filing threshold is $400 net
- [ ] Over-withheld tax is refunded only by filing; refunds generally must be claimed within 3 years
- [ ] Using returns as proof of income for rentals/loans is common; no claim about immigration benefits

### 05. Tu estado civil en la declaración cambia lo que pagas
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Marital status determined on Dec 31
- [ ] Head of household: unmarried, paid more than half the cost of keeping up a home, qualifying person lived with you more than half the year (with exceptions)
- [ ] IRS 'What is my filing status?' interactive tool exists in Spanish (check)

### 06. Tu reembolso, a tu cuenta
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] IRS warns against refunds deposited to a preparer's account
- [ ] Federal paper-check phase-out (EO of March 2025, effective Sept 30, 2025) and IRS guidance on refunds; confirm current wording
- [ ] Many banks/credit unions accept ITIN (see the ITIN banking article)

### 07. Divide tu reembolso en hasta 3 cuentas
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Form 8888 allows splitting a direct-deposit refund into up to three accounts in the filer's name
- [ ] Example arithmetic only

### 08. Guarda copia de tu declaración
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] IRS: keep records generally 3 years (longer in some cases)
- [ ] Preparers must give the taxpayer a copy of the return

### 09. El PIN que impide que alguien declare con tu número
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] IP PIN: six digits, new every year, available to anyone with SSN or ITIN who can verify identity
- [ ] Online verification may not work for everyone (alternatives: Form 15227 or in-person)

### 10. 'Ya se presentó una declaración con tu número'
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Rejected e-file due to duplicate SSN/ITIN: file Form 14039 with a paper return (IRS identity theft guide)
- [ ] IRS contacts by letter; IP PIN afterwards
- [ ] robodeidentidad.gov is the FTC's Spanish site

### 11. No olvides la declaración de tu estado
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Texas, Florida and Nevada have no state income tax on wages (also AK, SD, TN, WY, NH on wages; WA has capital gains tax)
- [ ] CalEITC available to ITIN filers since tax year 2020 (check current rules)
- [ ] Some other states also allow ITIN filers to claim state credits; not named

### 12. Te llegó una carta del IRS: qué hacer
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Notice number top right corner; CP2000 is an underreporting notice
- [ ] IRS never demands gift cards, crypto or wire transfers
- [ ] Taxpayer Advocate Service is free and offers Spanish help

### 13. ¿Debes impuestos y no te alcanza? Declara igual
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Failure-to-file penalty 5% of unpaid tax per month (max 25%); failure-to-pay 0.5% per month (max 25%)
- [ ] Online payment plans available for individuals owing up to IRS limits (e.g. $50,000 long-term); not quoted

### 14. Después de declarar, ajusta tu W-4
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] $3,000 / 12 = $250 a month (arithmetic)
- [ ] IRS Tax Withholding Estimator exists (Spanish version available; check)
