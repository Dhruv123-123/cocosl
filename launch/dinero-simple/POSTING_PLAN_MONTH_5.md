# Dinero Simple: month 5 posting plan (April 2027)

14 Shorts, 8 carousels and 2 long videos. The first half of April is taxes for workers before Thursday April 15: W-2 or 1099 (with numbers), expenses, cash pay, the new tips and overtime deductions (which need a valid SSN), two jobs, filing with an ITIN, and the last week. The second half, when refunds arrive, is sending money home for less: how much and how often, how to pay, how the family receives it, the 1% cash tax, your rights and the transfer-number scam.

Mon/Wed/Fri at 12:30 for Shorts, 20:00 for carousels, Sunday 20:00 for the long videos (plus one Short on Wed Apr 7 at 20:00 and Sun Apr 11 at 12:30). Times America/New_York.

Rules: nothing goes out until a fluent Spanish speaker has signed off the piece (`REVIEW_CHECKLIST_MONTH_5.md`) and the facts under CHECK BEFORE POSTING in its caption.txt are confirmed. **7 pieces mention the ITIN and carry an ATTORNEY REVIEW line: a licensed immigration attorney or DOJ-accredited representative reads them the week they post.** '¿Miedo de declarar con ITIN? No lo decidas solo' is the riskiest piece this month; if no attorney can read it that week, skip it. Never name a remittance company on camera. Everything is general information, not tax, legal or financial advice.

**producto** marks pieces whose caption mentions one of our own kits. The long videos end their YouTube description with UTM-tagged links to the site's tax-prep and money-transfer comparisons and the budget kit (`LINKS.md`).
