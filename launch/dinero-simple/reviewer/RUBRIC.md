# Scoring the paid review test

Score the returned `Prueba-Pagada-Revision.pdf` against `ANSWER_KEY.md` (12 planted problems). Pay the $45
either way. Fill in the sheet in about 20 minutes.

## Points (100)

| Area | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| **Planted problems caught** | 36 | 3 points for each of the 12 that the reviewer fixed or flagged. For #4 (the 24 paychecks), a FLAG is worth 3 and a silent fix only 1, because rule 4 says don't change numbers. |
| **Quality of their other edits** | 30 | Pick 10 changes that aren't planted. For each: natural for a US Latino reader (+1), correct (+1), keeps the meaning (+1). |
| **Register and audience** | 10 | Kept tú throughout, used US Latino words (celular, cuenta de cheques, renta), and introduced no Spain-only words. Take off 2 for each slip they introduced. |
| **No new errors, no fact changes** | 10 | Start at 10. Take off 3 for each number, date or fact they changed instead of flagging, and 2 for each new grammar error. |
| **Notes** | 7 | A one-line reason on most changes, and a short "words this audience wouldn't use" list. |
| **Time and instructions** | 7 | Returned on time, wrote down start and end times, and left `{placeholders}` unchanged. |

## Decision

- **Hire for the lean set** at **70 or more**, if they caught **at least 9 of the 12** and changed **no facts**.
- **55–69:** a maybe. Hire only if no one better applies, and read their first 2 hours of lean work yourself.
- **Under 55, or 2 or more fact changes:** thank them and pay. Don't hire.

## Price the lean set from their speed

- **Their speed** = 1,913 words ÷ hours taken (from their start and end times).
- **Lean set hours** = 14,383 words ÷ their speed. The lean packet already counts each repeated line once.
- **Quote** = lean hours × their rate, capped at $500.

Example: if they took 1.6 hours, that's about 1,200 words an hour, so about 12 hours for the lean set. At $22 an
hour, that's about $264.
