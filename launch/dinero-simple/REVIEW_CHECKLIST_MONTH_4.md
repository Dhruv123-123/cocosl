## Shorts (videos/01–14)

### 01. Sí puedes tener crédito con ITIN
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Bureaus can maintain a credit file under an ITIN; lenders' ITIN acceptance varies
- [ ] Secured cards and credit-builder loans that accept ITIN exist, mostly at credit unions and CDFIs

### 02. La tarjeta asegurada, explicada en 30 segundos
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Secured card deposit usually equals the limit and is refundable when closed in good standing or upgraded

### 03. El préstamo que te da el dinero al final
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Credit-builder loan funds held in savings until paid; payments reported; offered by many credit unions/CDFIs

### 04. Usuario autorizado: el atajo, con cuidado
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Authorized-user history often appears on the AU's report; issuers' reporting practices vary; negative history can also affect the AU

### 05. Tu renta puede contar para tu crédito
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Rent reporting exists via landlords and third-party services; some charge fees; not all scoring models use rent data (e.g., FICO 8 does not; newer models do)

### 06. Un error en tu reporte se corrige gratis
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Disputes are free; bureaus generally must investigate within 30 days (45 in some cases)
- [ ] Credit repair companies cannot do anything you can't do for free

### 07. Cuenta de banco sin Seguro Social
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Banks may accept ITIN/passport/foreign government ID under CIP rules; acceptance of matrícula varies by bank

### 08. Apaga el sobregiro de tu tarjeta de débito
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Reg E opt-in required for overdraft fees on ATM and one-time debit card transactions; consumer can revoke
- [ ] Typical overdraft fee around $35 (varies; some banks dropped them)

### 09. Lo que te cuesta cambiar tu cheque cada quincena
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Check-cashing fees vary by state and store; 3% is an illustrative example (some charge 1-10%)
- [ ] Math: $800 x 3% = $24; x 24 paychecks = $576

### 10. ¿Banco o cooperativa? La diferencia en 30 segundos
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] NCUA and FDIC insure up to $250,000 per depositor, per institution, per ownership category
- [ ] Credit unions are member-owned not-for-profits with field-of-membership rules

### 11. La prórroga te da tiempo para declarar, no para pagar
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Form 4868 automatic extension to Oct 15, 2027 to file, not to pay; payment due Apr 15, 2027
- [ ] No failure-to-file penalty if a refund is due

### 12. Si trabajas por tu cuenta, el 15 de abril pagas dos veces
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Estimated tax generally required if you expect to owe $1,000+; 2027 due dates Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15 2028
- [ ] 25-30% set-aside is a rule of thumb, not a rate

### 13. Tu puntaje no sabe cuánto ganas
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Income, savings, immigration status, own-report checks (soft inquiries) and debit use don't affect FICO scores

### 14. Una llamada para quitar el cargo mensual del banco
- [ ] Native Spanish read (screen, caption, narration)
- [ ] Monthly maintenance fees often waived with direct deposit or minimum balance (varies by bank)
