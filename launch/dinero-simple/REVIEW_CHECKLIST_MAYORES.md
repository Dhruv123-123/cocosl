# Review checklist: Estafas: guía para papás y abuelos

Nothing from `launch/dinero-simple/mayores/` is printed, shared or listed until every box is ticked. Edit `contenido.py`, never the PDFs, then run `python3 launch/dinero-simple/mayores/build_mayores.py --pdf`.

## Sign-off
- [ ] **Native-speaker review** of every page (machine-written Spanish). Check that "usted" reads warm, not stiff, for Mexican, Central American and Caribbean grandparents, and that words like giro, clave, envío and peaje are the ones they use.
- [ ] **ATTORNEY REVIEW:** a licensed immigration attorney or DOJ-accredited representative reads the items below, the week the guide goes out.
- [ ] **Read it aloud with one abuelo or abuela** before the first print. If they stumble on a sentence, shorten it.
- [ ] **Print test** at 100% on Letter paper: wallet cards line up front to back.
