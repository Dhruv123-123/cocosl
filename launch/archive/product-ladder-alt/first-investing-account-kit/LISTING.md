> - **The employer match,** with worked numbers, plus vesting and what to pick inside your plan.

## FAQ (for the listing)

**Is this financial advice?**
No. It explains how accounts and index funds work so you can make your own decisions. It doesn't know your situation and doesn't recommend any company, fund or product.

**Do you recommend a specific brokerage or fund?**
No, on purpose. The guide shows you what to check (fees, minimums, fund costs, automation, protection) so you can compare the providers available to you.

**Is it US-only?**
Yes. The account types, tax rules and limits are US-specific.

**Are the contribution limits current?**
They're the 2026 figures as announced by the IRS, marked "verify" so you check irs.gov before relying on them. Limits change every year.

**How much money do I need to start?**
The guide covers how to check minimums. Many providers let you open an account with no minimum, but check the provider and fund you choose.

**What format is it?**
A 17-page PDF. Instant download. Read on screen or print the worksheet and checklist pages.

**Is it a subscription?**
No. One payment, yours to keep.
