# Tiny Histories: 14-day launch posting plan

Window: Sat Nov 7 – Fri Nov 20, 2026. All posts at **17:00 America/New_York** (brand `post_times`).
Source: `content.json` (index `#n` = position in the list, 0-based). 8 long-form, 8 shorts, 8 carousels.

Rules this plan follows:
- **Max 1 post per platform per day** (cap from the brand file). YouTube gets 14 posts (8 long + 6 Shorts), Instagram gets 14 (8 carousels + 6 Reels). Every day has exactly one post on each platform.
- **Long-form leads.** It earns far more per view than Shorts and builds the watch hours YouTube monetization needs, so the launch front-loads 8 long videos (roughly every other day). That's well above the steady-state cadence (1 long, 5 shorts, 2 carousels per week); drop back to it from Day 15 and start cutting Shorts and carousels out of the long videos.
- **Shorts go to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels on the same day.** Carousels go to Instagram only. Long-form goes to YouTube only.
- **Pairing:** on long-form days, the Instagram carousel is on a related theme (stationery, drinks, kitchen, timekeeping) so the two feeds cross-promote. Mention the long video in the carousel caption's first comment ("full story on YouTube").
- Before each post: human review; check every item in `claims_to_verify` (items marked CONTESTED/LEGEND/UNPROVEN must stay worded as uncertain on screen); open each URL in `sources` (deep links were written without network access and must be confirmed live); add the brand AI disclosure; set the platform AI-label toggle where required. The long-form descriptions already include sources and the AI line.
- No product or affiliate links yet. The brand file lists "Tiny Histories: 50 Origin Stories (PDF)", but it has not been built. Don't link it until it exists.
