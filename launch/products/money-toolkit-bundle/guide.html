Which kit, and when

You don't have to do all five at once. Each kit has its own folder in your download with a guide (PDF) and a spreadsheet. Here's the order most people get the most from.

  1. The Bill Cut Kit · one evening
    Find every recurring charge, cut what you don't use, and lower the bills you keep with word-for-word scripts. It frees up money for everything that follows.
  2. The 20-Minute Budget · one evening, then 20 minutes a week
    Bills, savings and goals on autopilot; everything else is one weekly spending number. This is the engine the other kits run on.
  3. The Credit Report Audit Kit · about 90 minutes
    Pull all three reports free, find and dispute errors, freeze your credit. Do it before applying for any loan, card or apartment.
  4. The Debt-Free Date Planner · 45 minutes, then 5 minutes a month
    If you have debt: put a date on it, choose avalanche or snowball, and check whether a balance transfer or consolidation loan would help.
  5. Start Investing With $100 a Month · an hour to read, 30 minutes to set up
    Once high-interest debt is handled and you have a cushion: which account first, what to buy, fees, and automation.

Your first month

A 30-day plan using all five

About an hour a week. By day 30, your money runs on a system instead of willpower.

WeekDo thisKit
Week 1Audit every recurring charge. Cut, downgrade, and make two negotiation calls. Decide where the savings go.Bill Cut Kit
Week 2Set up the budget: bills, sinking funds, goals and your weekly number. Automate the transfers. Do your first 20-minute check-in.20-Minute Budget
Week 3Pull all three credit reports. Freeze your credit. Send disputes for any errors. Fill in the Utilization tab.Credit Report Audit Kit
Week 4If you have debt, enter it in the planner and set up the extra payment. If not, or once you have a cushion, open (or check) your retirement account and automate it.Debt-Free Date Planner or Start Investing
Day 30Monthly reset of the budget (20 minutes). Log your debt balance on the Progress tab. Put the next six-month bill audit in your calendar.All

How the spreadsheets connect

  • The Summary tab in the Bill Cut Tracker tells you how much per month you freed up. Put that into the Goals section of the 20-Minute Budget.
  • The 20-Minute Budget's extra debt payment is the number you type into Extra per month in the Debt-Free Date Planner. (The budget has a simple payoff comparison built in; the planner adds up to 10 debts, a custom order, transfer and consolidation checks, and progress tracking.)
  • When the Debt-Free Date Planner shows your last payoff, move that whole monthly amount into the investing kit's Growth tab and see what it becomes.
