# "Fill it with me": weekly hands-only clips

**What it is:** a 20–40 second overhead clip of real hands filling in one of our own Etsy printables (`content-factory/etsy/`, Letter or A4 PDFs) with a pen. Boxes get ticked, and the running total adds up on screen. It ends with "The sheet is in my shop. Link in bio." It shows the product being used, and real hands and real paper keep it a human recording. That matters for TikTok's rule that fully AI-generated videos don't earn Creator Rewards. The idea comes from scouting's faceless-accounts research (`research/scouting/faceless-accounts-ig-tiktok.md`, PR #2). That research mentions Baddies & Budgets, whose cash-stuffing videos grew a supplies business (reported by CNBC in 2023; we haven't checked the figures ourselves). These clips don't show cash. They're a pen, a printout and example numbers.

**Who:** this needs Dhruv, or anyone with a printer, a pen and a phone. Add the clips to the recording-session prep sheets (`content-factory/recording-sessions/`), not to the render queue. Eight clips take about 45 minutes in one sitting.

## Rules for every clip

- **Example numbers only.** Write the fictional numbers listed below, never a real balance. Don't show bank apps, statements or anything with a name on it. The caption says "example numbers".
- **Only film sheets that are live in the shop.** Print the Letter PDF from each listing folder in `content-factory/etsy/`. Hold a clip until its Etsy listing is published, so the link in bio works.
- **No results claims.** Say "this is how the sheet works", not "this sheet will get you out of debt". No income or savings promises. The guardrail word list applies to the captions too.
- **Product disclosure:** every caption ends "Our own product: we make money if you buy it." Turn on TikTok's commercial content toggle ("Your brand").
- **The AI label isn't needed** for a clip with no AI visuals or voice. If AI text-to-speech is added over it, turn the label on and keep the caption line "Voice made with AI."
- **Platform:** TikTok only, at 12:30 on the posting day. Instagram, YouTube and Facebook are already at the 2-a-day cap, but TikTok has a spare slot on Thursdays. Check the combined calendar first, because the Dec 31 savings-challenge finale is also a Thursday, so that week's clip moves to Fri Jan 1.

## Filming setup (5 minutes)

- Phone held straight down over a table near a window: vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Use a phone arm or two stacks of books with a ruler across them.
- Plain table, the printed sheet taped flat, one dark felt-tip pen. Sleeves rolled up; no rings, watches or nail details that could identify you.
- Film each clip in one take, writing at a normal speed. Speed it up 2–4× in the edit so it lands at 20–40 s.
- Add text in the edit: a hook in the first second, the running total after each tick, and the end card. Use trending audio at low volume, or no audio at all. Don't use music we don't have rights to.

## After eight weeks

Compare these clips with the same weeks' AI-voiced TikTok posts on three numbers: average watch time, saves, and link-in-bio clicks (from the link page's analytics). If the hands-only clips win on two of the three, keep the weekly slot through spring and rotate in the Google Sheets and Notion versions from `content-factory/templates/` as screen recordings. If they don't, drop to one clip a month, filmed whenever a new sheet is launched.
