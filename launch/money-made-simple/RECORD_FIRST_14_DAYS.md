## 1 · AnnualCreditReport.com walkthrough (12 min)

**Never show personal data.** Don't type a real name, SSN, date of birth or address, and don't sign in. Stop at the first form.

- **Setup:** a private browser window at 1920×1080 with zoom at 125%. Bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off (Do Not Disturb). Use macOS Shift-Cmd-5 → Record Selected Portion, or OBS, or the Windows Game Bar (Win-G). 30 fps.
- **Shots,** pausing about 2 seconds on each so the editor has room:
  1. Type `annualcreditreport.com` into the address bar and load the home page. This shows it's the official site, typed rather than found through an ad.
  2. Hover over the "Request your free credit reports" button. Click it.
  3. Scroll slowly through the first form page. Don't fill it in. Hover over the fields so the viewer sees what's asked.
  4. Go back to the home page. Scroll to where it explains reports from all three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion).
- **Length:** about 60–90 seconds raw. It plays under the narration "Go to AnnualCreditReport.com, the official free site, and get all three. Check four things…" (month-1 pack, `videos-need-voice/11-credit-scores-explained-from-zero`, beat 8).
- **Where the account list goes:** don't record a real report. Over the "Check four things" part, the editor uses the on-screen text card ("AnnualCreditReport.com: all three") and the video's chart.
- **Save as:** `footage/money-made-simple/credit-scores-explained-beat8.mp4`. Then run `factory batch-record attach <session folder>`, or set the beat's footage by hand.

## 2 and 3 · "Fill it with me" clips 1 and 2 (14 min)

Follow `FILL_WITH_ME.md`. In short:

- **Setup:** the phone straight overhead near a window, filming vertically. A printed Letter PDF taped flat, and one dark felt-tip pen. Sleeves rolled up, no rings or watch.
- **Clip 1, Bill Payment Tracker:** write rent $1,400, phone $45, internet $60, car insurance $120 and streaming $16. Tick "paid" on 3 of them. The total is $1,641. Then do the second take for clip 4 (Dec 24) straight after: tick the last 2 bills.
- **Clip 2, Debt Payoff Coloring Tracker** (100-squares page): write a fictional $2,000 card at the top, then shade 10 squares. The balance goes from $2,000 to $1,800.
- **Only if the Etsy listing for that sheet is live.** If it isn't, film anyway and hold the clip until it is.
- **Example numbers only.** No real balances, statements or names.
