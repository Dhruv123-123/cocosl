# Money Basics: YouTube series 2 (12 parts)

This is the evergreen follow-up to the first 12-part series (launch/money-made-simple/youtube-series/). That series covered budgeting, debt, the emergency fund, credit, investing, IRAs, the 401(k) match, savings accounts, apps, card debt, sinking funds and credit freezes. Money Basics covers the other foundations: pay stubs, where money lives, interest, taxes, net worth, index funds, cars, insurance, statements, renting vs buying, negotiating, and a one-page plan. None of the topics repeat.

- **Parts 1–8 are written**, in `content.json`, at 8–10 minutes each. Each one has chapters, UTM-tagged links (`utm_campaign=yt-basics-NN`) and the AI and education lines in the description. Each also has a commission note wherever a /compare/ page is linked. Parts 9–12 are outlined below.
- **Evergreen:** no dated facts. Tax brackets in part 4 are simplified, round illustrative numbers, labelled that way, so the video doesn't go out of date.
- **Original element per video**, against YouTube's inauthentic-content risk: across the 12 parts that's 6 worked-example charts, 3 screen recordings and 3 human segments. The human segments need the host on camera (parts 2, 7 and 11).
- **Schedule:** Sundays at 19:00 ET, starting **Jun 13, 2027**, which fills the two empty month-7 Sundays (Jun 13 and 20), then weekly through Aug 29.
- **Examples** are fictional and labelled. There are no income promises or testimonials, and every investing mention carries a risk note.
