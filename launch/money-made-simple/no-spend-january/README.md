# No-spend January, the realistic version (TikTok challenge)

This is the second savings-challenge series, following "$300 by New Year's Eve" (`../savings-challenge/`). Both come from the scouting finding that challenge hooks get about 6 times the reach of other hooks on finance TikTok. It also matches Chime's FinTok survey, where "No Spend" and "Low Budget Living" are among the trends people try most.

**The idea:** a no-spend month doesn't mean spending $0. Bills, rent, groceries and gas still get paid, and only *unplanned* extras stop. There's one rule per Sunday, and a planned treat every week, so the month doesn't collapse on day nine.

Example maths: about $400 a month of unplanned extras, cut to $100 (one $25 treat a week), leaves $300 kept. Every caption says it's an example and not a promise.

**Lead-in:** month 2's Short "4 rules for a no-spend January that actually sticks" (Sat Dec 26) is the trailer. Pin day 1 on Jan 3, and link it in a pinned comment on each check-in.

**Pack:** `pack/` comes from `factory pack export --file content.json --start 2027-01-03 --days 29 --mid-days sun`. The videos are TikTok-only `mid` posts (65–90 s), so Instagram, YouTube and Facebook slots aren't touched. On TikTok, Sundays in January have no other post.

**Rules:** same as the first challenge. Numbers are examples, nobody's real results are faked, each video stands alone, and nothing is engagement bait. Human review before posting, keep the AI disclosure, and set TikTok's AI-generated label.
