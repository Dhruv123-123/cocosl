Hi,

Yesterday you listed your monthly bills. Today we find the ones that hide.

Most budgets break on bills that come once or twice a year: car registration, insurance paid every six months, the annual subscription, the dentist, birthdays, the holidays you just paid for. They aren't on the monthly list, so each one feels like an emergency.

**Today's task (15 minutes):**

1. Scroll back through last year's bank and card statements and list every bill or cost that wasn't monthly.
2. Write down what each cost and roughly when it came.
3. Add them up and **divide by 12**.

That monthly number is what you set aside, into a separate savings account or "bucket", so the money is there when the bill arrives. These are called sinking funds.

Example (fictional): car registration $180, car insurance $540 twice a year, Amazon Prime $139, a dental cleaning $120, gifts all year $900. Total $2,419 ÷ 12 ≈ **$202 a month**. In Sam's plan, that comes out of the $1,450 from yesterday, leaving $1,248.

Two quick wins while you're in the statements:

- **Cancel one subscription** you wouldn't sign up for again today at that price.
- **Note your renewal dates** in your calendar a week ahead, so you decide on purpose instead of auto-renewing.
