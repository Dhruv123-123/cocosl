Hi,

No debt? Skip to the credit check at the bottom. It's still worth the ten minutes.

**Part 1: one debt plan (10 minutes)**

Take the debt list from Day 1 and pick an order:

- **Highest interest rate first** (avalanche): saves the most money.
- **Smallest balance first** (snowball): quick wins that keep you going.

Both work. The plan you'll stick with is the right one. Then:

1. Pay the minimum on everything.
2. Put every extra dollar on the first debt in your order.
3. When it's gone, roll that whole payment onto the next one.

Example (fictional): Sam has $1,200 on a store card at 29% and $3,000 on a card at 22%. Sam goes highest rate first and puts $300 a month toward debt: the minimum on the 22% card and everything else on the 29% one. The store card is gone in about five months, then the full $300 goes to the second card.

Holiday balances on pay-later plans? List them too. They have fixed end dates, so they often clear first.

**Part 2: free credit check (10 minutes)**

Go to **AnnualCreditReport.com**, the official free site, and pull your reports. Look for accounts you don't recognise and errors in balances. Not applying for credit soon? A **credit freeze** with each bureau is free and blocks new accounts being opened in your name.
