Hi,

Today is quick and it can be worth a lot.

If you're paid every two weeks, you get **26 paychecks** this year, not 24. That means two months in 2027 will have three paydays instead of two. Most people don't plan for them, so the extra paychecks quietly disappear into normal spending.

**Today's task (15 minutes):**

1. Open a calendar and mark every 2027 payday. Use the date of your next paycheck and count forward two weeks at a time.
2. Find the two months with three paydays.
3. **Give each extra paycheck a job now**, before it arrives: the emergency fund, a debt, next December's holiday fund, or a trip.

Then set up one automatic transfer: a fixed amount into savings **on payday**, not at the end of the month when it's already gone. Even $25 a paycheck is $650 by December.

Example (fictional): Sam's paychecks are $1,570 each. Sam sends the first extra paycheck to the credit card and splits the second between the emergency fund and next year's holidays.

The **26-paycheck tracker** in the [2027 Budget Planner]({{LINK_2027_PLANNER}}) is one row per payday, with a column for where each one goes. Paid monthly or twice a month? Skip the extra-paycheck part and just set up the payday transfer.
