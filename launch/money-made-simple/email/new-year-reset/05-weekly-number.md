Last day. Today everything from this week becomes a plan you can run all year.

**Today's task (15 minutes):**

1. Start with take-home pay (Day 1).
2. Subtract fixed bills (Day 1), sinking funds (Day 2), the payday savings transfer (Day 3) and your debt payment (Day 4).
3. What's left is your spending money for food, fun and everything else. Turn it into a **weekly number**: monthly amount × 12 ÷ 52.

Example (fictional): Sam has $3,400 take-home. Minus $1,950 bills, $202 sinking funds, $54 savings ($25 a paycheck × 26 ÷ 12) and $300 debt leaves $894 a month. $894 × 12 ÷ 52 ≈ **$206 a week**. Groceries, takeout, a concert: all of it comes from $206. When it's gone, the week's done, and next week starts fresh.

Then **book a 20-minute check-in** on the same day every week. Check one thing: how much of this week's number is left. A bad week is a small problem. A bad month you only spot in February is a big one.
