Hi,

Happy New Year, and welcome to **the New Year Money Reset**: five short emails, January 1 to 5, one task a day, about 15 minutes each.

No resolutions, no "this is your year" speeches. Just five small jobs that leave you with a money plan you can actually run in 2027.

**Today: your money snapshot.** Open your banking app and write down four numbers:

1. **Take-home pay per month.** What actually lands in your account, not your salary. Paid every two weeks? One paycheck × 26 ÷ 12.
2. **Fixed bills per month.** Rent, utilities, phone, insurance, minimum debt payments, subscriptions.
3. **Total debt.** Every card, loan and buy-now-pay-later plan, with its interest rate.
4. **Total savings.** Everything in savings and checking that isn't already spoken for.

That's it. Don't fix anything yet. A real number is less scary than a vague one, and you can't plan around a vague one.

Example (fictional): Sam takes home $3,400 a month, has $1,950 of fixed bills, $4,200 of debt across two cards and $600 in savings. Sam now knows there's $1,450 a month to plan with. That's tomorrow's job.
