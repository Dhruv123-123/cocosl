# New Year Money Reset: 5-email mini-course

A newsletter opt-in for Money Made Simple. One email a day, **Fri Jan 1 – Tue Jan 5, 2027**, 07:00 America/New_York. Each one is a single task that takes about 15 minutes. After email 5, subscribers join the regular weekly newsletter.

- **Signup tag:** `source=new-year-reset`
- **Signup window:** the landing page can go live in late December. Anyone who signs up before Jan 1 gets email 1 on Jan 1. Anyone who signs up after that gets email 1 right away and the rest one a day after it.
- **Product tie-in:** the 2027 Budget Planner ($12, Etsy: `content-factory/etsy/2027-budget-planner/`). Each email maps to a planner page, and email 5 carries the main offer. The Sinking Funds Tracker, the Debt-Free Date Planner and The 20-Minute Budget get one mention each.
