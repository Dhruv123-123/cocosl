# $300 by New Year's Eve: a TikTok savings challenge

Scouting found that savings-challenge hooks get about 6 times the reach of other hook types on finance TikTok (Draper, May 2026; see `research/scouting/finance-formats-nov-2026.md` on PR #2). None of our packs had a challenge, so this is Money Made Simple's first.

**Shape:** a day-1 video with the whole plan, then one short move every Sunday, ending on New Year's Eve. The 8 videos are TikTok-only `mid` posts (65–90 s), so they don't take Instagram, YouTube or Facebook slots. Those platforms are already full on most days from Nov 21. On TikTok each one is the second post that day, which stays inside the 2-a-day cap.

| # | Date (19:00 ET) | Move | Example amount | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sun Nov 15 | Day 1: the plan, open a named savings bucket | $20 | $20 |
| 2 | Sun Nov 22 | Black Friday week: the 72-hour cart pause | $40 | $60 |
| 3 | Sun Nov 29 | Cancel 1 subscription, sell 1 thing | $50 | $110 |
| 4 | Sun Dec 6 | Pantry week: 5 dinners from what you have | $45 | $155 |
| 5 | Sun Dec 13 | Call 1 bill with a script | $35 | $190 |
| 6 | Sun Dec 20 | Set a gift cap | $40 | $230 |
| 7 | Sun Dec 27 | Returns, resales and gift cards | $50 | $280 |
| 8 | Thu Dec 31 | The round-up, plus what to do with the money | $20 | $300 |

**Packs:** `part-1` (weeks 1–7, `--start 2026-11-15 --days 43 --mid-days sun`) and `part-2` (the finale, `--start 2026-12-31 --days 1 --mid-days thu`). Both are in the project files under `savings-challenge/`.

**Rules (from the scouting brief):**
1. The numbers are an example plan, not a promise. Every caption says so, and none of them says "you will save".
2. There is no fake personal progress. We're faceless, so we show the plan's maths. Real viewer results are used only with their permission.
3. Each video stands alone: it opens with "New here?" and points to day 1. **Pin day 1** on the profile, and add a pinned comment linking it on every check-in.
4. The calls to action are "comment the week you're on" and "follow for next Sunday's move". There's no engagement bait and no DM-for-advice.

**Before posting:** human review as usual, keep the AI disclosure, and set TikTok's AI-generated label. If a later start date is needed, the same eight videos work as "$300 by Valentine's Day" with the dates in the hooks changed.

**Printable tracker (lead magnet idea):** a one-page sheet with "$300 by New Year's Eve" at the top and eight boxes labelled with the moves above. A product thread can turn this into a free PDF for the email list. The challenge doesn't depend on it.
