# Money Moves by Age: compliance review

A human reviewer ticks every line below for each part before it's uploaded. This is an education series, not advice, and it should read that way throughout. The repo's guardrails already block hype words, promises and testimonials; this list covers what they can't check.

## Every video

- [ ] **No specific securities, insurers or providers.** Products are named only as categories (target-date fund, 529 plan, HSA, term life).
- [ ] **Hypothetical returns are labelled** in the narration, on the chart (its note) and in the caption or description. No return is described as expected or likely.
- [ ] **Risk disclosure** is in the description.
- [ ] **Rules of thumb are called rules of thumb** (15% savings rate, 3–6 months of expenses, 4% withdrawal, ~8% debt line, 70–80% income replacement).
- [ ] **Tax and benefit rules** are checked against IRS.gov, SSA.gov and Medicare.gov in the month of posting. No yearly dollar limits appear on screen.
- [ ] **Not personalised advice.** The education line is in the description. Every example is labelled fictional.
- [ ] **Sources** in the description resolve and support the claims listed under `claims_to_verify`.
