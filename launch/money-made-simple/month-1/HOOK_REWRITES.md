# Month 1 Shorts: hook rewrites

The 20 weakest Shorts hooks in months 1–2, rewritten with templates from the hook library (`src/contentfactory/hooks_library.json`). All 20 turned out to be in month 1; month 2's hooks already lead with a number. Each item now has a `hook_template` field, and its first spoken line matches the new hook.

How "weakest" was scored: no number in the first five words (+2), a "Here's…" setup (+1), over 18 words (+1), vague words like "might" or "some" (+1), no number at all (+1). Rate My Budget episodes were left alone.

Every new hook only uses facts already in the script, so the script's sources and claims still cover it.

|---|---|---|---|
