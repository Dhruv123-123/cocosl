#launch-15 Credit freeze, the full walkthrough (3 bureaus + 2 bonus) · save as voice/scam-decoded/launch-15.wav

On screen: [voice] Read the whole TikTok cut straight through, in your own voice.

Three bureaus, two bonus freezes almost nobody does, and one for your kids. The big three are free by law.

Most identity theft that costs real money is a thief opening a new account in your name. A freeze means the lender can't see your report, so the application dies.

Go to Equifax, Experian and TransUnion directly. Make an account at each one and switch the freeze on. Save the logins in a password manager, because you'll need them later.

Say you apply for a car loan next spring. You log in to the bureau the dealer uses, lift the freeze for a day, and it relocks itself. Online lifts have to happen within an hour.

Bonus round. Innovis is a smaller fourth bureau, and ChexSystems tracks bank accounts. Both will freeze your file if you ask, which helps stop a thief opening a checking account in your name.

Second example: kids. A child's clean credit file is a prize for thieves, and nobody checks it for years. Parents can request a freeze for a child under sixteen at each bureau.

A freeze won't stop someone using a card you already have. For that, turn on transaction alerts with your bank. And check your reports free at AnnualCreditReport dot com.
