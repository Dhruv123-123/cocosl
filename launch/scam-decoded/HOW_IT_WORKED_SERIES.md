# How the scam actually worked: series plan

One real, documented scam per long video, told step by step from public records (DOJ, FTC, court filings), ending with the three checks that stop it. From the competitor teardown on PR #2 (idea 4). Every part has an original element: a case board we draw from the public record, plus Dhruv on camera with the source document, in his own voice.

Schedule: one part a month, in the first Sunday 10:00 long-video slot of the month, starting Jul 11 2027 (no weekly plan covers July yet). That keeps the one-long-video-a-week cap. Future weekly plans must leave these Sundays free. Nothing has been posted.

Rules for every part: say "alleged" for anything not proven in court, and say defendants are presumed innocent until convicted. Use only facts in the cited public record. No real victims' names or faces. Re-check every claim against its source the week it posts.
