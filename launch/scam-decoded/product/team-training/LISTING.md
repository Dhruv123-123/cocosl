## Full description

> Most scams that hit small businesses don't hack anything. They ask a person to pay an invoice, change bank details, buy gift cards or read out a code.
>
> Spot It, Stop It gives you everything to run a 45-minute training session yourself, no security background needed:
>
> - **25 editable slides** with speaker notes: the 3 tells, phishing, the fake boss, invoice and bank-change fraud, payroll diversion and W-2 requests, fake IT calls and code theft, AI voice clones and deepfake calls, QR codes, the verify rule, reporting and "I clicked, now what?"
> - **3 practice scenarios** with model answers
> - **A 12-question quiz** with answer key and explanations
> - **Completion certificates** (editable PowerPoint and printable PDF) and an attendance log
> - **A one-page payment and data verification policy** you can adopt as-is
> - **Follow-up emails** and a "Verify before you pay" poster
>
> Licensed for one organization, any number of employees. Rerun it every year and for new hires.
>
> *General education, not legal, compliance or financial advice. Training reduces risk; it can't remove it.*
