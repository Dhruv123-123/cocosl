## Rules

- One email and one follow-up per venue. Stop at "no" or no reply.
- Include a real postal address or P.O. box in your signature (CAN-SPAM covers commercial emails, even to organizations).
- Don't promise outcomes ("your members will never be scammed").
- Free talks: the pack mention is one slide at the end. Never sell during the talk.
