Before each talk:

- Check slide 3 against the latest FTC Consumer Sentinel Data Book and FBI IC3 Elder Fraud Report, and say this year's numbers out loud.

- Don't give advice on anyone's specific case. Point them to their bank, the Elder Fraud Hotline or local legal aid.
