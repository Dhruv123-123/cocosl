## Full description

> Scammers now clone voices, fake caller ID, and know your parents' address from data breaches. "It sounded just like him" isn't proof anymore.
>
> This kit turns one 15-minute family conversation into a plan everyone follows when a scary call comes in:
>
> - **The family meeting, scripted**: what to say, minute by minute, without making anyone feel gullible
> - **Family plan sheet**: safe word, the call-back rule, the payment rule, and your trusted person
> - **Credit freeze tracker**: all three bureaus plus the two bonus freezes, for every adult and child
> - **Account lockdown checklist**: five locks, one a night
> - **The Scam Decoder**: 12 common scams, the tell for each, and the move that beats it
> - **Fridge card + 4 wallet cards**: big type, made for parents and grandparents
> - **"It happened" plan**: the first 60 minutes, in order, plus an incident log banks and police will ask for
>
> Printable PDF, Letter size. Written for families, not tech experts.
>
> *General education, not legal or financial advice. No product can promise you'll never be scammed; this kit helps your family slow down and check.*
