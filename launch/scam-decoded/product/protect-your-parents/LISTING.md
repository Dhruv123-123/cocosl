## Full description

> Your parents answer the phone. Scammers know it.
>
> This guide walks you through three short visits that make your parents much harder to scam, without making them feel foolish:
>
> - **Visit 1, the conversation:** how to bring it up, what not to say, and answers to "I'd never fall for that"
> - **Visit 2, the setup:** 40+ checks across their phone, email, bank, credit and computer, in plain English (silence unknown callers, carrier PIN, bank alerts, a trusted contact, credit freezes, removing remote-access apps)
> - **Visit 3, practice:** 10 phone scripts your parent can read straight from the page, for the grandchild-in-trouble call, the "bank fraud department", Medicare, the IRS, tech support, utility shut-offs, prizes and online romance
> - **Fill-in sheets:** Family Plan, Important Numbers (with official numbers checked November 2026), and a monthly check-in tracker
> - **If it happens:** warning signs, the first hour, the calls to make together, and how to spot the "recovery" scam that follows
>
> Printable PDF, 20 pages, Letter size. Written for adult children, not tech experts.
>
> *General education, not legal or financial advice. No guide can promise someone will never be scammed; this one helps families slow down and check.*
