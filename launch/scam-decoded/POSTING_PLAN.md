# Scam Decoded: 14-day launch posting plan

All times America/New_York (brand `post_times`: 12:00 and 19:30). Source: `content.json` (`#n` = 0-based index).
14 Shorts (#0–13, 30–45s, Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts), 14 TikTok cuts (#14–27, 60–90s, TikTok only), 8 carousels (#28–35, Instagram).

Why two cuts: research/03-hooks-and-formats.md found TikToks over 60s break out about twice as often, while Reels are flat
across lengths. Each item in `content.json` names its `platforms`, so `factory schedule` never double-posts.

Rules this plan follows:
- Max 2 posts per platform per day. Short and its TikTok cut go out the same day (same topic, different cut).
- Every hook opens with a number (research: number openers 6.8% breakout on TikTok vs ~5% baseline).
- Don't delete or repost a TikTok after a slow first day; TikTok breakouts often start slow.
- Before each post: human review, tick every `CHECK BEFORE POSTING` line, confirm example messages contain no real
  phone numbers or domains, and turn on the platform AI-label toggle.
- Affiliate pieces: #1/#15 and #29 (Aura), #31 (Aura), #21 and #32 (1Password), #32 (NordVPN). Kit pieces: #3/#17, #30, #35.
  Make sure the link-in-bio page has real tracking links before these go out (README.md).
