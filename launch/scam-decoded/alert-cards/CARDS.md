### 2026-11-16: Gift cards drained before you even give them

Image: `2026-11-16-gift-card-drained-at-the-rack.png`

```
Scam alert: Gift cards drained before you even give them.

The giveaways: 1) A scratched, peeled or re-stuck PIN strip on the back. 2) A torn or resealed package, or a second barcode sticker on top of the real one. 3) The balance is gone before the person you gave it to ever used it.

What to do: Check the package and the PIN area before you pay. Pick one from the back of the rack.
```
