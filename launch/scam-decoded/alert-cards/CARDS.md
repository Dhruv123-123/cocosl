### 2026-11-16: Gift cards drained before you even give them

Image: `2026-11-16-gift-card-drained-at-the-rack.png`

```
Scam alert: Gift cards drained before you even give them.

The giveaways: 1) A scratched, peeled or re-stuck PIN strip on the back. 2) A torn or resealed package, or a second barcode sticker on top of the real one. 3) The balance is gone before the person you gave it to ever used it.

What to do: Check the package and the PIN area before you pay. Pick one from the back of the rack.

Source: FTC: Check out gift cards before you buy them (https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/12/check-out-gift-cards-you-buy-them). The example on the card is made up.
```

```
Scam alert: "Your $1,299 order is confirmed" emails with a number to call.
```

```
Scam alert: Marketplace "buyers" who ask for a code to prove you're real.
```

### 2027-01-12: "Unpaid toll" texts are back

Image: `2027-01-12-unpaid-toll-texts.png`

```
Scam alert: "Unpaid toll" texts are back.

The giveaways: 1) A small amount with a big threat attached: late fees, license suspension, today. 2) A link that isn't your toll agency's real website, often with an odd ending like .top or .xyz. 3) It arrives as a text from a number you don't know, sometimes even if you haven't driven a toll road.

What to do: Don't tap. Log in to your toll account by typing the agency's website yourself, or call the number on your statement.

Source: FTC: Got a text about unpaid tolls? It's probably a scam (https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/01/got-text-about-unpaid-tolls-its-probably-scam). The example on the card is made up.
```
