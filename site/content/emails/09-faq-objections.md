Hi,

Here are straight answers to the questions people usually have before buying a budgeting template like [The 20-Minute Budget](/products/the-20-minute-budget/).

**"I've quit every budget I've tried."**
That's who it's designed for. You don't track categories. You check one weekly number for 20 minutes a week. The guide covers what to do after a bad week, because there will be bad weeks.

**"I'm paid every two weeks / my income changes."**
The Setup tab converts weekly, biweekly or twice-monthly pay. The guide has a section on irregular income: budget from your lowest normal month and use a buffer fund in good months.

**"I don't have Excel."**
It works in Google Sheets (free) and Apple Numbers.

**"Does it connect to my bank?"**
No, on purpose. Nothing to sync, nothing to break, no account access. If you want automatic syncing, a budgeting app is the better choice, and I'd rather you buy the right tool.

**"I'm in debt. Is this the right first step?"**
The debt payoff tab plans up to six debts with avalanche or snowball and shows your debt-free date. If minimum payments themselves feel unmanageable, a nonprofit credit counsellor (an NFCC member agency in the US) is a good next step, and it's often free or low-cost.

**"What if it's not for me?"**
Email within 14 days and you get a full refund.
