SoFi Checking and Savings is a combined checking and savings account from SoFi Bank, N.A. It's popular because it pairs no account fees with a savings rate well above most big banks. The thing to understand before you open it is **what you need to do to get the top rate**.

*How we wrote this: based on SoFi's published terms and fee schedule, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this overview and haven't tested the account. Rates and conditions change; read SoFi's current terms.*

## The short version

| | |
|---|---|
| Who holds the money | SoFi Bank, N.A., Member FDIC (a chartered bank, not a middleman app) |
| Monthly fees | None |
| Minimum balance | None |
| Top savings rate | Has required direct deposit or a minimum amount of qualifying deposits each month |
| Extra insurance | A sweep program spreads money across partner banks for coverage above $250,000 (check the current limit) |
| Branches | None (online and app) |

**Best for:** people who can send a paycheck by direct deposit and want checking and savings in one app.
**Not for:** people who can't meet the deposit condition (the base rate is much lower), or who want a branch.

## What's good

- **It's a bank.** SoFi has its own bank charter, so your deposits are held by an FDIC-insured bank directly. That avoids the "pass-through" risk some fintech apps have. [Why that matters](/blog/is-my-savings-app-fdic-insured/).
- **No account fees and no minimums.**
- **Vaults.** You can split savings into named pots, handy for [sinking funds](/blog/sinking-funds-explained/).
- **Early paycheck.** Direct deposits can arrive up to two days early, depending on when your employer sends them.

## What to check

- **The rate conditions.** Read the current requirement for the top APY. If you can't meet it, compare the base rate with other banks.
- **Promotions.** SoFi often runs sign-up bonuses tied to direct deposit amounts. Read the full terms and the deadline before counting on one.
- **Cash deposits.** Depositing cash at retail locations can carry a third-party fee.
- **It's one more app.** If you already like your checking account, you can just open a savings-only account elsewhere.

## Our verdict

If you can point a paycheck at it, [SoFi Checking and Savings](/go/sofi/) is a strong, fee-free all-in-one account from a chartered bank. If you can't meet the deposit condition, a bank with a single flat rate is simpler.

## Common questions

### Is SoFi a real bank?
Yes. SoFi Bank, N.A. is a nationally chartered bank and a member of the FDIC. You can confirm it in FDIC BankFind.

### Does SoFi have fees?
SoFi Checking and Savings has no monthly account fees. Some services, like cash deposits at retailers, can involve third-party fees.

### Do I need direct deposit for SoFi?
Not to open the account. The top savings rate has depended on direct deposit or qualifying deposits, so check the current terms.
