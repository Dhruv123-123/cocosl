If you have no credit history, it's hard to get the credit that builds one. Self is one way around that: it's a **credit-builder loan**. You make monthly payments into a savings account you can't touch yet, Self reports those payments to the credit bureaus, and at the end you get the money back, minus fees and interest.

## How it works

1. You pick a plan: a monthly payment (Self's plans have ranged from about $25 to $150 a month) for a set term, often 12–24 months.
2. The loan amount is held in a certificate of deposit at a partner bank. You don't get it upfront.
3. Each on-time payment is reported to the three credit bureaus.
4. When the term ends, the CD is released to you, minus interest you've paid. You can usually close early and get back what you've paid in, minus fees and interest.

## What it costs

There's usually a **one-time administrative fee** and an **interest rate (APR)** on the loan. The total cost over the term is often somewhere in the tens of dollars to around $100, depending on the plan. Look at the "total of payments" versus what you get back: the difference is your cost to build credit.

## Who it helps

- People with **no credit history** who can't get approved for a card.
- People rebuilding after missed payments who want a payment history that's on time.
- People who'd benefit from **forced saving** as a side effect.

## Who should skip it

- **Anyone who might miss a payment.** A late payment gets reported too, and it can hurt the score you're trying to build. Only pick a monthly amount you're sure you can pay.
- People who can get a **secured credit card**, which builds credit at little or no cost if you pay it in full.

## Our verdict

[Self](aff:self) can be a useful first step if you have no credit and can't get a secured card, and if you're certain you can make every payment. Read the total cost, pick the smallest plan that works, and check whether a secured card or credit union loan would do the same job for less.

## Common questions

### Does Self actually build credit?
It reports payments to the credit bureaus, so on-time payments add positive history. Results vary by person, and missed payments hurt.

### Can I get my money back from Self?
At the end of the term you get the savings, minus interest and fees. Read the early-closure terms if you might need it sooner.

### Self or a secured credit card?
If you can get a secured card and will pay it in full each month, it's usually cheaper. Self is an option when you can't.
