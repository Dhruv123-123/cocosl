Rocket Money is a money app best known for one job: finding the subscriptions you forgot about and helping you cancel them. It also does budgeting, spending alerts and bill negotiation. Here's what's free, what costs money, and whether you need it.

*How we review: we haven't been paid for this review and we didn't test every feature hands-on. It's based on Rocket Money's published features and pricing, checked in November 2026. Prices change, so check the app before paying.*

Comparing apps? See **[Rocket Money vs YNAB vs Monarch](/compare/rocket-money-vs-ynab-vs-monarch/)**. Only interested in lowering bills? See **[bill negotiation services vs doing it yourself](/compare/best-bill-negotiation-services/)**.

## The short version

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | Choose your own price, roughly $7–$14 a month (billed monthly or yearly) |
| Finds recurring charges | Yes | Yes |
| Cancels subscriptions for you | No (you cancel yourself) | Yes, a "concierge" does it |
| Budgets and categories | Basic | Unlimited budgets, custom categories |
| Bill negotiation | Available, for a fee | Available, for a fee |
| Credit score and reports | Basic | More detail |

**Best for:** people who suspect they're paying for things they don't use and want it handled in one place.
**Not for:** people with a handful of subscriptions who'd rather spend 20 minutes doing it by hand.

## What it does well

- **Subscription detection.** Link your accounts and it lists every recurring charge it finds, with how much each costs a year. Seeing "$186/year" next to an app you forgot about is the whole point.
- **Cancellation help.** With Premium, the team cancels some subscriptions on your behalf. You still need to check each one actually stopped.
- **Alerts.** Low balance, large purchases and upcoming bills, which can help you avoid overdraft fees.

## What to watch out for

- **Bill negotiation costs a share of the savings.** If Rocket Money lowers a bill (internet, phone), it charges a percentage of the first year's savings, which has been in the 35%–60% range, and you choose the percentage. It's charged up front even though the savings arrive monthly. You can often get the same discount yourself with a 15-minute call.
- **Premium is a subscription to cancel subscriptions.** If you'll cancel three things and stop using the app, the free version plus a calendar reminder may be all you need.
- **Bank connections.** Like any linked app, connections can drop, and you're sharing account data with another company. Read what they collect.

## The free alternative: do it yourself in 20 minutes

1. Open your last two months of bank and card statements.
2. Highlight anything that repeats.
3. Cancel what you don't use, and set a reminder for anything on a free trial.

That's the method in our [subscription audit guide](/blog/find-and-cancel-subscriptions/). If you'd rather the app do the searching, the free version of [Rocket Money](/go/rocket-money/) covers the finding part.

## Rocket Money vs other budgeting apps

Rocket Money is strongest at subscriptions and alerts. If budgeting is the main job, [Monarch](/blog/monarch-money-review/) and [YNAB](/blog/ynab-review/) are built more around planning. See our [budgeting apps comparison](/compare/best-budgeting-apps/).

## Our verdict

Start with the free version. Let it find your recurring charges, cancel the ones you don't need yourself, and see how much it saved you. Only pay for Premium if you want the concierge to handle cancellations or you'll actually use the budgeting features every week.

## Common questions

### Is Rocket Money safe?
It uses read-only connections through data aggregators and says it uses bank-level encryption. As with any app, use a strong, unique password and two-factor login.

### Is Rocket Money free?
The core features are free. Premium is optional and you pick the monthly price within a range.

### Can I cancel Rocket Money Premium?
Yes, in the app's settings or through the app store you subscribed through. Check the renewal date so you aren't billed for another period.

### Does Rocket Money hurt your credit score?
Checking your own score inside the app is a soft inquiry, which doesn't affect your score.
