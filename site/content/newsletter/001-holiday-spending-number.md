Hi there,

This week's money move takes about five minutes: **decide one number for everything the holidays will cost you, before the sales start.**

Not a gift budget. A *holidays* budget: presents, travel, the extra food, the secret-santa you forgot about, the new outfit for the party. One number.

## Why one number works

Sales are designed to make each purchase look small and sensible on its own. A total you picked in advance is the only thing that sees all of them together. If you've ever looked at a January card statement and wondered how it happened, this is how it happened: thirty sensible purchases.

## Do it tonight

1. **List the people and the events.** Names, trips, dinners. Write them down, rough is fine.
2. **Put a number next to each.** Then add them up.
3. **Check it against what you actually have.** Look at your savings and at what's left in your monthly plan after bills. If the total doesn't fit, shrink the list now, while it's a list and not a stack of receipts.
4. **Move the money.** Put the whole amount in a separate savings "bucket" (most banks let you name one) and spend from there. When it's gone, the holidays are paid for.

If you're short this year, that's useful information too. Start a monthly sinking fund for next year: total divided by 12. The [sinking funds guide](/blog/sinking-funds-explained/) walks through it.

## One thing to skip

Buy-now-pay-later at checkout. Splitting $200 into four payments doesn't make it cheaper, it makes it harder to see. If something only fits your number in installments, it doesn't fit your number.
