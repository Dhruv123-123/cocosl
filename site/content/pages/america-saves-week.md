**America Saves Week 2027 runs from Monday, April 5 to Friday, April 9.** It's an annual campaign, held every year since 2007, that America Saves describes as "an annual celebration as well as a call to action for everyday Americans": a week to review your finances, pick a savings target and map out how to reach it.

You don't need to sign up for anything to take part. Below is a five-day plan with one task a day, each taking about 15 minutes, and the guides that go with it.

## The five-day plan

### Monday, April 5: set one goal
Pick one thing to save for, put a number and a date on it, and work out the monthly amount. If you have no savings yet, start with a small emergency fund. Guide: [How to set a savings goal](/blog/how-to-set-a-savings-goal/).

### Tuesday, April 6: make it automatic
Set up a payday transfer or split your direct deposit, so saving happens before you can spend the money. Guide: [How to automate your savings](/blog/how-to-automate-your-savings/).

### Wednesday, April 7: build a buffer
Open or name a separate savings account for emergencies at an insured bank or credit union. Guides: [How to build an emergency fund](/blog/how-to-build-an-emergency-fund/) and [How much emergency fund do I need?](/blog/how-much-emergency-fund-do-i-need/).

### Thursday, April 8: give every goal a home
Sort your goals into short, medium and long term, and decide where each one's money should sit. Guide: [Short-term vs long-term savings goals](/blog/short-term-vs-long-term-savings-goals/).

### Friday, April 9: check your future self
Look at your retirement savings: are you getting your full employer match, and is your contribution set to rise? Guides: [The 401(k) match, explained](/blog/401k-match-explained/) and [Retirement savings in your 20s](/blog/retirement-savings-in-your-20s/).

## Common questions

### When is America Saves Week 2027?
America Saves Week 2027 runs from April 5 to April 9, 2027. It takes place every year in the spring.

### Who runs America Saves Week?
It's run by America Saves, a national campaign that encourages people to save and to set and reach savings goals. Its website, americasaves.org, has the official materials for the week.

### Do I have to sign up to take part?
No. Anyone can use the week as a prompt to set a goal and start saving. America Saves also offers a pledge on its website for people who want to commit to a goal and get encouragement.

### Is Money Made Simple part of America Saves?
No. This page is our own plan for the week, written to help readers take part. For the official campaign, materials and pledge, visit americasaves.org.
