Money Made Simple is for students and early-career workers who feel behind on money. Every guide here is one idea you can use this week, with the numbers shown.

**Not sure where to begin?** Take the 1-minute [money personality quiz](/quiz/), or start the free [7-Day Money Reset](/free/): seven 15-minute tasks that get your money organised in a week.

## "I don't know where my money goes"

1. Find your real take-home pay and fixed bills (Day 1 of the [free reset](/free/)).
2. [Find and cancel forgotten subscriptions](/blog/find-and-cancel-subscriptions/) in 10 minutes.
3. Set up [a budget you'll actually keep](/blog/how-to-make-a-budget-you-will-keep/), built on one weekly spending number.

Tools: [best budgeting apps](/compare/best-budgeting-apps/), [Rocket Money vs YNAB vs Monarch](/compare/rocket-money-vs-ynab-vs-monarch/), or [The 20-Minute Budget](/products/the-20-minute-budget/) spreadsheet.

## "Surprise bills keep wrecking me"

1. Build a [$1,000 starter emergency fund in 8 weeks](/blog/how-to-build-an-emergency-fund/).
2. Turn predictable costs into [sinking funds](/blog/sinking-funds-explained/).
3. Keep both in a [high-yield savings account](/compare/best-high-yield-savings-accounts/) that's actually insured: [how to check](/blog/is-my-savings-app-fdic-insured/).

## "I'm paying off debt"

1. See [how much faster avalanche or snowball gets you out](/blog/debt-avalanche-vs-snowball/), with the real maths.
2. Check whether a [balance transfer card](/compare/best-balance-transfer-cards/) would actually save you money.
3. Learn [how credit scores work](/blog/how-credit-scores-work/) so paying down debt also lifts your score.

Building credit from zero? [Credit-builder cards and tools compared](/compare/best-credit-builder-cards/).

## "I want to start investing"

1. Claim your [401(k) match](/blog/401k-match-explained/) first, if you have one.
2. Pick [Roth or Traditional IRA](/blog/roth-vs-traditional-ira/) (it mostly comes down to one question).
3. Follow [how to start investing with $100 a month](/blog/how-to-start-investing-with-100-a-month/), and check every fund's [expense ratio](/blog/expense-ratios-explained/).

Want it automated? [Robo-advisors compared](/compare/best-robo-advisors/), including the cheaper do-it-yourself option.

## "I want to protect myself from fraud"

1. [Freeze your credit for free](/blog/how-to-freeze-your-credit/) at all three bureaus, and check your reports.
2. Make sure your [savings app is really insured](/blog/is-my-savings-app-fdic-insured/).
3. Decide whether [paid identity theft protection](/compare/best-identity-theft-protection/) adds anything for you.
