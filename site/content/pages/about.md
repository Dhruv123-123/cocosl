Money Made Simple is for students and early-career workers who feel behind on money. Maybe friends seem to have savings and a plan, and you get a small wave of dread every time you open your banking app. That's common, and it's fixable.

We don't do overhauls. We do **one money move a week**: one specific thing you can do in under 30 minutes, explained clearly, with the numbers shown.

## What we cover

- **Budgeting that sticks:** weekly spending numbers, sinking funds, a 20-minute check-in.
- **First-time investing:** index funds, Roth vs Traditional IRAs, the 401(k) match, fees.
- **Credit and debt payoff:** how scores work, credit reports and freezes, avalanche vs snowball.
- **Money apps and tools:** honest comparisons, including when you don't need an app at all.

## How it's made

We use AI tools to help with visuals, voice and first drafts. A human chooses every topic, checks every number against primary sources (the IRS, CFPB, FDIC, FTC and Investor.gov) and approves each article, video and email before it goes out. Every article lists its sources.

## What we won't do

- Pick individual stocks or promote crypto.
- Promise returns or income, or show "I made $X" screenshots.
- Publish fake reviews or AI testimonials. If we ever quote a buyer, it's a real person, word for word, with permission.
- Hide affiliate links. They're always labelled. See [Disclosures](/disclosures/).

## Not financial advice

Everything here is general education. Your situation is yours, and rules change. For decisions about your specific taxes, investments or debts, talk to a qualified professional, such as a fee-only fiduciary adviser or a nonprofit credit counsellor.
