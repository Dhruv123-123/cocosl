Debt feels endless when there's no finish line. This planner gives it one: a month and a year, worked out from your real balances, rates and payments.

Type in your debts and your extra monthly payment, choose **avalanche** (highest rate first, least interest), **snowball** (smallest balance first, quickest wins) or your own **custom** order. Each paid-off minimum rolls into the next debt automatically, and you see the payoff month for every debt, your debt-free date, total interest, and what you save compared with paying minimums only.

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |

| Plan | Up to 10 debts, method switch, payoff month per debt, debt-free date, minimums-only comparison |

| Consolidation | Would one fixed-rate loan (with its fee) beat your current plan |
| Progress | Log your real balance monthly, with a chart of the plan |

**The guide (PDF, 10 pages):** a worked example with real numbers, how to choose a method, what $100, $200 or $400 extra a month actually does, a script for asking for a lower APR, automation, honest guidance on when a plan isn't enough, and a printable payoff tracker.

## FAQ

### I have the 20-Minute Budget. Is this different?
Yes. The budget compares avalanche and snowball for up to 6 debts. The planner adds a custom order, up to 10 debts, a minimums-only comparison, transfer and consolidation calculators, progress tracking and a much deeper guide.

### How accurate is it?
It uses monthly interest (APR / 12). Lenders calculate daily, so expect small differences.
