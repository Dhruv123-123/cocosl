Subscriptions don't feel expensive at $9.99 a month. They feel expensive at $120 a year, times six, for things you forgot you had.

**The Bill Cut Kit** is a one-evening audit. You check the five places recurring charges hide (including app-store charges that never show up on your bank statement), make one decision per charge (keep, cut, downgrade or negotiate) and use word-for-word scripts to ask for a lower price on the bills you keep.

## What's inside

**The guide (PDF, 9 pages):** where to look, the four-decision rule, cancelling so it stays cancelled, the free-trial habit, call scripts for internet, phone, car and renters insurance, credit card APR, late and annual fees, bank fees and gyms, and a printable worksheet.

**The tracker (.xlsx):** converts every charge to a yearly cost, adds up what each decision saves, logs every negotiation call and reminds you two weeks before a promo price ends.
