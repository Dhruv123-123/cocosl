Cost of living budget in Philadelphia

A modest one-bedroom in Philadelphia costs about $1,558 a month with utilities, the 13th highest of the 25 largest US cities. To keep it at 30% of your pay you'd earn about $63,000 a year. Here's what different salaries leave you with after PA taxes and rent.

Check your take-home pay in Philadelphia

Rent in Philadelphia

HUD's fair market rent for the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD area is $1,558 for a one-bedroom and $1,860 for a two-bedroom ($930 each if you split it). Fair market rent includes utilities and sits at the 40th percentile of rents for a standard unit: a realistic "modest apartment" number, not a luxury listing and not a bargain.

Common questions

How much do you need to make to live in Philadelphia?

To keep a fair-market one-bedroom ($1,558 a month) at 30% of gross pay, about $63,000 a year. Sharing a two-bedroom brings that down to about $38,000. Those are rent-based rules of thumb; your other costs decide the rest.

What is the fair market rent in Philadelphia?

HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD area is $1,558 a month for a one-bedroom and $1,860 for a two-bedroom, including utilities. It's set at the 40th percentile of rents for a standard unit, so many listings will be higher, especially in popular neighbourhoods.

Does Philadelphia have a state or city income tax?

Yes. On a $60,000 salary, Pennsylvania income tax is about $1,842 a year. There's also the Philadelphia Wage Tax (resident), about $2,241 a year at that salary.

How much is $60,000 after taxes in Philadelphia?

About $46,307 a year, or $3,859 a month, for a single filer in 2026. After a fair-market one-bedroom that leaves $2,301 a month for everything else.
