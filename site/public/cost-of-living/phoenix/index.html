Cost of living budget in Phoenix

A modest one-bedroom in Phoenix costs about $1,493 a month with utilities, the 16th highest of the 25 largest US cities. To keep it at 30% of your pay you'd earn about $60,000 a year. Here's what different salaries leave you with after AZ taxes and rent.

Check your take-home pay in Phoenix

Rent in Phoenix

HUD's fair market rent for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler, AZ area is $1,493 for a one-bedroom and $1,734 for a two-bedroom ($867 each if you split it). Fair market rent includes utilities and sits at the 40th percentile of rents for a standard unit: a realistic "modest apartment" number, not a luxury listing and not a bargain.

Common questions

How much do you need to make to live in Phoenix?

To keep a fair-market one-bedroom ($1,493 a month) at 30% of gross pay, about $60,000 a year. Sharing a two-bedroom brings that down to about $35,000. Those are rent-based rules of thumb; your other costs decide the rest.

What is the fair market rent in Phoenix?

HUD's FY2027 fair market rent for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler, AZ area is $1,493 a month for a one-bedroom and $1,734 for a two-bedroom, including utilities. It's set at the 40th percentile of rents for a standard unit, so many listings will be higher, especially in popular neighbourhoods.

Does Phoenix have a state or city income tax?

Yes. On a $60,000 salary, Arizona income tax is about $1,291 a year.

How much is $60,000 after taxes in Phoenix?

About $49,099 a year, or $4,092 a month, for a single filer in 2026. After a fair-market one-bedroom that leaves $2,599 a month for everything else.
