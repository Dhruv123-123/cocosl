  1. Our own products, like The 20-Minute Budget. You pay once; there's a 14-day no-questions refund.

A commission never decides what we say. We only link to products we would describe the same way without the commission, and we point out who a product is not for. The full plain-words version, including which affiliate programs are live right now, is on How we make money.
