Debt payoff timeline for $50,000 of debt

Paying $1,766 a month, a hypothetical $50,000 of debt is gone in 2 years 8 months with the avalanche method and 2 years 8 months with the snowball. Paying just the minimums (rolling each paid-off minimum into the next debt) takes 5 years 6 months. Here's the month-by-month maths.

Ways to speed it up

The fastest lever is the interest rate on the cards. Moving $12,500 of card debt to a 0% balance transfer card would stop most of that interest for the intro period (watch the transfer fee and the rate after it ends): see balance transfer cards compared. Or ask your card issuer for a lower APR: it's one phone call. Then find money for the extra payment in the budget for your salary.

Common questions

How long does it take to pay off $50,000 of debt?

With the debts on this page and $1,766 a month (minimums plus $750 extra), about 2 years 8 months using avalanche, paying $6,278 in interest. Paying only the minimums it takes 5 years 6 months and costs $16,672.

How much do I need to pay a month to clear $50,000 in three years?

About $1,591 a month in total ($575 on top of the $1,016 of minimums) using avalanche. To be debt-free in two years it's about $2,285 a month.

Snowball or avalanche for $50,000 of debt?

Avalanche saves $110 in interest. Snowball's first win comes in month 6 instead of month 9. If that early win is what keeps you going, it can be worth $110; if you'll stick with it anyway, avalanche is cheaper.

Are these my real numbers?

No. They're a hypothetical mix of debts with typical-looking rates, to show how the maths works. Put your own balances, rates and minimums into the debt payoff calculator for your plan.
