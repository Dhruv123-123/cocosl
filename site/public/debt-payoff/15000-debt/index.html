Debt payoff timeline for $15,000 of debt

Paying $731 a month, a hypothetical $15,000 of debt is gone in 2 years 1 month with the avalanche method and 2 years 1 month with the snowball. Paying just the minimums (rolling each paid-off minimum into the next debt) takes 4 years 5 months. Here's the month-by-month maths.

Ways to speed it up

The fastest lever is the interest rate on the cards. Moving $8,250 of card debt to a 0% balance transfer card would stop most of that interest for the intro period (watch the transfer fee and the rate after it ends): see balance transfer cards compared. Or ask your card issuer for a lower APR: it's one phone call. Then find money for the extra payment in the budget for your salary.

Common questions

How long does it take to pay off $15,000 of debt?

With the debts on this page and $731 a month (minimums plus $300 extra), about 2 years 1 month using avalanche, paying $2,931 in interest. Paying only the minimums it takes 4 years 5 months and costs $7,454.

How much do I need to pay a month to clear $15,000 in three years?

About $548 a month in total ($117 on top of the $431 of minimums) using avalanche. To be debt-free in two years it's about $752 a month.

Snowball or avalanche for $15,000 of debt?

Avalanche saves $74 in interest. Both methods clear their first debt in month 3, so snowball's usual head start doesn't apply here: avalanche is the better pick for this mix.

Are these my real numbers?

No. They're a hypothetical mix of debts with typical-looking rates, to show how the maths works. Put your own balances, rates and minimums into the debt payoff calculator for your plan.
