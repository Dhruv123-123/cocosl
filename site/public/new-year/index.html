What's in the five emails

Day 1: Your 2027 money snapshot

Four numbers on one page: take-home pay, fixed bills, total debt and savings. No judgement, just the starting point every other day builds on.

Day 2: The bills that aren't on your list yet

Car registration, renewals, gifts: last year's non-monthly bills, each divided by 12 so they stop being surprises. Plus one subscription to cancel.

Day 3: The 3-paycheck months

Paid every two weeks? That's 26 paychecks this year, so two months have three paydays. You'll mark them and give each extra paycheck a job before it arrives, then set up an automatic payday transfer.

Day 4: Your debt plan and a free credit check

Pick avalanche or snowball and set a target date. Then pull your free credit reports and decide whether a credit freeze makes sense for you.

Day 5: Your weekly number

Bills, funds and goals on autopilot, and one weekly spending number to watch, with a 20-minute weekly check-in to keep it running.

How it works

The emails go out at 7am Eastern, one a day from Friday, January 1 to Tuesday, January 5, 2027. Each one is a single task that takes about 15 minutes. By January 5 you have a money plan you can actually run in 2027.

You don't need an app: a notes app or a spreadsheet is enough. After Day 5 you'll get the regular weekly Money Made Simple email, and you can unsubscribe in one click at any time.

Common questions

Is the New Year money reset free?

Yes. It's five emails and you can unsubscribe in one click. After the course you'll get the weekly Money Made Simple email, which you can also stop at any time.

When does it start?

Day 1 goes out on January 1, 2027. If you sign up after that, Day 1 arrives right away and the other four follow one a day.

What if I'm already behind on bills?

Start anyway: Day 1 is just the real numbers, and Day 4 is about which debt to tackle first. If you can't cover everything this month, the CFPB's free worksheet on prioritizing bills (linked below) shows what to pay first.

Do I need to link my bank account to anything?

No. Nothing in the course asks you to connect an account or share login details.
