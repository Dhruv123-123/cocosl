Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Short version: if you join the email list, we keep your email address so we can send you the emails you asked for. We don't sell it. You can unsubscribe in one click from any email, and ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your email address (and the page you signed up from), when you sign up for the free reset or the newsletter.
  • Basic, aggregate site statistics (pages viewed, rough location by country, referrer), if analytics is enabled. We don't use advertising trackers.
  • Purchase details are handled by our checkout provider (for example Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy or Stripe). We see your name, email and what you bought; we never see your full card number.

Who processes it

Your email address is stored by our email service provider, which sends our emails on our behalf. The site is hosted on a static host (Netlify or GitHub Pages), which may keep standard server logs. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Why

To deliver the download you asked for, send the emails you signed up for, deliver products you buy, and understand which articles are useful.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: one click, at the bottom of every email.
  • Delete your data: reply to any email or write to us and we'll delete your address from our list and our provider.
  • Emails and the law: every email includes our postal address and an unsubscribe link, as required by the US CAN-SPAM Act. If you're in the EU or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.
