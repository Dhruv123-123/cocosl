Pay off debt

For credit card balances, loans, or a bill you're behind on.

  1. List every debt with its balance, interest rate and minimum payment. Day 1 of the free reset walks you through it.
  2. Pick an order. The avalanche or snowball method decides which debt gets your extra money, and the debt payoff calculator shows how long each takes.
  3. Pay a fixed amount, not the minimum, and stop adding to the card you're paying off. Then see whether a lower rate would help: balance transfer vs personal loan.

Build savings

For anyone with no cushion, or whose plans keep getting wrecked by surprise bills.

  1. Build a $1,000 starter emergency fund, then grow it toward a few months of expenses: the 8-week plan.
  2. Turn predictable costs into sinking funds (car repairs, gifts, annual bills), so they stop being emergencies: sinking funds.
  3. Keep it in an insured high-yield account, separate from checking: best high-yield savings accounts.

Freelance or side income

For freelancers, gig workers, and anyone whose pay changes from month to month.

  1. Separate the money. Open a savings account just for income and another just for tax. Any high-yield savings account with free extra accounts works.
  2. Set aside a share of every payment for tax, from the day it arrives. Many freelancers start with 25% to 30%.
  3. Pay yourself a steady amount each month from the income account, and build a buffer from the good months. Sinking funds handle the irregular bills.
