How it works

The 20-Minute Budget splits your take-home pay into four lines:

  1. Bills: rent, phone, insurance, minimum payments. Fixed, automated, ignored.
  2. Funds: monthly amounts for the irregular costs that usually wreck budgets (car repairs, gifts, renewals).
  3. Goals: emergency fund, extra debt payments, investing. Paid first, on payday.
  4. One weekly spending number: groceries, eating out, fun. It all comes out of that one number. When it's gone, the week's done. When there's some left, you choose where it goes.

That's the whole system. The guide walks you through setting it up step by step, with a fully worked example (real numbers, a fictional person), and the spreadsheet does all the maths.
