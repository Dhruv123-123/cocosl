The Money Made Simple editor

This page will introduce the person who edits Money Made Simple: who they are, why they write about money, and what they do and don't have expertise in. Until then, here's what the editor does on every page.

What the editor does

  • Chooses every topic and approves every article and comparison before it's published.
  • Checks every number, rate and fee against the source listed at the bottom of the page, and re-checks comparison pages on the date shown at the top.
  • Reads every page that was drafted with AI help line by line, including titles, descriptions and alt text, and rewrites what isn't right.
  • Decides which products we mention. Affiliate relationships never decide a recommendation (how we handle affiliate links).

Spotted a mistake? Email hello@example.com and it'll be fixed, with the correction noted on the page.
