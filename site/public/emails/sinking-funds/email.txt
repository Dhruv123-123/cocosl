Car registration isn't an emergency. It shows up every single year.

So do holiday gifts, the annual software renewal, the dentist, and the friend's wedding you RSVP'd to in March. They feel like surprises because they're not monthly. And they're the number one reason emergency funds quietly drain: the "emergency" money pays for Christmas, and then the real emergency goes on a credit card.

The fix is a sinking fund: a small monthly amount saved for a cost you know is coming.

| Fund | Cost | Due in | Per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Car registration | $180 | 12 months | $15 |
| Holiday gifts | $420 | 12 months | $35 |
| New phone | $720 | 24 months | $30 |
| Dentist + glasses | $300 | 12 months | $25 |

Total: $105 a month, one automatic transfer on payday. When the bill arrives, the money's already there, and nothing went wrong.
