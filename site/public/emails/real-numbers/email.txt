If not, here's the one-minute version, because it's the number everything else depends on.

Most people plan their money around their salary. But you can't spend your salary. You can spend what lands in your account after tax, retirement contributions, health insurance and everything else your payslip quietly removes.

To find your real monthly take-home pay:

- Paid every two weeks: one paycheck × 26 ÷ 12
- Paid weekly: one paycheck × 52 ÷ 12
- Income varies: use your lowest normal month from the last six

Notice it's ×26÷12, not ×2. Paid every two weeks, you get 26 paychecks a year, not 24. Two months a year you get a third paycheck, and if you plan around "two a month" those extra paychecks quietly disappear.

> If this weren't already running, would I sign up for it today at this price?
