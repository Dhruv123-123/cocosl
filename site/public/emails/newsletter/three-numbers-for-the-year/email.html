Five minutes. No resolutions required.

Hi there,

Short one this week, because it's Christmas Eve and you have better things to do.

This week's money move: write down three numbers, and nothing else.

  1. What you have. Add up your checking, savings and any investment or retirement account balances.
  2. What you owe. Add up every card balance, student loan, car loan and anything else you're paying off.
  3. What you saved this year. Rough is fine: how much went into savings, retirement or extra debt payments in 2026.

The first minus the second is your net worth. Don't judge it, especially if it's negative: that's normal early in a career, and student loans alone put most people there. The point is to have a starting line. Next December you write the same three numbers down and see the difference.

Put them somewhere you'll find again: a note on your phone, or the top of your budget spreadsheet. Next December, this newsletter will remind you to look.

No resolutions required. If you want one, make it small and automatic: a $25 monthly transfer to savings on payday beats a big plan you stop in February.

Happy holidays, and thanks for reading this year.
