How we make money

Everything on this site is free to read, and the email is free too. It costs money to run, so here is how it pays for itself, in plain words. There are three ways, and none of them changes what an article says.

1. Our own products

We sell simple money tools: printable planners, spreadsheets, Notion templates and guide-and-spreadsheet kits. They're in the Shop and sold through Gumroad and Etsy.

Right now: 58 products (43 printables, 6 kits, 5 Notion templates, 4 spreadsheets), priced $3 to $39, 1 of them free.

When an article mentions one of our products, it's because it fits that article. The free version of the idea is always in the article itself: you never have to buy anything to follow the advice. The 20-Minute Budget comes with a 14-day refund: email us and you get your money back, no forms.

2. Affiliate links

Some links to banks, apps and services are affiliate links. If you open an account or buy through one, the company may pay us a commission. You pay the same price either way.

How we handle them:

  • Affiliate links are marked rel="sponsored" for search engines, and articles that contain them say so at the top.
  • Our comparisons include options that pay us nothing, such as Vanguard, when they're the better fit.
  • We say who a product is not for, and we'd describe it the same way without the commission.
  • A company can't pay to be included, to rank higher or to approve what we write about it.

Affiliate partners right now: none. Until an affiliate program approves us, links go straight to the company and pay us nothing. When that changes, the list appears here.

3. Sponsors (not yet)

We don't have sponsors today. If we take them later, every sponsored email, video or post will say "sponsored" in the piece itself, not only in a footnote. A sponsor buys a labelled slot, never an article's opinion, and we won't take sponsors for things we tell readers to avoid, such as high-fee loans or "get rich quick" schemes.

What we never do

  • No paid reviews. Nobody can buy a good write-up or a place in a comparison.
  • No trading tips or stock picks, and no "guaranteed" returns.
  • No fake testimonials, and no AI-generated people presented as real customers.
  • No selling your email address. It's used to send you the emails you signed up for, through our email provider. See Privacy.
  • No display ads or ad trackers. We use cookieless analytics to count visits.

How AI is used

We use AI tools for visuals, voice and first drafts. A human chooses every topic, checks the facts against primary sources and approves everything before it's published. More on this, and the legal small print, on the Disclosures page.
