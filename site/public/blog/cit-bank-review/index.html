CIT Bank Platinum Savings review: great if you have $5,000 saved

CIT Platinum Savings has paid a top rate on balances of $5,000 or more. Who it suits, what to watch, and picks for smaller balances.

CIT Bank is an online bank that's a division of First Citizens Bank. Its Platinum Savings account is often near the top of high-yield savings comparisons, with one important condition: the high rate has applied to balances of $5,000 or more. Below that, the rate is much lower.

How we review: based on CIT Bank's published account terms, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review and haven't opened the account. Check the current rate table before opening.

Best for: people with an emergency fund of $5,000 or more who want a high rate and won't touch it. Not for: people just starting to save, who'll sit below the $5,000 tier for months.

What's good

  • A strong rate at the top tier. If you're above $5,000, it has been among the higher rates from an established bank.
  • No monthly fee.
  • Backed by a large bank. First Citizens is one of the bigger US banks, and deposits are FDIC-insured up to the limits.

What to check

  • The tier. Look at the current rate table on CIT's site, not just the headline APY. Work out what your actual balance would earn.
  • Transfers. Moving money to your checking account usually takes a few business days. Fine for an emergency fund; plan ahead.
  • Bonuses. CIT sometimes runs sign-up bonuses. Read the deposit amount, deadline and how long the money must stay.

If you have less than $5,000

Look for an account that pays its best rate on any balance, such as Ally Bank, or compare options in our high-yield savings guide. Once you pass $5,000, CIT is worth another look.

Our verdict

CIT Bank Platinum Savings is a good home for an established emergency fund. It's the wrong account for the first $1,000. Read the rate tiers and pick based on the balance you actually have.

Common questions

Is CIT Bank legit?

Yes. CIT Bank is a division of First Citizens Bank, an FDIC-insured bank. Check "First Citizens Bank" in FDIC BankFind.

Is CIT Bank FDIC insured?

Deposits are insured through First Citizens Bank up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category. Deposits you hold at First Citizens and CIT count toward the same limit, because they're the same bank.

What happens if my balance drops below $5,000?

You earn the lower tier rate on the account until the balance goes back up.
