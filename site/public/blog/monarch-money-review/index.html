Monarch Money review: the budgeting app for people who want the whole picture

Monarch Money tracks every account, budget and goal in one place, and works well for couples. What it costs, what it does well, where it falls short, and cheaper alternatives.

Monarch Money is a paid budgeting and net-worth app. Link your bank, card, loan and investment accounts and it puts everything on one dashboard, with budgets, goals and shared access for a partner.

How we review: this is based on Monarch's published features and pricing, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review. Check the current price before subscribing.

Best for: couples managing money together, and people with accounts at several banks who want one view. Not for: anyone who wants a free app, or who will set it up and never open it.

What it does well

  • Everything in one place. Checking, savings, cards, loans, retirement accounts and property values on one screen, so net worth updates by itself.
  • Couples. Both partners get their own login to the same household. You can see joint and individual accounts and agree on one budget.
  • Flexible budgeting. You can budget every category, or set fixed bills and one "flex" number for everything else, which is close to the weekly-number method.
  • Recurring charges and goals. It spots subscriptions and tracks savings goals.

Where it falls short

  • No free tier. About $100 a year is real money if you're just starting out.
  • Automatic doesn't mean you'll look. The risk with any synced app is that categorising happens without you, so nothing changes. Book a weekly 10-minute check-in.
  • Bank connections drop. You'll occasionally need to re-link an account.

Monarch vs YNAB vs Rocket Money

  • Monarch: the best all-in-one dashboard, strong for couples.
  • YNAB: a stricter method where every dollar gets a job. More hands-on, more behaviour change. Our YNAB review and Monarch vs YNAB.
  • Rocket Money: strongest at finding and cancelling subscriptions, with a free tier. Our Rocket Money review.

Our verdict

If you'll use it weekly, and especially if you share money with a partner, Monarch is one of the most complete budgeting apps available. Use the trial to link every account and run one weekly check-in. If you haven't opened it by week two, a simple spreadsheet will serve you better and costs nothing.

Common questions

Is Monarch Money worth it?

It is if you'll use it every week and value seeing every account in one place. If you only need to track spending, cheaper or free tools do that.

Does Monarch Money have a free version?

No. It's a paid subscription, often with a free trial for new users.

Is Monarch a good Mint replacement?

Many former Mint users moved to Monarch because it covers the same "see everything" job, with more budgeting features. The difference is that it isn't free.
