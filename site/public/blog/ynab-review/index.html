YNAB review: is You Need a Budget worth $109 a year?

YNAB (short for You Need a Budget) is a budgeting app with a strong opinion: you plan every dollar you have now before you spend it. People either love it or bounce off it in a week. Here's how to tell which you'll be.

How the YNAB method works

  1. Give every dollar a job. When money arrives, assign all of it to categories: rent, groceries, savings, fun.
  2. Plan for big, irregular costs. Car insurance, gifts and annual fees get a small monthly amount, the same idea as sinking funds.
  3. Roll with the punches. Overspent on food? Move money from another category. No guilt, just adjusting.
  4. Age your money. Over time, you aim to spend money you earned last month, not this week.

The method is free on YNAB's website. The app makes it easier to follow.

What it does well

  • It changes behaviour. Because you assign money before spending it, you see trade-offs in real time.
  • It handles irregular income. You budget only the money you have, which suits freelancers and shift workers.
  • Teaching material. Free workshops and guides on the method.

Where it falls short

  • It's work. Expect a few hours to set up and a learning curve in the first month.
  • It's not cheap. $109 a year is about $9 a month. Use the trial and decide by week four.
  • It's not an investment tracker. You can track account balances, but it's built for spending, not net worth.

Our verdict

If money feels out of control and you're willing to do the work for a month, YNAB is one of the most effective budgeting methods around, and the 34-day trial is long enough to know. If you're a student, check the free-year offer first. If you want to see everything without much effort, choose a different tool.

Common questions

Is YNAB worth it?

It is for people who use it. The trial is the honest test: if you're still opening it in week four, it's probably working.

Can I use YNAB for free?

For the trial, and for a year if you're an eligible college student. The method itself is free to learn and use in a spreadsheet.

Does YNAB connect to my bank?

Yes, it can import transactions from most US banks, or you can enter them by hand, which many users prefer because it keeps them paying attention.
