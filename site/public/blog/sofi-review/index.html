SoFi Checking and Savings review: good rate, with one condition to read

SoFi is a chartered bank with no account fees and a competitive rate, but the top rate has conditions. What to check first.

SoFi Checking and Savings is a combined checking and savings account from SoFi Bank, N.A. It's popular because it pairs no account fees with a savings rate well above most big banks. The thing to understand before you open it is what you need to do to get the top rate.

How we review: based on SoFi's published terms and fee schedule, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review and haven't tested the account. Rates and conditions change; read SoFi's current terms.

Extra insuranceA sweep program spreads money across partner banks for coverage above $250,000 (check the current limit)
BranchesNone (online and app)

Best for: people who can send a paycheck by direct deposit and want checking and savings in one app. Not for: people who can't meet the deposit condition (the base rate is much lower), or who want a branch.

What's good

  • It's a bank. SoFi has its own bank charter, so your deposits are held by an FDIC-insured bank directly. That avoids the "pass-through" risk some fintech apps have. Why that matters.
  • No account fees and no minimums.
  • Vaults. You can split savings into named pots, handy for sinking funds.
  • Early paycheck. Direct deposits can arrive up to two days early, depending on when your employer sends them.

What to check

  • The rate conditions. Read the current requirement for the top APY. If you can't meet it, compare the base rate with other banks.
  • Promotions. SoFi often runs sign-up bonuses tied to direct deposit amounts. Read the full terms and the deadline before counting on one.
  • Cash deposits. Depositing cash at retail locations can carry a third-party fee.
  • It's one more app. If you already like your checking account, you can just open a savings-only account elsewhere.

Alternatives

  • No conditions on the rate: Ally Bank.
  • Larger balance ($5,000+): CIT Bank Platinum Savings.
  • Full comparison: how to pick a high-yield savings account.

Our verdict

If you can point a paycheck at it, SoFi Checking and Savings is a strong, fee-free all-in-one account from a chartered bank. If you can't meet the deposit condition, a bank with a single flat rate is simpler.

Common questions

Is SoFi a real bank?

Yes. SoFi Bank, N.A. is a nationally chartered bank and a member of the FDIC. You can confirm it in FDIC BankFind.

Does SoFi have fees?

SoFi Checking and Savings has no monthly account fees. Some services, like cash deposits at retailers, can involve third-party fees.

Do I need direct deposit for SoFi?

Not to open the account. The top savings rate has depended on direct deposit or qualifying deposits, so check the current terms.
