Ally Bank: what to know about the online bank with savings buckets

Ally is an online bank with no minimums, no monthly fees and savings buckets for each goal. What's good and what to watch.

Ally Bank is one of the longest-running online banks in the US. It's not usually the single highest rate on the market, but it's simple: no minimum to open, no monthly fees, and tools that make saving for several goals easy.

How we wrote this: based on Ally's published terms, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this overview and haven't tested the account. Check the current rates on Ally's site.

Best for: people who want one reliable online bank for checking and savings, and like organising savings by goal. Not for: rate chasers who'll switch every few months, or anyone who needs to deposit cash often.

What's good

  • Buckets. Split one savings account into labelled goals (emergency fund, car, holiday) without opening extra accounts. This is the easiest way we know to run sinking funds.
  • Boosters. Optional round-ups and "surprise savings" that move small amounts automatically.
  • No minimums or monthly fees, and the same rate on $10 as on $10,000.
  • Everything under one roof if you also want checking and CDs.

What to watch

  • Cash deposits. As an online bank, Ally doesn't take cash directly; you'd deposit it at another bank and transfer it.
  • Not always the top rate. Competitive, but some banks pay more. For an emergency fund, a small gap matters less than simplicity.
  • Transfer times. External transfers usually take 1–3 business days.

Alternatives

  • Higher rate on a $5,000+ balance: CIT Bank.
  • Checking and savings with direct deposit: SoFi.
  • Full checklist: how to pick a high-yield savings account.

Our verdict

If you want one online bank that's easy to live with, Ally is a safe default, and the buckets feature is genuinely useful. If you only care about the top rate, compare first.

Common questions

Is Ally Bank safe?

Ally Bank is an FDIC member, so deposits are insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.

Does Ally have a minimum balance?

No minimum to open savings and no monthly maintenance fees.

How do buckets work?

They're labels inside one savings account. Your money and rate are the same; you just see how much belongs to each goal.
