Aura review: is identity theft protection worth paying for?

What Aura's credit monitoring, insurance and security tools add, what you can do yourself for free, and who should pay.

Aura is an all-in-one identity theft protection service: credit monitoring across all three bureaus, alerts if your details show up in data breaches, identity theft insurance, and security tools like a VPN and antivirus. The honest question isn't "is Aura good" but "do you need to pay for this at all?" Some of the most powerful protections are free.

How we review: based on Aura's published plans and features, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review and haven't tested the service. Check the current plans before buying.

For how Aura stacks up against LifeLock, Identity Guard and the free steps, see our identity theft protection comparison.

What you can do for free first

Before paying anyone, do these. They take under an hour:

  1. Freeze your credit at all three bureaus. It's free and it's the single strongest protection against new accounts being opened in your name. Step-by-step guide.
  2. Check your credit reports. Free weekly at AnnualCreditReport.com, the official site.
  3. Turn on two-factor login for your email, bank and phone carrier accounts, and use a password manager.
  4. If something happens: IdentityTheft.gov (from the FTC) builds a recovery plan for free.

Who it's worth it for

  • Families, especially with children (whose credit can be stolen for years unnoticed) or older parents who are common scam targets.
  • People who've already been victims and want monitoring plus a team to call.
  • People who'll never do the free steps. Paying for automation can beat doing nothing.

Who can skip it

People who'll freeze their credit, check their reports and use a password manager. That covers most of the risk at no cost.

What it costs

Aura sells individual, couple and family plans, usually cheapest when billed annually. Prices change and introductory discounts are common, so check the current price and the renewal price. Put the renewal date in your calendar.

Our verdict

Do the free steps first. If you want ongoing monitoring, insurance and a team to call, especially for a family, Aura bundles it in one app. If you're an individual who has frozen your credit, you may not need it.

Common questions

Does identity theft protection prevent identity theft?

No service can stop every case. Monitoring alerts you faster; a credit freeze actually blocks most new-account fraud.

What does identity theft insurance cover?

Typically eligible costs of recovering your identity, such as lost wages, legal fees and some stolen funds, up to the policy limit and subject to its terms. Read the policy summary.

Is a credit lock the same as a credit freeze?

They do a similar job. A freeze is free by law at all three bureaus; a lock is a company's product and terms vary.
