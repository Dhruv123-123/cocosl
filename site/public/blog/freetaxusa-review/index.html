FreeTaxUSA review: free federal filing that's actually free

FreeTaxUSA files federal returns free, including freelance income, and charges a small state fee. What's included and what isn't.

FreeTaxUSA is an online tax-prep service whose federal return is free for most situations, including freelance income, rental income and itemised deductions, which other "free" products often charge for. You pay a modest fee for a state return and for optional extras.

How we review: based on FreeTaxUSA's published pricing and features for the most recent filing season, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review. Prices for next season may change; check the pricing page. Not tax advice.

The short version

ItemItem
Federal return$0, including many forms other free products upsell
State returnA fee per state (about $15–$16 last season)
Deluxe add-onOptional, a few dollars: priority support, amended returns and more
Freelance and gig incomeIncluded (Schedule C)
Import last year's returnYes, including from some other providers
Live expert helpLimited compared with pricier software

Best for: people with W-2s, student loans, freelance income or itemised deductions who are comfortable answering questions themselves. Not for: people who want a human to review their return, or very complex situations.

What's good

  • Federal really is free, even for self-employed income. That's unusual.
  • Low state fee compared with big-name products.
  • Clear interview format that walks through each section.

What to watch

  • Upsells are light, but read each screen. Add-ons like audit defence are optional.
  • Support. Basic support is by email; the Deluxe add-on adds priority and chat.
  • Complex returns. Multiple states, a business with employees or large investment transactions may need more help.

Free options to check first

  • VITA: free in-person tax help from IRS-certified volunteers, generally for people under an income limit (around $67,000 in recent seasons).
  • IRS Free File: guided software from IRS partners, free if your income is under the yearly limit. Start from IRS.gov so you land on the free version.

The IRS Direct File pilot wasn't offered for the 2026 filing season; check IRS.gov for what's available when you file.

Our verdict

For most people who can do their own return, FreeTaxUSA is one of the best-value options, especially if you have freelance income. Check VITA and IRS Free File first if your income qualifies; they can make the state return free too. Comparison: tax software for simple returns.

Common questions

Is FreeTaxUSA legit?

Yes. It's an IRS-authorised e-file provider that's been around for many years.

Is FreeTaxUSA really free?

The federal return is free. State returns and optional add-ons cost extra.

Can I use FreeTaxUSA for 1099 income?

Yes. Self-employment and gig income are included in the free federal return.
