Empower Personal Dashboard review: free net-worth tracking, with a sales pitch attached

Empower's free dashboard tracks every account and flags investment fees. What it does well and how the paid-advice upsell works.

Empower Personal Dashboard (the free tool that used to be called Personal Capital) links your bank, card, retirement and brokerage accounts and shows your whole net worth in one place. It's free because Empower also sells paid investment management, and it will offer it to you.

How we review: based on Empower's published features and disclosures, checked in November 2026. We haven't been paid for this review. This is general education, not investment advice.

The short version

BudgetingBasic spending tracking, not a full budget app

Best for: people with a 401(k), an IRA and a few other accounts who want to see them together for free. Not for: people who want a detailed monthly budget, or who don't want any sales contact.

What it does well

  • Net worth in one place. Watching one number move over time is motivating, and it updates automatically.
  • The fee analyser. It estimates how much your funds' fees could cost over decades. This alone can be worth signing up for: a high-fee fund in an old 401(k) is a common, fixable problem. Why expense ratios matter.
  • Retirement planner. Shows whether your current saving is on track, using assumptions you can adjust. Treat the output as an estimate, not a forecast.

What to know before signing up

  • The advice upsell. Empower's paid management charges an annual fee on assets it manages. You don't have to use it. If an adviser contacts you, it's fine to say no, and you can ask what the total yearly fee would be in dollars.
  • Budgeting is light. For a real budget, pair it with a spreadsheet or another app.
  • Data sharing. You're linking a lot of accounts. Use a strong password and two-factor login.

If you do talk to an adviser

Ask three questions: what's the total annual fee in dollars on my balance, are you a fiduciary for this advice, and what would I do differently with you than in a low-cost index fund? You can look up any adviser on the SEC's Investment Adviser Public Disclosure site.

Our verdict

The free Empower Personal Dashboard is one of the best free tools for tracking investments and net worth, especially for the fee check. Use it for that, and treat the advisory pitch as optional.

Investing involves risk, including loss of principal. Past performance doesn't predict future results.

Common questions

Is Empower Personal Dashboard really free?

Yes. The dashboard is free; Empower makes money from its paid advisory services and other products.

Is Empower the same as Personal Capital?

Yes. Personal Capital was acquired by Empower and renamed.

Will Empower call me?

It may, especially if you link larger investment balances. You can decline and ask to be contacted less.
